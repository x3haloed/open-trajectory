A fresh process may propose up to three bounded scoring branches over the raw
integer coordinates of an event. The world validates each exact branch against
independently completed contact. A later fresh process receives the bounded
branch ledger and authors the next ledger, including which exact branch is
active. It may retain competing branches, introduce a replacement, or omit a
branch. The controller validates identities and consequence receipts but does
not search for, rank, or supply a useful branch.

The active branch selects the event with the larger score. Every scoring branch
uses only integer literals, raw-coordinate subscripting, and ordinary bounded
arithmetic. No task relation, derived feature, solved example, or desirable
branch is supplied.
