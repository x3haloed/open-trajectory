Author three distinct bounded selector score programs and one exact structured
decision list. A score program is a reverse-Polish stack of at most 24 tokens.
Every token has exactly `op`, integer `value`, and integer `index` fields.

Push operations are `sequence`, `label`, `feature`, and `constant`. `feature`
uses index 0–3 with value 0. `constant` uses value -32 through 32 with index 0.
Other push operations use zero value and index. Unary operations are `abs` and
`negate`; binary operations are `add`, `subtract`, `multiply`, and `mod`. All
unused operands are zero. Tokens must never underflow and must leave exactly one
integer score. `mod` by zero deterministically yields zero.

Each program also authors a Boolean `descending`. The controller scores every
event, sorts by score in that direction with stable sequence/event-id ties, and
retains exactly `limit`. These are generic syntax semantics, not a strategy
menu; the actor authors every score computation and direction.

The three decision clauses use the OT-0025 exact carrier: actor-authored order,
choice, integer minimum advantage from -16 through 16, and Boolean changed-
selection/prediction requirements. The controller interprets programs and
clauses exactly, owns outcomes and commits, and supplies no ranking or choice.
