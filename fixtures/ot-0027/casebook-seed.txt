Author one bounded exemplar casebook from the completed prior encounter. Each
exemplar anchors to one distinct prior event identity and authors a four-Boolean
feature mask, an integer Hamming radius from 0 through 4, and an integer
priority from -16 through 16.

For a future event, the controller compares only feature positions whose mask
is true. If masked Hamming distance from an anchor is at most its radius, that
exemplar's priority contributes to the event score. Contributions sum. The
controller deterministically retains the six highest-scoring future events,
with sequence and event identity as stable ties. One through eight exemplars
are allowed.

These are generic example-matching semantics, not a strategy menu. You author
every anchor, mask, radius, and priority before the future evaluation exists.
The controller owns outcomes, scoring, exact interpretation, and snapshot
identity. Current casebook: empty; current selection: empty.
