Maintain a bounded pure expression that chooses which prior contacts become
available to a deterministic controller-owned prediction instrument. The
initial expression is `[]` and carries no selection operation.

At execution the expression receives only `events`, `queries`, and `limit`.
Each event is a mapping with `event_id`, `sequence`, `features`, and `label`;
queries are feature lists. The expression must deterministically return exactly
`limit` unique event_id strings. Allowed calls are abs, all, any, enumerate,
len, max, min, sorted, sum, and zip. Imports, attributes, private names,
mutation, and statements are forbidden. Comprehensions, lambdas, indexing,
arithmetic, comparisons, and conditionals are available.

Use only controller-supplied consequence receipts when proposing revisions.
State a falsifiable expected effect for every change. Do not claim success;
later sealed outcomes decide that.
