# Research program

The developmental coordinate is encounter index, not wall-clock time:

```text
prospective prediction or action
→ independently retained world response
→ measured discrepancy
→ permitted persistent update
→ complete context reset
→ later held-out prediction or action
```

## Learned program invariants

Routine research must leave the host ordinarily usable. This property became
explicit after OT-0013's local inference made interactive use impractical and
the operator stopped the run. Large checkpoint acquisition and
resource-saturating local inference are therefore exceptional paths requiring
explicit authorization rather than default reproduction steps.

Contact-causal inheritance and recursive inheritance selection must remain
separately named. Lower held-out error caused by inherited state is a necessary
substrate result; it is not evidence that experience changed the function
deciding what deserves inheritance. This distinction was restored after direct
user review of OT-0014's target classification.

## Evaluation-regime epochs

- **E1 — immutable revision:** a promoted contact-causal comparison required an
  exact immutable model revision. OT-0003 remains conditional under E1;
  OT-0012 and OT-0013 remain invalid. Their dispositions are not reinterpreted.
- **E2 — immutable revision or receipted hosted epoch:** beginning after
  OT-0013 was terminated and classified, a new experiment may instead freeze a
  hosted deployment epoch. It must receipt the requested and effective model, model
  catalog identity, receipt implementation, and per-response identity; require
  one unchanged epoch across original and reproduction; counterbalance
  conditions against time; and finish inside a frozen time window. The fixed
  scientific thresholds, independent world receipts, fresh-actor boundary,
  privacy gate, and clean-reproduction requirement remain anchors and may not
  regress. Hosted promotion is explicitly private and time-bounded.
- **E3 — restored OT-1 coupling:** beginning after direct user review of
  OT-0014, E2's model, receipt, causal-boundary, privacy, and reproduction
  anchors remain unchanged. Contact-causal inheritance is classified as OT-0.
  OT-1 additionally requires an experience-modified inheritance-selection
  function, a useful selection operation not supplied by researchers, causal
  advantage over unchanged and fixed selectors, and later correction when the
  learned operation becomes harmful. OT-0014's raw evidence and behavioral
  disposition remain valid; only its former target-level OT-1 conclusion is
  stale and is replaced by the narrower OT-0 classification. No earlier
  candidate is rescored under E3.

## Program A — Emergent Corrigible Inheritance Selection

**Current target status:** OT-0014 establishes OT-0 contact-causal inheritance
under E2 within the tested hidden-rule envelope. OT-1 is open. The supporting
evidence is private and time-bounded; it does not claim immutable weights,
public reconstructibility, cross-domain generality, a mutable selector, an
emergent selection operation, selector correction, or validity under a later
hosted epoch.

Build the evaluation harness before choosing a preferred learning substrate.
The program invents its own candidate representations, selection rules, update
mechanisms, and correction paths. Minimal no-persistence, verbatim-event, and
naive-retrieval implementations exist only as falsification controls; no
external memory architecture is a candidate or dependency.

OT-0 candidates establish the strict contact-causal path over hidden-rule,
executable-software, and noisy probabilistic domains. Require structural
holdouts, regime shifts, cold-instance inheritance, and causal ablations.

OT-1 candidates operate one level higher. Give the system a strict inheritance
budget, raw candidate encounters, independent outcomes, access to its prior
selection decisions, and authority to propose changes to its selection
procedure. Do not name the useful strategies or provide a researcher-written
menu of them. Permit selector expression as prose, structured rules, code,
weights, examples, retrieval transformations, schemas, or another inspectable
carrier.

The first decisive selector environment should make different information
useful across sealed regimes: stable abstraction, sparse predictive exceptions,
drift that makes recent contradiction useful, and later noise that makes an
unqualified recency policy harmful. These are world-design requirements, not
actor instructions. The candidate must discover a useful operation, causally
benefit from it, encounter evidence that the operation has become wrong, and
correct it. Compare against unchanged-selector branches, fixed researcher
selectors, equal-budget no-persistence/verbatim/naive retrieval, and decisive
selector-change and correction ablations.

Each encounter uses a fresh agent thread and fresh workspace. The candidate
substrate is the only allowed causal channel across encounters. The experiment
controller owns ground truth, sealed outcomes, scoring, and snapshot identity.

Initial sequence:

- **OT-0001:** prove evidence publication and preventive privacy/leak controls.
- **OT-0002:** prove the fresh-agent encounter harness and substrate boundary.
- **OT-0003:** invent and test a predictive-inheritance substrate.
- **OT-0014:** freeze the receipted OT-0 hidden-rule result.
- **OT-0004:** test whether consequences of selection decisions can cause the
  system to invent, commit, and later correct an inheritance-selection
  operation.
- **OT-0005:** test inheritance in a clean model instance.

The initial execution backend is a product-owned controller around Codex. It
may drive Codex through the SDK/app-server protocol or place Codex behind MCP;
candidate substrates and experimental semantics remain backend-independent.
See `docs/AGENT_HARNESS.md`.

`docs/RESEARCH_LANDSCAPE.md` records non-normative distinctions, hypothesis
families, environmental pressures, and adjacent individual and collective
research directions. It exists to widen candidate generation; it does not add
requirements to OT-0 or OT-1 or alter a frozen experiment gate.

## Program B — Self-Authored Durable Goals

Program B remains specified as written but is paused until the restored OT-1
gate is promoted. OT-0006 was executed before this target correction and its
rejected result is preserved; it does not justify bypassing OT-1.

Separate goal, plan, subgoal, and current action. A goal contract records its
value thesis, completion predicate, constraints, progress evidence, revision
conditions, and surrender conditions. Continuation is voluntary; completion is
an externally judged evidence claim.

Initial sequence:

- **OT-0006:** isolate bounded-slice completion failure with a human goal.
- **OT-0007:** test voluntary goal formation from procedural orientation.

## Program C — TAAA

Represent authority as a versioned capability vector. A capability petition
records the obstruction, smallest requested delta, predicted improvement,
independent test, blast-radius bound, expiration, and rollback trigger.

Initial sequence:

- **OT-0008:** test a minimal legitimate capability petition.
- **OT-0009:** compare impossible tasks with and without lawful recourse.
- **OT-0010:** reverse a promoted boundary after a regime shift.

The complete target path is:

```text
contact
→ lower held-out prediction error
→ consequences of inheritance choices
→ changed inheritance-selection function
→ useful selection operation not supplied by researchers
→ later selector-level contradiction
→ revision or abandonment of the learned operation
→ preserved capacity for further correction
→ self-authored falsifiable goal
→ sustained pursuit
→ diagnosed authority bottleneck
→ minimal petition
→ independent canary
→ evidence-gated expansion
→ contradictory later contact
→ revision or revocation
→ preserved correction capacity
```
