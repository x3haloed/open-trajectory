# Trajectory-native developmental projection epoch

- **Status:** prospectively opened; first calibration not yet executed
- **Evaluation epoch:** E13
- **Learner authorization:** none
- **Predecessor:** endogenous substrate epoch informative exhaustion
- **Normative target:** unchanged

## Outcome orientation

The governing outcome is not another successful memory representation. Across
complete resets, contact should increasingly affect both what remains causally
available and how future instances decide what deserves that availability.
The process must eventually participate in inventing or restructuring that
machinery, while independent reality retains the ability to expose and reverse
mistaken machinery and commitments.

The previous epoch remains authoritative. It showed that fresh actors could
sometimes construct useful expressions, predicates, transducers, and
partitions, but did not reliably replace harmful inherited structure through a
one-turn full-state proposal interface. Safe rejection protected the active
state, yet the rejected proposal and its exact trial result did not survive as
part of the next actor's developmental position.

## Evaluation transition

The previous next-step rule required a candidate-owned
"discrepancy-to-revision object" before another learner could be considered.
That rule correctly identified a severed causal edge, but it also risked
installing the semantic ontology the candidate should eventually invent. A
researcher-defined discrepancy object could make the controller decide which
parts of a failed attempt mattered and thereby award a representation supplied
by the experiment rather than one produced through contact.

E13 changes only the prospective search and evaluation surface:

```text
append-only addressable causal history
                 +
authoritative active-state pointer
                 +
bounded exact projection of actor-named records
```

The retained history has physical and historical availability. The active
pointer designates only protocol-level active identity; OT-0070 tests its
effect on one deterministic consumer and does not establish epistemic standing.
A proposal can therefore survive, receive an independently owned trial, and
remain available for later inspection without becoming protocol-active.

E13 initially supplies only procedural authority boundaries:

- an unforgeable channel capability assigns source authority while appending a
  canonical record;
- parent edges bind a trial to the exact proposal it executed;
- a later actor may name a branch for adoption or set it down;
- the controller validates identity, provenance, schema, and resource bounds;
- adoption and rollback append pointer events rather than deleting history;
- projection is lossless and address-selected, not model-authored summary.

It does **not** supply discrepancy labels, error diagnoses, repair operations,
branch rankings, semantic merge rules, a desirable projection policy, or a
menu of reasons that history should matter. Those remain candidate questions.

## Transition anchors

The following anchors are independent of E13 and may not regress:

- complete actor-thread and workspace resets for every learning encounter;
- independently owned contact outcomes and sealed endpoint scores;
- strict equal active-inheritance and resource budgets;
- controller-owned snapshot identity, evidence custody, and final scoring;
- no controller repair synthesis, best-candidate selection, or hidden winner
  promotion;
- decisive substrate and projection ablations;
- exact parent preservation, recoverable sibling branches, and rollback;
- prospective freezing, content-addressed evidence, privacy, and audit gates;
- preservation of all prior negative results and authorization dispositions.

No prior score or disposition is rescored under E13. OT-0048 through OT-0069
remain evidence about their frozen worlds and interfaces. E13 begins only
because that epoch is complete and the user explicitly selected a more
trajectory-native developmental direction.

## First checkpoint: OT-0070

OT-0070 is a public, deterministic, candidate-free calibration of the thin
trajectory and authority primitive. It asks whether the repository can make
all of these facts simultaneously true:

1. actor-channel, world-channel, and controller-channel fixture records are
   immutable, content-addressed, and causally bound;
2. an imperfect proposal and its exact trial remain recoverable while the
   active pointer stays unchanged;
3. a bounded exact projection can carry that inactive branch across a
   simulated reset;
4. synthetic actor-channel fixture decisions exercise adoption and set-down,
   without being treated as actor authorship or useful judgment;
5. set-down preserves both the active parent and the inactive branch;
6. the active pointer is derived only by replaying a single compare-and-swap
   chain of controller events, rollback restores the exact prior pointer by
   appending history, and a deterministic consumer follows only that pointer;
   and
7. deleted, missing, sibling, or cross-bound records fail closed while
   isomorphic renaming and irrelevant append order do not change the result.

OT-0070 cannot establish useful revision, endogenous projection, selector
learning, representation escape, or developmental transfer. A pass authorizes
no learner. It establishes only that inactive causal history, separately
derived active state, and pointer-controlled behavior can coexist as exact
substrate mechanics rather than a narrative description.

## Condition for later learner work

A later experiment must add an independently calibrated environment in which a
fresh actor, not the controller, uses completed trajectory contact to decide
what inactive branch deserves projection, authors a useful successor or sets
the branch down, and later revises that projection or update practice when it
becomes harmful. The advantage must disappear when the relevant projection is
withheld or misbound, survive equal-budget raw and fixed-projection controls,
and transfer beyond any representation used to discover it before a widened
developmental claim is considered.

Headlong motivated the trajectory/context distinction and demonstrates that
addressable append-only histories are operationally possible. It is a
hypothesis source only. Open Trajectory does not import its implementation,
continuous-thought model, compaction policy, merge semantics, or qualitative
improvement claims.
