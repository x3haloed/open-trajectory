# Public work frontier

## Outcome

The repository target remains open. The prospective E14 target is longitudinal
continual adaptation: many fresh inference passes from a base held under the
immutable-revision or receipted hosted-epoch identity rule, connected only
through an inherited external substrate whose updates from independent contact
cause reliably lower future prediction error across a changing and recurring
stream. No learner-controlled model-weight update is part of the path.

The subject may inherit the initial researcher-designed learning machinery.
Consequence-driven refinement of that machinery is the next stronger claim;
repeated cross-environment expansion belongs to Open Developmental Trajectory.

## Decision-relevant evidence

- `OT-0014` establishes bounded private, time-limited OT-0 contact-causal
  inheritance in one hidden-rule envelope. It is a narrow inherited-state
  foothold, not a longitudinal result.
- `OT-0037` establishes bounded publicly reconstructible OT-1 selector
  refinement in one synthetic domain. It is selection-function evidence
  relevant to machinery refinement, but it has not passed the new parent/child
  future-learning gate, a long horizon, or structural expansion.
- `OT-0045` establishes bounded private, time-limited OT-2 durable goal pursuit
  in one synthetic domain. It does not add a continual-learning result.
- `OT-0048` through `OT-0069` preserve calibrated opportunities,
  invalidations, and negative learner results across expressions, predicates,
  transducers, and partitions. They show that one-shot full-state construction
  and correction was unreliable even when useful structure was sometimes
  inferable.
- `OT-0070` establishes thin append-only history, active-pointer, projection,
  and rollback mechanics without a learner.
- `OT-0071` remains operationally invalidated and exploratory-only.
- `OT-0073` retains exact candidate-free reconstruction evidence with zero actor
  calls. Later probing found a missing fail-closed E13 case/regime binding;
  `OT-0074` suspends reliance on its recorded promotion and authorization before
  task or actor output.

No prior score or disposition is changed by E14. Exact records and receipts
remain in `experiments/` and `evidence/manifests/`.

## Prospective evaluation boundary

OT-0075 was rejected on a declared public design stream before private
derivation: an unrelated immutable control made the causal-loss rule count
fixed-prior accuracy as surviving learning. It generated no private seed, task,
attempt, candidate, actor output, or learner authority. OT-0076 implemented the
matched-counterfactual and dual-ancestry repair, but its public oracle found that
the frozen schema required `consequence-withholding` while the frozen byte count
and digest identified the otherwise identical vector using `withholding`. It
was rejected before private derivation. Before actor output, OT-0077 must freeze
one coherent public identity and prospectively calibrate:

- a long prequential stream with multiple changes and at least one recurring
  regime;
- frozen horizon, order, replications, cumulative and rolling prequential loss,
  post-change recovery, retention, relearning, and promotion thresholds;
- no-persistence, immutable-seed, outcome-free clock, strongest-fixed, and one
  exact matched-frozen control per reference, plus reported history and
  retrieval comparators;
- consequence withholding, update without projection, and projection without
  update as hard matched-trace severings; informative stale binding as a
  separate temporal degradation test; and state/lineage misbinding; and
- exact replay of every substrate transition from the inherited seed and
  ordered encounter receipts.

Design worlds may tune the evaluator. After evaluator implementation is frozen,
one private seed and the implementation identity mechanically derive a
disjoint anchor bundle used once for promotion. Two materially different
online-admissible controller references must pass and seeded causal defects,
including high-scoring future/hidden oracles, must fail. The derivation
function, private-seed length, implementation binding, one-attempt rule, and
collision handling are frozen before implementation; reseeding and anchor
shopping are forbidden. The checkpoint calibrates an evaluator-visible
surrogate, not a base-model learning claim.

The first actor-bearing E14 candidate must preserve its immutable revision or
unchanged receipted hosted epoch, keep learning machinery fixed, reset actor and
workspace state at every encounter, update the declared external substrate
after independently resolved contact, and beat all eligible controls on the
frozen comparative trajectory. A regime-change spike is allowed; reliable
recovery and lower cumulative error are what matter.

Freeze the candidate seed substrate and updater implementation before deriving
its mechanically bound, one-attempt hidden stream. Task seed, regime schedule,
references, heldout probes, evaluator instructions, and control winners remain
outside candidate reachability.

## Subsequent machinery-refinement boundary

After a longitudinal foothold, a versioned child may modify one inherited
learning operation. Fork it and its unchanged parent from the same content state
under matched prospective encounters, count proposal and trial costs, and
require later learning improvement plus a decisive machinery-delta ablation.
Then make the adopted change harmful and require revision, set-down, or
rollback. A self-refinement claim requires an actor-specified useful delta. One
change is a bounded foothold; repeated improvements are required for continual
meta-learning.

Representation escape remains a later structural-expansion test, not a
prerequisite for E14, a first machinery refinement, or widened OT-2 work. See
[`LONGITUDINAL_CONTINUAL_LEARNING_EPOCH.md`](LONGITUDINAL_CONTINUAL_LEARNING_EPOCH.md)
for the prospective transition and [`PROGRAM.md`](../PROGRAM.md) for the complete
preserved ledger.
