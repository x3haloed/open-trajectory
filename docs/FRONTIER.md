# Public work frontier

## Outcome

Develop a continuing subject whose exact inherited state carries consequence,
unfinished pursuit, and executable reopening across fresh actors and turn
endings. Useful continuation should increasingly arise from what matters to the
subject under objective correction, rather than from an experiment-specific
researcher choosing each pursuit, phase, repair, or redesign.

The target is not endless activity. The subject must preserve earned floors,
remain able to revise or surrender what no longer survives contact, select
better future moves because of retained consequence, and open coherent new
possibilities without acquiring authority over world outcomes or admission.

## Goal invariants

- A model turn, actor, run, or observer budget may end without closing an open
  subject. The exact last valid state must remain mechanically reopenable.
- Continuity is developmental position, not repeated wording: what has been
  earned, contradicted, surrendered, left unresolved, and made live next must
  change later behavior.
- Independent world consequence, not actor self-report or researcher
  preference, decides what becomes authoritative subject state.
- Fresh actors receive cross-encounter continuity only through the exact named
  subject projection and begin in fresh workspaces.
- Broad actor tools remain available. Isolation, complete trace retention,
  effect audit, sealed authority, and quarantine protect the experiment rather
  than tool deprivation.
- A valid operational successor is sealed before controls. Controls govern the
  strength of the claim, not whether an already valid subject is allowed to
  continue.
- Surrender is neither success nor termination. When grounded in contact it may
  become non-authoritative developmental evidence that improves the next
  actor's position; empty repeated surrender must not fabricate motion.
- Local verification exhausts a pursuit surface. Once a bounded surface is
  independently earned, more examples alone do not keep it developmentally
  live; continuation must widen, revise, or truthfully complete locally while
  remaining globally open.
- More state, tools, actors, cycles, or novelty are not progress by themselves.
  Preferred moves preserve floors, increase consequence-sensitive future-move
  selection, widen coherent contact, and remove hidden researcher causation.
- Subject priorities must eventually shape which future possibilities are
  sought. Arbitrary unseen-world generation can preserve motion but cannot by
  itself establish self-directed trajectory.
- Exact history may grow while it remains reconstructible and does not prevent
  reliable continuation. Compaction is an efficiency mechanism, not permission
  to erase contradiction or surrendered stakes.

## Evaluation regime

Claim regime G3 remains the outer interpretation boundary: report operational
validity, causal validity, generative reproducibility, and actor provenance
separately. Freeze the decision and authority envelope before live output, but
do not require a researcher-authored itinerary when self-direction is the
claim.

The active G10 apparatus keeps actor tools broad while accepting only
mechanically contained effects and cleanly classified denials. Actors may
propose code, pursuits, contact, and substrate changes, but may not alter sealed
world outcomes, scores, evidence, or admission. Exact ancestry, bind-before-
consequence order, independent outcomes, unchanged or ablated comparisons,
no-regression floors, privacy, and preserved negative evidence remain hard
anchors.

## Prediction errors

- Standing renewal removed the researcher from choosing a concrete next world,
  but it did so with a subject-blind provider. This sustains novelty without yet
  showing that novelty is pursued because it matters to the subject.
- The fixed world-package language and independent scorer make causal contact
  legible, but repeated success inside that envelope does not establish open
  developmental expansion or self-revision of the envelope.
- Experiment-specific observer work still diagnoses apparatus failures,
  chooses candidate repairs, freezes experiments, and promotes claims. The
  subject can carry its operational trajectory without those choices for long
  stretches, but it cannot yet refine the complete continuation mechanism
  end-to-end without them.
- One live surrender event has become useful later evidence: OT-0301 retained
  it without false success, and OT-0302's fresh actor used the enlarged contact
  to reach 6/6 versus unchanged 2/6. This is a mechanism instance, not a
  reliability estimate across goals, worlds, or models.
- The current subject is large, but no observed continuation failure is caused
  by its size. Representation compression is not the active bottleneck.

  selected `harbor-three-watches-v1`, although the stake had a unique eleven-
  point margin and the operational offer passed. Uncertain: whether the stake
  lacks behavioral selectivity or one catalog plus one arbitrary blind rule is
  simply too collision-prone to estimate it. Evidence: OT-0305 aggregate

The immediate experiment must distinguish better selection from merely
different selection. Seal the operational contact in the stake-selected world,
then compare its downstream continuation against a matched counterfactual
branch carrying the blind-selected offer from the exact preselection parent.
