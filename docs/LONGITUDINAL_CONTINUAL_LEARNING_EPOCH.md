## Outcome orientation

Continuous learning is measured across ordered inference encounters, not by
uninterrupted wall-clock activity. A fresh actor from an immutable revision or
unchanged receipted hosted deployment epoch may inherit a fully functional
external learning substrate designed by the program. No learner-controlled
model-weight update is part of the causal path. Each scored encounter must
place a prediction before independently owned contact and append the outcome
and update decision afterward. Every nonterminal encounter also binds the exact
projection consumed by the next fresh actor; the terminal state and bounded
projection remain receipted for reconstruction or an explicit audit consumer.

The first claim is that accumulated external state causes future prediction
error to improve reliably over a long changing trajectory. A later, stronger
claim asks whether consequences improve the machinery that represents,
retrieves, selects, updates, or internally assigns credit to that state. The
initial machinery need not be invented by the subject. Authoritative outcomes,
scoring, and promotion evaluation remain outside the mutable machinery.

## Claim separation

Let `C_t` denote learned content at encounter `t`: observations, beliefs,
examples, summaries, predictions, and parameters that encode only object-level
world state. Let `M_t` denote learning machinery: the functions governing
representation, selection, retrieval, update, projection, internal credit,
consolidation, or candidate-proposed adoption. A parameter belongs to `M_t`
only if changing it from the same content state under the same encounter
evidence changes how later experience is processed. Freeze this role-based
partition before execution.

`M_t` excludes independently owned outcomes, authoritative scoring, promotion
evaluation, and the controller's frozen commit rule. Those authorities remain
outside the candidate and cannot be refined by it during a claim.

- **Longitudinal continual adaptation:** `C_t` changes under an inherited,
  potentially fixed `M`, and the live lineage improves future prediction over
  the frozen controls.
- **Continual machinery refinement:** contact causes `M_t → M_{t+1}`, and the
  child machinery improves a later learning trajectory relative to its
  unchanged parent from the same starting content under matched prospective
  encounters.
- **Learning-side contribution to Open Developmental Trajectory:** repeated
  machinery changes cross certified limits of prior machinery across
  qualitatively new environments while preserving correction, retention, and
  rollback. At least the material limit-crossing change must be causally
  specified by the subject or developmental process, not synthesized by the
  controller. The complete target also requires integration with durable
  commitments whose revision retains causal standing.

Representation escape is strong evidence for the third level. It is not
required for the first or for a bounded first refinement.

## Prospective E14 longitudinal gate

OT-0075 is reserved for the candidate-free evaluator checkpoint. Its design
data, held-out evaluator anchors, and later candidate stream must be disjoint.
The design data may be used to construct and tune E14. After the evaluator
implementation is frozen, one private seed must be generated and sealed anchor
worlds must be mechanically derived from that seed and the clean implementation
identity. They are used exactly once to decide whether E14 is promoted. At
least two materially different controller-private positive references must
pass the evaluator-visible surrogate gates using only the current query and
previously released outcomes. Every fixed control must retain its
prospectively expected limitation, and the interventions must reject seeded
causal defects. A future- or hidden-truth oracle is a negative authority test,
never an admissible positive reference. No candidate or hosted output is
permitted in OT-0075.

Before evaluator implementation, freeze the anchor derivation function, domain
tag, private-seed length, implementation-identity binding, one-attempt rule, and
collision handling. Generate the private seed only after a clean implementation
exists; an implementation hash alone is not a private anchor. Do not reseed or
shop anchor worlds after derivation.

The checkpoint must freeze and exercise a long prequential stream with multiple
changes and at least one recurring regime. It must predeclare:

- horizon, task order, change points, recurrence schedule, and replication;
- prediction-before-outcome and update-after-outcome ordering;
- cumulative and rolling prequential loss;
- any evaluation-only held-out probes whose outcomes never reach the updater
  during the claim window;
- excess-error area and recovery latency after each change;
- retention or relearning savings when an earlier regime returns;
- paired live-state versus frozen-snapshot lift at later checkpoints;
- exact active-state and projection budgets; and
- numerical promotion thresholds and failure handling.

Treat independently derived streams—not their internally dependent
encounters—as the inferential units. Keep the number of streams, pairing,
familywise decision rule, and intention-to-treat handling of missing or invalid
predictions frozen.

The updating lineage must be compared with no persistence, the immutable seed
state, outcome-free clock state, and the strongest frozen fixed rule. Lossless
history, recent verbatim history, and naive retrieval are adaptive comparisons
whose exact trajectories are reported without forced failure. When their
online consequence-driven state improves later prediction, they are alternate
positive substrates; beating them is a stronger compression or inductive-
efficiency claim. The gate also requires consequence withholding and
misbinding, update-without-projection, projection-without-update, and lineage or
state-identity interventions. Downstream application must be controller-
deterministic or use a
prospectively powered paired stochastic design with counterbalanced order,
replicated lineages, and an identical-substrate/projection placebo. A clean
reproduction must reconstruct every state transition from the seed plus ordered
receipts.

For the calibration references and every later actor-bearing lineage, bind a
fresh consumer-process and empty-workspace receipt at every encounter, prove
that only the declared projection crosses the boundary, and execute
forbidden-channel sentinels. Rewind a frozen checkpoint, reproduce the same
suffix byte-exactly, execute an alternate suffix in an isolated sibling branch,
and reject cross-branch projection substitution.

Raw error need not decrease monotonically when the world changes. Promotion
requires preregistered comparative trajectory improvement, recovery within
frozen bounds, retention or faster relearning, replication, and loss of the
advantage under decisive causal ablation.

Only a complete held-out anchor pass promotes E14 and may authorize one
actor-bearing candidate. After that promotion, freeze the candidate seed
substrate and updater implementation before deriving a fresh candidate stream
from a hidden post-implementation seed mechanically bound to the clean
implementation identity. Freeze its derivation function, domain tag, one-
attempt rule, and collision handling before candidate implementation; do not
reseed or shop tasks. The candidate's reachable code, prompt, workspace,
projection, and tool surfaces must exclude the task seed, regime schedule and
change points, reference answers, held-out probes, evaluator instructions, and
control winners. The candidate stream cannot reuse the design or anchor worlds.

The candidate-free checkpoint calibrates only an evaluator-visible surrogate
path. It cannot establish fresh base-model identity, actor behavior caused by
the inherited substrate, or continual learning; those claims require the later
actor-bearing causal path.

## Later machinery-refinement gate

Only after a longitudinal foothold should an experiment permit a bounded
change to one learning operation. Every candidate change requires a versioned
parent, exact delta, proposal and adoption receipts, a common starting content
identity and matched future encounters for parent/child comparison, separate
author/tune, adoption-validation, and sealed post-adoption confirmation
partitions, proposal and trial cost accounting, decisive delta ablation, and
recoverable rollback. Freeze the proposal and commit rule before validation; if
validation selects among alternatives, only a disjoint confirmation window can
establish future benefit.

Parent and child application must be controller-deterministic or use the
prospectively powered paired stochastic design and pass an identical-machinery
placebo. Downstream content may diverge as a causal consequence of the machinery
delta; receipt that divergence rather than forcing post-fork content equality.

The changed machinery must improve later learning rather than only the episode
that proposed it. Later contact must make an adopted change incomplete or
harmful and cause revision, replacement, set-down, or rollback. One successful
change is a bounded foothold; multiple successive improvements are required for
a continual meta-learning claim. Calling it self-refinement additionally
requires the subject's output to specify the useful delta or update operation;
a researcher-supplied option selected by a fixed controller is only system-level
machinery adaptation.
