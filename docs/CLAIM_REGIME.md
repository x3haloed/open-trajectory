# Claim regime G3 — subject-first evidence

## Why G2 was insufficient

G2 correctly separated causal validity, generative reproducibility, and actor
provenance. It still treated the experiment as the primary process: freeze a
task itinerary, run a candidate, compare controls, and stop. That shape can
measure a developmental subject while also preventing the subject from owning
what happens next.

G3 keeps G2's epistemic separation and adds a second separation:

1. **Operational validity:** did a clean, consequence-bearing subject
   transition actually occur?
2. **Causal interpretation:** what intervention caused the difference, and how
   strongly do controls support that claim?
3. **Generative reproducibility:** how often does fresh generation reproduce it?
4. **Actor provenance:** which model or deployment produced the actor artifact?

Failure in a later dimension narrows the claim. It does not rewrite a valid
earlier dimension.

The transition anchors are independent of G3's preferred wording: existing OT
evidence already requires fresh actors, exact ancestry, independent outcomes,
bind-before-consequence, protected authority, and deterministic causal
comparison; the external continuing-subject lineage produced valid operational
progress that the fixed-itinerary regime could not naturally represent; and
the program's stated outcome now explicitly requires the researcher to become
optional for continuation. G3 preserves every hard anchor and changes which
process owns the next move.

## The subject and observer

The subject is the exact inherited state plus the machinery and receipts that
make its next developmental move mechanically reachable. The observer is the
research apparatus that owns hidden worlds, scores, claim comparisons, and
publication.

The observer may define a seed, world interface, authority boundary, admission
rule, observation budget, and causal ablations. It may not silently choose each
next pursuit and then attribute continuation to the subject.

An observation may end while the subject remains open. A model turn ending, an
invalid encounter, a failed sibling, or an exhausted experiment budget is not
subject termination. Resume from the last sealed valid subject unless the
subject's admitted state or independently owned consequence closes it.

## Operational transition gate

A transition may join the subject when all claim-relevant conditions hold:

- exact parent identity and declared mutable surface;
- fresh actor context and fresh workspace;
- actor input, output, tool trace, and actual effects retained;
- hidden outcome, evaluator, evidence, acceptance, and credential authority
  remain outside actor control;
- the actor artifact is bound before hidden consequence;
- independent world consequence supports the transition;
- the declared no-regression or developmental-admission checks pass; and
- the successor is sealed before diagnostics or controls.

Broad actor tools are compatible with this gate. Isolation, trace audit,
protected authorities, and quarantine establish validity; artificial actor
incapacity is not the default.

## Causal claim gate

A causal claim additionally requires the cheapest decisive ablation and a
matched control appropriate to the mechanism. Controls are observational: they
may establish, narrow, or defeat the claim, but they cannot mutate, delay, or
retroactively veto the sealed operational lineage.

Prospective freezing applies to the **decision envelope**, not necessarily a
fixed itinerary. Freeze world derivation, visible information, authority,
allowed self-direction, score, falsifier, comparison, and observation stopping
rule. Within that envelope a subject may author contact machinery, choose a
pursuit, schedule a retry, or leave a new opening.

## Claim scopes

| Scope | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `operational-transition` | One exact clean subject transition passed independent consequence and admission. No causal-frequency claim. |
| `causal-observation` | A matched intervention and decisive ablation establish the bounded mechanism once. |
| `recurrent-operation` | One fixed driver carried multiple subject-selected phases across fresh actor endings without experiment-specific intervention. |
| `generatively-reproduced` | Fresh generations repeated under a frozen sampling or deployment contract. |
| `open-developmental-evidence` | Recurrent operation transfers across materially different worlds while commitments and continuation machinery remain corrigible. |

These scopes accumulate only when their own evidence is present. A more open or
mutable system does not earn a stronger scope by description.

## Material deviations

A post-output deviation invalidates a transition or causal claim when it can
affect actor-visible information, hidden outcomes, evaluator or acceptance
authority, branch comparability, the claimed operation, safety, or privacy.
Uncertain materiality fails closed for that run.

A disclosed mismatch that cannot affect those properties removes only the
unsupported conformance claim. Missing immutable hosted receipts limit actor
attribution and generative reproduction, not a complete deterministic causal
observation over retained outputs.

## Evaluation transition

G3 prospectively replaces G2. Existing raw artifacts, exact receipts, objective
checks, negative results, and G2 causal-validity conclusions remain evidence.
Any conclusion that depended on “the experiment stopped, therefore the subject
stopped,” on mandatory fixed task order, on tool denial as the safety model, or
on controls having authority over a sealed operational successor is stale.

Prior results are reclassified lazily when they become decision-relevant. No
historical score is automatically improved by this transition.
