## What became known

successful causal exoskeletons. This epoch did not establish that a persistent
process can reproducibly construct, correct, transfer, and integrate its own
developmental machinery. It did establish a narrower boundary for the current
locked model, fresh-context harness, bounded carriers, and one-turn proposal
interface.

Across multiple prospectively valid candidate families, independently measured
consequences sometimes elicited useful new structure. The failure was not
simple carrier inexpressibility. The failure was reliable complete-lineage
construction and correction: useful structure was not produced, preserved, or
reconstructed across every required worker and contradiction without moving
selection or repair authority into the controller.

## Precise obstruction at epoch close

The smallest reproduced obstruction is:

> Under complete resets, the epoch's one-turn full-state proposal interface
> does not reliably convert independently receipted discrepancy against a
> harmful inherited structure into an exact valid structural successor across
> two lineages, even when the same model can infer the structure from scratch
> and the carrier is prospectively proven sufficient.

The interface coupled four causal jobs into one actor output:

1. interpret completed consequences;
2. detect which inherited structural commitments are contradicted;
3. construct a complete bounded successor; and
4. make that successor eligible for safe adoption.

Safe contact-perfect commit prevents silent drift, but a rejected proposal
leaves no candidate-owned, causally persistent record of which structural
relations failed. Branching does not solve this when no viable branch exists,
and forced fresh revision can degrade a zero-error proposal. Retrying a prompt,
task, grammar, grouping, state-machine topology, or threshold would therefore
be another attempt inside a closed assumption, not a scientifically distinct
test.

This is a bounded obstruction, not a claim that endogenous development is
impossible for other models, richer independently receipted interactions, or
different substrates.

## Smallest scientifically distinct next affordance

Another candidate becomes warranted only after a candidate-free calibration
of a discrepancy-to-revision substrate with all of these properties:

- an actor-authored provisional structure is executed against completed
  independent contact before final adoption;
- the exact proposal-local structural failures, not a researcher diagnosis,
  can cross a complete reset as a bounded receipted object;
- a later fresh process can preserve a contact-perfect proposal, revise an
  imperfect one, or set one down without a controller selecting the best
  candidate on its behalf;
- ablation of that inherited discrepancy object removes any revision
  advantage;
- the learned reconstruction or update operation is symbol-generic rather
  than a copied full solution;
- later evidence changes make that operation itself incomplete or harmful and
  require structural revision of the operation, not only new contents; and
- exact parent preservation, committed-successor rollback, provenance, equal
  budgets, and future correction remain causal.

This is more than another actor turn or error-count hint. It requires a new
bounded authority object and a prospective causal separation among proposal,
independent validation, revision, and adoption. The repository does not yet
contain a calibrated substrate meeting those conditions. That missing
affordance was the smallest reason another run would have been scientifically
different.
