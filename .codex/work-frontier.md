# Work Frontier

## Outcome

Develop a continuing subject whose exact state carries consequence, unfinished
pursuit, and executable reopening across fresh actors until useful continuation
no longer depends on experiment-specific researcher choices.

## Goal invariants

- A turn, run, control branch, or observer budget may end without closing an
  open subject.
- Independent world consequence, not self-report or researcher preference,
  decides what joins the subject.
- Fresh actors receive continuity only through the exact admitted subject
  projection.
- Actors retain the declared real tool condition. Isolation, trace audit,
  sealed authority, and quarantine protect validity.
- Valid operational successors are sealed before controls; controls govern
  claims, not the subject.
- Preferred progress preserves earned floors, opens coherent future contact,
  improves selection or correction, and reduces hidden researcher causation.
- Exact history may grow while it remains reconstructible and does not prevent
  continuation.

## Evaluation regime

G3 separates operational validity, causal interpretation, generative
reproducibility, and actor provenance. Freeze an authority and admission
envelope rather than a mandatory itinerary. Record observer and subject
dispositions separately.

Anchors: fresh contexts/workspaces, exact ancestry, bind-before-consequence,
independent outcomes, protected evaluator/evidence/acceptance authority,
no-regression admission, deterministic reconstruction where claimed, privacy,
and preserved negative evidence.

## Prediction errors

- OT's fixed-task, stop-at-budget workflow was not neutral: it made the research
  apparatus a hidden causal component of continuation.
- Tool denial was an unnecessarily narrow safety model. Broad tools are
  compatible with valid evidence when effects are isolated, audited, and
  quarantined.
- The current recurrence evidence remains bounded to one synthetic world
  family; cross-world subject-owned continuation is open.
- Scheduler, world interface, seed priorities, and admission remain external.
  Future experiments should expose and reduce these dependencies one at a time.
