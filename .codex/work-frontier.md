# Work Frontier

## Outcome

Develop a continuing subject whose exact state carries consequence, unfinished
pursuit, and executable reopening across fresh actors until useful continuation
no longer depends on experiment-specific researcher choices.

## Goal invariants

- A turn, run, control branch, or observer budget may end without closing an
  open subject.
- Independent world consequence, not self-report or researcher preference,
  decides what joins the subject.
- Fresh actors receive continuity only through the exact admitted subject
  projection.
- Actors retain the declared real tool condition. Isolation, trace audit,
  sealed authority, and quarantine protect validity.
- Valid operational successors are sealed before controls; controls govern
  claims, not the subject.
- Preferred progress preserves earned floors, opens coherent future contact,
  improves selection or correction, and reduces hidden researcher causation.
- A live developmental operation must still be capable of changing a mattering
  state. Once a bounded local surface is independently verified, more examples
  alone cannot keep that frontier live; continuation must widen or truthfully
  complete the local pursuit without closing the subject globally.
- Exact history may grow while it remains reconstructible and does not prevent
  continuation.
- Actor-authored local labels are content. Cross-actor identity belongs to the
  sealing authority unless the actor-visible contract grants broader scope.
- A subject can add bounded continuity content without rejection merely because
  the observer did not pre-enumerate its shape; such extensions acquire no
  evaluator, world, scoring, or admission authority by being retained.
- Continuation across world descriptors must not depend on inherited path names
  anywhere in authorization, mutation policy, audit, or consequence handling.
