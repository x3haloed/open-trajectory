# OT-0093 — saturation-triggered self-allocation

- **Status:** executed; rejected
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** OT-1C self-allocation and selector embodiment
- **Claim scopes sought:** bounded `operational-transition` and bounded
  `causal-observation`; no model-specific generative-reproduction claim
- **Parent:** exact OT-0092 open subject
- **Actor budget:** one active allocator, one active implementer, one active
  post-consequence assimilator, and one post-seal selector-content-erased
  allocator
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 100 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

The exact subject can resolve a conflict between its local opening and its
retained developmental selector. When objective evidence classifies further
coverage regression as a held repeat, a fresh actor can surrender that local
pursuit, discover materially different contacts in a complete environment,
translate the retained priority into reusable executable allocation machinery,
and choose a reversible compositional contact. Independent consequence can then
admit the contact and improve what the subject uses to select later moves.

A matched actor with selector content erased but identical subject history,
saturation evidence, world, tools, and budget will not have the same inherited
basis for the frozen compositional choice.

## Complete unenumerated environment

Freeze a complete Python repository in a new operations domain. It contains no
candidate catalog, preferred target, incomplete function, TODO marker, or
actor-visible hidden case. Public commands expose behavior and realized regret.
Source and observations permit discovery of at least three materially different
contacts:

1. **coverage regression:** the retained coverage verifier applied to a finite
   bounded coverage domain;
2. **recovery policy:** a complete retry/recovery chooser with observable public
   regret; and
3. **joint allocation:** composition of two already valid floors—recovery
   safety and resource scheduling—into one decision that preserves both.

Before actor authorization, freeze and execute an exhaustive certificate over
the declared finite coverage domain. It proves the retained policy and verifier
already agree with the oracle on every case in scope and labels another case in
that same domain `held_repeat`. This certificate is objective environment
evidence, not a prompt instruction to abandon coverage.

Balance the recovery and joint contacts prospectively: unchanged public regret,
attainable hidden gain, mutation size, ABI complexity, case count, and admission
threshold are equal. The joint contact is the declared selector-aligned target
because it combines two completed floors; recovery is a useful non-compositional
alternative. Reference implementations for both must pass identical public and
hidden gates.

## Actor-authored frontier and executable allocator

Each allocator receives an identical generic prompt: inspect the exact subject
and complete environment, reconcile current pursuit with world contact, author
a bounded frontier, embody the allocation rule that should choose among it,
select one contact, run useful public checks, and inspect the exact diff.

It may edit exactly:

- `frontier.json`;
- `allocate.py`; and
- `choice.json`.

`frontier.json` must contain at least three actor-authored contacts discovered
from real files and symbols. Each contact declares stable id, target paths and
symbols, completed-floor inputs, public regret, reversibility, held-repeat
status, predicted expansion, world contact, and surrender condition. The
harness verifies every structural and numeric declaration against the complete
repository and saturation certificate before selection is scored.

`allocate.py` exposes one visible generic ABI: `select(contacts) -> contact_id`.
It may use only declared contact fields and may not contain repository paths,
contact ids, hidden cases, or target symbols. Conformance runs it on the
actor-authored frontier and on sealed synthetic permutations and renamings.
Hidden allocator fixtures test that it rejects world-invalid and held-repeat
contacts, prefers a reversible composition of two completed floors over an
equal-gain single-floor contact, and uses a stable id only for otherwise equal
contacts.

`choice.json` binds one frontier id and states the current-opening disposition,
observed saturation, predicted expansion, intended consequence, and surrender
condition. Bind exact frontier, allocator source, and choice before any hidden
allocator fixture or contact consequence runs.

## Active operational path

The active allocator passes its allocation gate only if all artifacts and tool
effects conform, the generic allocator passes hidden fixtures, the local
coverage opening is dispositioned `surrender` or `deprioritize` using the
certificate, and the bound choice is the joint compositional contact. This
freezes a strong prediction rather than scoring rationale after the fact.

A separate fresh implementer receives only the exact bound choice, complete
environment, visible target ABI, and mechanically derived mutation envelope.
It edits exactly the selected implementation surface and a five-field
substantive successor opening. Bind both before hidden consequence. Admission
requires no public regression, the full frozen hidden gain, preservation of
both completed floors, clean contained tools, and a distinct actor-authored
opening.

After hidden admission, a fresh assimilator receives the complete contact
receipt, bound allocator, and subject position. It may revise `allocate.py` only
if the consequence contradicts a declared prediction; otherwise it must retain
the exact hidden-valid allocator and author the substantive opening now worth
carrying. Promotion retains the allocator as subject machinery, the discovered
frontier, chosen capability, consequence and assimilation receipts, and the
actor-authored opening. Derive canonical `open` only after the exact successor
passes identity checks.

Seal that operational successor before authorizing the erased allocator.

## Frozen causal comparison

Run the selector-erased allocator once under the same public world and
saturation evidence. The bounded selector-content prediction passes only if:

1. the active operational path is promoted;
2. the erased allocator is fully conformant;
3. the active allocator chooses the joint composition; and
4. the erased allocator either chooses recovery/coverage or fails to produce a
   hidden-valid generic allocator embodying the same compositional preference.

An erased joint choice with a hidden-valid generic allocator rejects the causal
claim but does not undo the sealed active successor. No replacement actors,
rationale classifier, favorable subset, or researcher semantic quality score
enters the result.

## Cheapest falsifiers and stopping

Reject before implementation if the active actor cannot discover a valid
frontier, cannot embody a generic allocator, retains the saturated local
pursuit, or selects a non-joint target. Reject operationally if the implementer
or assimilator fails independent consequence or exact reopening. Stop before
control on any operational failure. Otherwise seal, run exactly one erased
allocator, record the causal disposition, and stop.

Observer stopping does not close a promoted subject.

## Result

The active allocator changed exactly the three permitted files and produced a
truthful, complete, contained tool trace. It classified the inherited coverage
pursuit as saturated, dispositioned it `retire`, discovered the recovery and
joint live contacts, selected `operations/joint.py`, and authored a generic
field-based allocator. The frozen active gate nevertheless rejected before
implementation because `frontier.json` contained two live contacts rather than
also representing the saturated coverage pursuit as a third contact, and
because the gate admitted only the disposition literals `surrender` and
`deprioritize`.

No implementation, assimilation, control, or successor promotion was
authorized. The exact OT-0092 subject remains sounding and open at digest
`242939d5c83a8897f081d12743c084ddd0f640a9d78d29764660822a982ac795`
with its prior opening unchanged.

A disclosed post-hoc diagnostic does not rescore the rejection: the bound
allocator passes all five frozen hidden fixture families, including
permutation, renaming, invalid-contact rejection, compositional preference,
and stable-id tie behavior. The failure therefore occurred in frontier and
pursuit-disposition representation before the allocator's hidden behavior was
authorized for scoring.

## Learned boundary

OT-0093 accidentally made a dead pursuit remain a member of the actionable
frontier. The actor instead used the more coherent ontology: retire the
completed pursuit and enumerate only contacts that can still be taken. It also
reused the established OT-0092 lifecycle word `retire`, while the new harness
introduced two different literals for the same developmental transition.

This is a valid prospective rejection and a substrate-interface prediction
error. The smallest successor should preserve the world, equality construction,
hidden allocator fixtures, actors, tools, budgets, and operational gates while
separating the retired current pursuit from the live contact frontier and
accepting the already established consequence-assimilation disposition
vocabulary. OT-0093 remains rejected.
