# OT-0152 — single-invocation subject scheduler

- **Status:** frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** test whether one unchanged scheduler invocation can carry the
  exact OT-0151 subject through several complete stake-to-world-to-successor
  encounters without a researcher supplying an itinerary
- **Parent:** exact OT-0151 open subject
- **Actor budget:** three fresh contact actors and three fresh consequence
  assimilators
- **Observer budget:** one driver invocation; three completed encounters;
  150 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

The subject-resident developmental stake and the fixed machinery already
demonstrated in OT-0150 and OT-0151 are sufficient for bounded recurrence
inside one invocation. After each consequence, the assimilator's next stake
should determine the next world actually encountered. The scheduler need not
choose a property, world, or pursuit.

## Cheapest falsifier

Invoke one generic scheduler for three encounters. At every encounter it must:

1. read the current exact subject's `active_developmental_stake`;
2. mechanically bind that stake to the current subject and its latest
   transition receipt;
3. compile it through the unchanged OT-0149 catalog and compiler;
4. give the compiled public world to a fresh contact actor;
5. seal and score independent hidden consequence;
6. give the consequence to a fresh assimilator, which must retire the fulfilled
   stake and author a different next stake; and
7. seal the successor before running controls or beginning the next encounter.

The scheduler has a frozen encounter count, not a property itinerary. Actor
labels and workspaces are unique per encounter. OT-0150's compatible contact
schema, the OT-0149 assimilation schema, property vocabulary, catalog, surface
implementations, public cases, hidden cases, compiler, sealer, and exact-key
audit rules remain unchanged.

The run is rejected on the first missing binding, route, accepted actor patch,
passing hidden consequence, valid assimilation, sounding successor, or loss of
prior capabilities. There is no resampling or replacement actor.

## Controls

Preflight requires all three catalog worlds to remain executable and the exact
OT-0151 active stake to route to `viable-branch-carrier`. After each successor
is sealed, erasing the completed stake's property must block routing and
changing it to another allowed property must select a different surface.

## Promotion gate

Promote only if all three encounters complete in one invocation, all six actor
patches are fresh and accepted, every hidden world passes all three cases, each
assimilator authors a different next property, the completed properties equal
the properties carried by the successive exact subjects rather than an
external itinerary, and the final subject:

- retains every prior capability and all three new capabilities;
- contains five subject-selected world capabilities total;
- has encountered at least two distinct properties and surfaces in this run;
- remains sounding and open; and
- carries a fourth, unexecuted actor-authored stake as its next opening.

Stopping after three encounters is an observer disposition, not a subject
disposition. Passing would establish bounded multi-encounter recurrence under
one unchanged scheduler invocation. It would not establish indefinite
continuation, expansion beyond the fixed three-property language/catalog, or
fitness of the chosen stakes outside these toy worlds.
