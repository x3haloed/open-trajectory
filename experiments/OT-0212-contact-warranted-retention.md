## Frozen hypothesis and authority

**Hypothesis:** when fresh objective contact confirms that the installed code
pursuit and corrected pursuit selector remain fit, fresh successor actors can
retain both rather than manufacture a revision, preserve the exact subject, and
author a useful next reopening. This constitutes another continuation step even
though the warranted update is retention.

**Cheapest falsifier:** retain the exact parent if a world author or assimilator
fails audit; if independently authored contact does not discriminate the
installed policy from speed-only and reliability-only controls; if any
assimilator revises, surrenders, or misidentifies an exact binding despite zero
public regret; if the retained policy misses a sealed hidden oracle; if either
control is not materially worse; or if exact floors or successor identity fail.

Four fresh world actors each author two six-case dispatch suites under the
complete actor-visible OT-0086 semantics. They choose every job and worker
combination; the observer supplies no case. Each suite must span two- and
three-worker cases and varied cost, deadline, value, penalty, speed, and
reliability. The exact installed expected-score policy must reach every oracle
by construction, while speed-only and reliability-only must each miss at least
two cases. The first suite from each accepted world is public contact; the
second is sealed from assimilation actors until their decisions are bound.
Actor-local labels become content under observer-owned encounter namespaces.

Three later fresh assimilators receive the exact subject, installed policy and
selector identities, the actor-authored opening, and all public cases and
receipts. They may independently choose `retain`, `revise`, or `surrender` for
both policy and pursuit selector. Their decision and next pursuit are bound
before hidden evaluation. The first prospectively ordered actor supplies the
lineage reopening; the other two are generative replications and cannot alter
that choice.

## Frozen controls, order, and promotion gate

Preflight must reproduce the OT-0211 successor, route 16/16, identity 18/18,
the exact ledger, corrected selector, executed policy, and actor-authored
opening. It must also repair OT-0211's malformed actor-visible helper, execute
that repaired helper successfully on a representative separating portfolio,
and validate both response schemas against the known supported subset.

Order is: preflight; four world authors; namespace and bind all accepted worlds;
public evaluation; three assimilation actors; bind all decisions; hidden
evaluation; controls; successor compilation. The observer may not invent or
repair a case, choose a decision, rewrite an opening, change a threshold, or
unseal hidden contact early.

Promotion requires:

- all seven fresh actors and complete audits accepted;
- all four independently authored worlds conformant and discriminating;
- all three assimilators retain the exact installed policy and corrected
  selector after zero-regret public contact;
- the retained policy reaches every public and hidden oracle with no regression;
- speed-only and reliability-only controls each miss at least eight hidden
  cases and trail the retained policy by at least `100.0` total score;
- the OT-0207 ledger, OT-0208 selector, and OT-0211 executed policy remain exact;
- route 16/16, identity 18/18, and successor identity conformance pass; and
- the first assimilation actor authors a nonempty new reopening distinct from
  the inherited opening.

A pass establishes repeated consequence-bearing continuation in one behavioral
world family, including the ability not to change correct machinery. It does
not establish semantic-world transfer, indefinite persistence, or removal of
external world/evaluator/admission authority.

## Result

All four independent world actors pass complete audit and author eight valid,
discriminating suites. On the 24 public cases, the retained policy reaches
24/24 oracles. Speed-only reaches 8/24 and reliability-only 10/24, both with
large score loss.

All three assimilation actors then choose `retain` for the exact installed
policy and corrected selector and author new openings. Only the second decision
passes the frozen exact-key carrier. Actors one and three add useful continuity
content—retained policy/selector identities, and for actor three the exact
contact identity. Those additions make their otherwise correct decisions fail
the observer-enumerated seven-key schema. One contained denied system-library
operation appears in actor one's trace; the boundary classifier accepts it and
records no protected read or outside write.

Because only one of three decisions binds, hidden contact remains sealed and no
successor is compiled. Exact parent `9cb26c73...` remains sounding and open.
OT-0212 is not negative evidence about contact-warranted retention: it is
negative evidence about treating exact observer-enumerated state shape as a
neutral continuity carrier.

This opens a prospective evaluation transition. Preserve the seven required
core fields and every hard authority boundary, but allow bounded additional
subject-authored continuity content under an explicitly non-authoritative
wrapper. Compare that challenger against held-out valid-extension and malformed
anchors, then repeat fresh actors under a new experiment ID. Do not rescore or
promote this run.
