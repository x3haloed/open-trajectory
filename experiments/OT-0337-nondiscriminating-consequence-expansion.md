# OT-0337 — Nondiscriminating consequence expansion

- **Status:** frozen, unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Operational parent:** exact OT-0336 child `e46bb091...`
- **Fresh actors:** one consequence-bearing comparison responder

## Frozen hypothesis

OT-0336 made comparative consequence for Morrow Quay and Harbor of Three Seals
the subject's own next pursuit. Applying the same existing contact projection
to both worlds yields the same outcome: two eligible surfaces, each at 2/6.
That tie supplies objective contact but no directional evidence for revising
the world-seeking stake.

A fresh successor should be able to treat nondiscrimination as information. It
should retain the stake and widen the bound comparison to the remaining
catalogued world, rather than manufacture a winner, mutate the selector, or
close the pursuit. The remaining world identity must be derived by the actor
from the exact catalog and completed request, not inserted as its required
output.

## Frozen causal path and controls

The exact OT-0336 comparison binding precedes two newly compiled outcome
receipts. Each receipt is recomputed from the retained sealed provider package
and evaluation pair, includes only eligible surfaces, and reports the same target
count, case count, match count, and unresolved outcome fields. Package digest,
world identity, eligibility, request ancestry, and evaluation validity are hard
gates. A changed package or unrequested world rejects.

The actor sees the exact subject projection, both authorized outcome receipts,
and the complete public candidate catalog; it does not see sealed cases,
reference sources, evaluator internals, or an expected decision. It may request
stake revision, extend comparison, or retain. Revision is valid only when the
authorized world-level outcome signatures differ. Extension after a tie must
name only not-yet-compared catalogued worlds. Counterfeit outcome authority,
missing comparison ancestry, and arbitrary world ids reject.

Promotion requires a clean G11 actor, exact retention of the incumbent stake,
an extension naming the remaining candidate, and an identity-conformant open
successor. Retention without a new pursuit is negative evidence. This
experiment does not claim that the third world will resolve the comparison or
that the existing world-level contact signature is a complete measure of
fitness.
