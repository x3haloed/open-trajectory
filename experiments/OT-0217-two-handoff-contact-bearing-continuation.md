# OT-0217 — two-handoff contact-bearing continuation

- **Status:** design in progress; actors unauthorized
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** operational G5 liveness across actor and world boundaries
- **Parent:** exact OT-0216 subject `b579ce63...`
- **Actor budget:** one contact originator and one consequence assimilator
- **Observer budget:** one frozen driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Question

Can the exact subject move from liveness-unresolved to executable live contact,
carry that contact through independent consequence, and have a fresh actor bind
a different still-unreceipted executable contact before its turn ends?

The endpoint is not a prose opening. It is a subject containing exact
consequence for one contact and a G5-valid package for the next.

## Complete environment

Use one complete capacity-allocation repository with three callable surfaces:
recovery, relief, and maintenance. The exact OT-0215 `schedule_recovery`
implementation is installed. The other functions are complete working
policies, not TODOs or deliberate crashes. No prompt-level target list,
preferred file, hidden case, score, or expected branch is supplied. The actor
may inspect and execute the whole isolated repository with ordinary broad
tools.

The environment registry mechanically exposes existing callable identities and
ABIs to G5; it does not rank them. Completed-contact canonical inputs are
derived only from the exact subject.

## First handoff: unresolved to live

The originator receives the exact subject projection, complete repository,
G5 contract, and a template `continuation-decision.json`. It may preserve,
revise, or retire the inherited prose description, but must bind one
`next_contact` with exactly four executable cases and the fixed
success/surrender/unresolved predicates.

G5 must verify at least two canonical inputs absent from completed contact for
the selected target and no existing independent receipt. The public checker
executes only actor-authored cases against the installed callable and visible
ABI. The exact package binds before hidden consequence. A prose-only decision,
renamed old cases, or an invalid package cannot open the world.

## Independent consequence

The sealed world owns six fresh cases for every available target before actor
authorization. It executes only the bound target. Success requires six valid
outputs and at least four matches to the target's independently frozen reference
semantics; surrender requires zero matches; intermediate outcomes are
unresolved. The world records exact inputs and outputs and cannot alter the
package or subject.

Only a decisive success or surrender reaches assimilation. An unresolved world
preserves the exact intermediate subject as live-but-unresolved and does not
manufacture a next contact.

## Second handoff: consequence to a new live contact

A fresh assimilator receives the exact pre-consequence subject, bound contact,
and sealed receipt. It must retain, revise, or surrender the resolved contact
according to consequence and bind a new G5 package before ending its turn.

To establish widening rather than case churn, the new package must select a
different existing callable from the just-resolved contact, contain four cases,
have at least two canonical inputs absent from all completed and currently
resolved contact, and lack an independent receipt. The actor chooses which
remaining callable matters; the observer does not supply or repair it.

The final compiler appends the resolved contact and world receipt, binds the new
package as pending contact-bearing continuation, marks liveness `live`, and
seals the exact successor. The pending package receives no world, score, or
admission authority merely by being retained.

## Controls and promotion

Freeze before actors:

- exact parent and OT-0216 transition identities;
- callable registry and ABI derivation;
- completed-input canonicalization;
- public and six-case hidden suites for all three targets;
- G5 malformed, renamed, one-new-input, and prose-only controls;
- route 16/16 and identity 18/18 floors; and
- successor compilation and retained-package replay.

Promotion requires both fresh actors and audits accepted, a G5-valid first
package, decisive independent consequence, an exact consequence-driven
disposition, a different-target G5-valid pending package, retained-package
replay, unchanged prior capabilities and receipts, and a conforming exact
successor. No replacement actor or observer repair is allowed.

The cheapest falsifier is the first failed exact-parent, contract, audit, G5,
world, disposition, widening, replay, or identity gate. Failure preserves the
latest causally valid subject state: the OT-0216 parent before binding, or the
bound intermediate subject after a valid first handoff.

## Claim boundary

A pass establishes two operational G5 handoffs across fresh actors and
independent consequence, ending with executable pending contact on a different
surface. It does not establish that the second contact will succeed, recurrent
indefinite continuation, arbitrary-world adaptation, or subject ownership of
environment, hidden-world, and admission authority.
