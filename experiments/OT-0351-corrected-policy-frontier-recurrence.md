routed policy correction and names the first as its next opening. The hypothesis
is that one unchanged driver can materialize and complete all four contacts
through fresh actors, let each actor choose the next remaining contact, derive

choice outside the carried frontier, public or hidden contact failure, audit

from its immediate predecessor, exhaustion becoming closure, or learned-policy
