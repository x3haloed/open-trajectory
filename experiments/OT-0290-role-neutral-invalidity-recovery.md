# OT-0290 — Role-neutral invalidity recovery

- **Status:** bounded result passed
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only

## Hypothesis

The invalid-encounter reopening mechanism can operate across actor roles. A
rejected provider encounter can become a non-authoritative scar, preserve the
waiting subject, and re-derive `renew-world-feed` without admitting the rejected
package. A fresh provider in a standalone contained workspace can then reach
G10-clean independent package consequence and discharge the active reopening
while the subject remains waiting and open.

## Frozen mechanism and apparatus change

Generalize invalidity recognition from the correction role to any operation
only when actor and G10 reject, the sandbox denial is retained, no world or
feedback exists, and final subject equals source. The scar binds operation,
invocation, and audit digest but excludes candidate patch, output, decision,
package, and implied consequence. It installs one active reopening whose next
operation is the rejected operation.

On a valid recovered provider output, append a recovery receipt and clear the
active reopening. The receipt may bind package and audit digests but not package
source. The standing wait, ledger, epochs, renewal policy, and prior scars remain
exact. The new package remains external until scanner-mediated wake.

The sole apparatus change is live evidence/workspace placement outside `$REPO`.
Actor tools remain broad; G10, exact-effect checks, scanner, package evaluator,
and protected repository boundary are unchanged. The rejected Bellwether bytes
must be absent from the new actor seed and may not be promoted.

## Prospective gates and limits

Both exact OT-0285 correction invalidity and OT-0289 provider invalidity must
qualify under the role-neutral rule; accepted, G10-passing, world-bearing,
feedback-bearing, or subject-changing variants fail. Scar compilation preserves
each rejected operation. A fixture recovery clears only the active reopening,
keeps operational state exact, and remains scanner-compatible. Route 16/16 and
identity 18/18 remain floors.

Any G10 weakening, in-repository live workspace, rejected-candidate leakage,
changed operational state, retry, invalid package, or missing scanner
availability rejects. Passing is one bounded cross-role recovery, not a general
reliability rate or proof that external placement eliminates every denial.
Live output remains sealed until design and passing preflight are pushed.
