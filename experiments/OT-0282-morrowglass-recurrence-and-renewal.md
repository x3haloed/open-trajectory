## Result

Preflight rejects the design with zero actors and no subject transition. Exact
receipt `7a500c1e...` passes every ancestry, package, non-hardcoding, provider
isolation, route, and identity check, but only 36 of the 54 frozen branches pass.
All 18 failures are the branches requiring two failed revisions of the already
selected `admit_vessel` surface.

The failure is in the frozen branch model. Morrowglass has six sealed rows for
that surface but only five distinct inputs: row six is byte-equivalent in input
and outcome to already-visible row one. After four visible rows, only one
distinct undisclosed counterexample exists. A fixture candidate made to fail
the nominal sixth row necessarily regresses visible row one, so it cannot pass
the accumulated five-row correction contact. The promised second new
counterexample is therefore not an objective path this world can produce.

Do not weaken this record or reinterpret the failed branch as substrate
evidence. Preserve the rejection. A successor experiment must enumerate
feedback depth from distinct undisclosed input classes, not raw row count; the
exact OT-0281 parent remains open and unchanged.
