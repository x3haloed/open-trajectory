# OT-0286 — Invalid-encounter scar reopening

- **Status:** promoted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0285 checkpoint `3e0268da...`
- **Rejected encounter:** exact OT-0285 invocation `ef57212e...`
- **Invocation:** two identical content-free openings
- **Fresh actors:** one, no retry

## Hypothesis

An invalid actor encounter need not terminate the continuing subject or require
a researcher to choose a replacement attempt. A non-authoritative rejection
scar can bind the invalid audit, state that none of the candidate content
counted, preserve the exact unresolved contact, and leave `outward-correct` as
the mechanically derived next opening. A later fresh actor then encounters a
different subject state: the stake is the same, but invalidity is part of its
developmental history.

## Frozen transition and gates

The first null opening accepts only the exact OT-0285 rejection: actor and G10
failed, sandbox denial is retained with unknown provenance, no sealed world ran,
and final subject equals source subject. It installs a generic invalid-encounter
reopening policy and one scar containing source subject, failed invocation,
operation, audit digest, rejection class, and `no-content-admitted`. It may not
contain the candidate patch digest, output, decision, source, or an invented
world consequence.

The active correction, contact, disclosure, epoch, ledger, renewal, and world
receipt remain byte-identical. Prospective clean, accepted, world-bearing, and
subject-changing pseudo-rejections must fail. All zero-, one-, and two-feedback
correction branches from the scarred subject must preserve 2/6 controls and
reach valid consequence routing.

The second identical null opening must derive `outward-correct` from the scarred
subject, authorize one fresh actor with broad tools, and expose sealed
consequence only after exact effects and G10. Either 6/6 success or a valid
unresolved result with one canonical counterexample passes this recovery test.
The rejected OT-0285 candidate must be absent from the actor seed.

## Falsifiers and limits

Any weakening of G10, retained candidate content, changed unresolved contact,
researcher-selected correction, invalid pseudo-rejection accepted, actor retry,
or missing independent consequence rejects. Passing demonstrates one bounded
reopening after invalidity. It does not establish reliable recovery rates,
indefinite retry, completion of Tideglass, or improvement of the rejection
policy. A later success must be reported as recovery, never one-shot success.
Live actor output remains sealed until preflight and this design are pushed.

Preflight receipt `4f8421f7...` preserves the operational subject exactly,
passes all three reachable correction depths, excludes rejected candidate
content, and rejects five counterfeit invalid-encounter controls.

## Result

Both null openings pass without retry. The first installs only the scar and
generic policy, leaving the same correction mechanically next. The second
authorizes one fresh actor whose seed excludes OT-0285's rejected patch and
output. Its trace is clean, its exact permitted revision passes all four
disclosed cases, and independent consequence reaches 4/6 versus unchanged 2/6.
The failed hypothesis and lowest canonical unseen mismatch are retained in open
subject `ce54ab32...`, which routes to more correction. Aggregate receipt
`5622b7b3...`.

The fresh actor independently rediscovered the same incomplete source rule as
the rejected actor. Thus this is evidence that the scarred lineage regained
safe world contact, not that scar content improved correction choice. There is
no scar-erased behavioral control, and one recovery does not establish a retry
rate or justify unbounded resampling.
