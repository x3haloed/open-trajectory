## Frozen evaluator repair

Every case must still execute the authored expression correctly, return a
nonempty subset of options, exclude blocked members, and make prediction equal
the target. Novelty is evaluated over the complete contact:

- compute the authored target and each earned target for every case;
- reject if any one earned function matches the authored target on every case;
- permit an individual boundary case to coincide with an earned output.

This distinguishes a new function from a demand for pointwise disjointness.
The public structural checker, target expression, renewal binding, contact
schedule, hidden token transformation, and all floors are unchanged.

## Frozen promotion gate

Require all twelve fresh audits, active suite alignment 6/6, control alignment
at most 2/6, advantage at least four, exact retained renewal and completion
receipt, candidate reconstruction, route 16/16, identity 18/18, and an open
successor. On passage install the exact renewal, active-01 contact and sealed
consequence, then open mechanism invention for the subject-authored target.
On failure retain exact parent.

Passing establishes one subject-authored target-language expansion that
causally constrains later contact. It does not establish mechanism invention,
external usefulness, or recurrent autonomous renewal.
