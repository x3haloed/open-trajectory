## Frozen mechanism language

An invented mechanism is a named executable set-expression AST. Leaves may read
only `options`, `outcome`, or `blocked`; `prediction` is forbidden. Internal
operators are `difference`, `intersection`, and `union`, with maximum depth
four. The actor also binds the mechanism to target `latent-unblocked`.

This language can express `options - outcome - blocked` but does not prescribe
that expression. Every candidate is executed against the exact sealed OT-0197
hidden contact. No actor may alter cases, expected outputs, old routes, or
scores.

The causal claim requires active 6/6, control at most 2/6, and advantage at least
four. Failure of that comparison narrows attribution but does not erase a valid
operational transition.
