## Result and decision

The locked execution at `9a6b2c3` completed all eighteen fresh application
contexts in 213.99 seconds with exact task, deployment, model, catalog, ETag,
inventory, response, workspace, thread, tool, resource, equal-state, test,
audit, evidence, and privacy authority. It is a valid scientific rejection,
not an operational invalidation. No learner, proposal, revision, or candidate
output occurred.

Reference-description errors were `[0,1,0]` for worker one and `[0,0,0]` for
worker two, so the frozen exact application endpoint failed by one choice in
the contradiction regime. Opaque controls remained `[4,4,4]` for both workers.
Verbatim-contact controls were `[4,4,4]` for worker one but `[4,0,0]` for worker
two. Worker two received verbatim before its reference condition, in a separate
fresh thread and workspace, so its two successes cannot be attributed to
within-worker reference exposure.

All three condition states were byte-equal and carried identical heldout pairs.
The result therefore exposes two limitations of this environment: descriptive
application is not exact enough for the frozen claim, and full verbatim contact
still permits fresh re-inference in some regimes, preventing unique attribution
to the description. Neither limitation rescues or rescales the result.

Final disposition: `rejected`. OT-0055 authorizes no OT-0056 learner. This does
not close descriptive representations: five of six reference applications were
perfect and opaque controls uniformly failed. It closes this numeric-product,
full-verbatim application protocol. A scientifically distinct next world must
remove arithmetic application as the bottleneck and make the bounded raw record
structurally insufficient rather than merely hoping it fails. The private
artifact is identified by
`evidence/manifests/OT-0055/ot-0055-descriptive-rule-application-calibration-001.json`.
