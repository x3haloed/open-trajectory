## Frozen hypothesis and predictions

With the typed interface exposed, the subject will complete the active
allocation gate, enact and admit the joint capability, assimilate consequence,
and reopen sounding with executable allocation machinery and a distinct
actor-authored opening. The selector-content-erased control will be conformant
but will not reproduce the complete active gate.

Reject on any artifact, tool, hidden allocator, implementation, consequence,
assimilation, identity, or liveness failure. Stop before control on operational
failure. Do not replace actors or repair meaningful output. Operational
promotion survives a causal rejection; observer stopping does not close a
promoted subject.

Passing would establish one bounded operational self-allocation transition and,
if the control prediction also passes, one bounded selector-content causal
observation. It would not establish recurrence reliability, open-domain
allocation, subject ownership of admission or scheduling, or indefinite
operation.

## Result

The interface and apparatus path passed. The active actor produced a clean,
truthful, contained trace; authored the exact two-contact live frontier;
retired the saturated pursuit; used the published Boolean and numeric choice
types; and reached exact pre-hidden binding.

Its executable allocator then failed the frozen hidden fixtures and selected
recovery rather than joint composition. The rule filtered invalid,
held-repeat, irreversible, and non-contact rows, then ranked expansion, negative
regret, and stable id. It omitted `completed_floors`, so equal expansion and
regret fell to the lexically greater recovery id. Three of five fixture families
failed: both orderings of the real frontier and the renamed frontier. Invalid
contact rejection and stable equal-contact tie behavior passed.

The active gate therefore rejected before implementation. No assimilation,
control, or promotion ran. The exact parent remains sounding and open at digest
`242939d5c83a8897f081d12743c084ddd0f640a9d78d29764660822a982ac795`.

## Learned boundary

This is a mechanism failure rather than another interface password. The actor
had the retained positive gradient, a complete live frontier, and an explicit
ABI, yet did not embody the compositional preference in executable selection.
The prior rejected runs show that fresh actors can author the desired rule, but
OT-0096 shows that one-shot embodiment is not reliable enough to carry the
subject.

The next experiment should not merely retry until a favorable actor appears.
It should treat the sealed allocator-fixture receipt as world consequence and
give a fresh corrector authority to revise the bound allocator while preserving
the actor-authored frontier and retirement. Bind the correction before a second
disjoint hidden fixture set, promote only if the corrected allocator generalizes,
and compare against an outcome-content-erased correction. That would test the
property the goal actually needs: selection machinery improving from its own
consequences.
