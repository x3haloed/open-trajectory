# OT-0323 — Applicability-routed search invocation

- **Status:** completed — promoted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0322 subject `09d58f1a...`
- **Claim:** inherited applicability state can make a fixed reusable driver
  choose whether and where retained proposal-search machinery runs, causing a
  fresh successor to expand cumulative held-out fitness without an
  experiment-specific researcher selecting the contact front.

## Frozen hypothesis

The exact subject already carries executable proposal search and the rule
`incumbent fails at least one available selection contact`. A fixed router that
applies that rule to multiple simultaneously offered, already completed contact
fronts will choose a contradicted front rather than a saturated front. A fresh
actor opened only on that subject-selected front will invoke the inherited
search, adopt a legal witness, preserve the existing 15/15 private floor, and
reach 20/20 after an orthogonal loop-only regime is added.

This tests subject-carried invocation choice through fixed machinery. It does
not claim that the actor invented the applicability rule or router: the rule
was researcher-authored in OT-0322, and OT-0323's generic executable router is
also researcher-designed and compiled before private contact.

## Cheapest falsifier

Offer two fronts at once after freezing: the first is saturated under the
incumbent and the second contains authoritative directional errors with a legal
bounded improvement. Fail if the exact applicability-bearing subject does not
route to the contradicted front, the fresh actor does not use inherited search
to repair it, the repair regresses the 15/15 floor, or applicability erasure
does not redirect the matched branch to the saturated first offer and remove
the expansion.

## Frozen protocol

1. Bind the exact parent and OT-0322 promotion receipt. Compile one generic
   router into both branches before private world generation.
2. The candidate retains the exact applicability rule. The matched ablation
   changes only `invoke_when` to null while preserving search source, router,
   outcomes, front order, tools, and actor budget. Null applicability invokes a
   declared first-offered fallback.
3. Generate a private post-freeze seed and two three-contact training fronts.
   The first reuses the call-only family and must be incumbent 3/3 with no
   improving bounded candidate. The second varies only `loop_nodes`, must be
   incumbent 0/3, and must admit a 3/3 search witness. Useful and decoy sources
   have equal byte length and equal branch, call, and comparison counts.
4. Execute the inherited search over both fronts to create public summaries.
   Run the identical retained router against those summaries and each branch's
   applicability state. Bind both routes before opening actors.
5. Open one fresh broad-tool actor per branch in separate workspaces. Each sees
   only its routed contacts, exact inherited search, route receipt, workbench,
   checker, and mutation contract. It must invoke all three tools; only the
   stake file may change. G10 effect audit remains mandatory.
6. Seal a passing candidate successor before opening the ablation actor. Score
   both on five private loop cases plus the fifteen-case prior floor.

## Frozen scoring and gates

Promotion requires every item:

- preflight identity, authority, ABI, feature-isolation, route, replay, and
  privacy fixtures pass before actor authorization;
- the same ordered front summaries feed both routers;
- the candidate route selects the second, contradicted front; the actor is
  clean, changes only the permitted stake, adopts an inherited-search witness,
  and replays 3/3;
- the candidate reaches new 5/5, preserves prior 15/15, and reaches cumulative
  20/20;
- the operational child is sealed before the control actor;
- applicability erasure routes to the first saturated front; its clean actor
  finds no improvement, retains exactly, and remains cumulative 15/20;
- the final child remains open and retains executable search, executable
  routing, exact route ancestry, and the admitted stake revision.

Any material actor-authority violation quarantines that encounter. Candidate
operational success without the causal ablation is conditional only. Otherwise
reject and reopen the exact OT-0322 parent.

## Scope

One passing run would establish one bounded causal observation that inherited
applicability can route retained improvement machinery among simultaneous
openings through a fixed driver. It is not a reliability estimate, autonomous
world generation, actor-authored scheduling, or full OT-2R recurrence.

## Result

Every frozen gate passed. The applicability-bearing route consumed the common
two-front search summaries and selected the contradicted second front. Its
fresh actor inherited the exact search source, changed only `loop_nodes` from 0
to -3, replayed 3/3, passed the checker and G10 effect audit, preserved the
15/15 private floor, and reached 20/20. The exact open child is `aba1513f...`.

The matched applicability-erased route consumed the same summaries but selected
the saturated first offer. Its fresh actor ran the same three tools, found no
improving candidate, retained the stake exactly, passed G10, and remained
15/20. Aggregate `ebb7336d...` therefore supports the bounded causal routing
claim.

After the candidate actor completed, the controller referenced the frozen
checker helper through OT-0322 rather than OT-0322's imported OT-0321 base and
raised `AttributeError`. Repair receipt `4bfbf0ac...` records the one-level name
resolution correction. The same actor output, patch, private seed, fronts, and
routes were reconstructed exactly; no actor was resampled and the world,
scoring, information, branch order, and gates did not change. The deviation is
disclosed but causally immaterial to the bounded claim.

The result leaves an important limitation: the retained rule routes on any
incumbent failure and breaks multiple eligible ties by opaque front identity.
It has not learned to distinguish a failure with reachable improvement from a
failure-bearing surface on which bounded search is already saturated.
