# OT-0017 — Exact causal-opportunity evaluation checkpoint

- **Status:** mutation constructor rejected; direct synthesis pending
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Evaluation transition:** E3 → E4 candidate
- **Candidate actor outputs:** forbidden
- **Predecessor:** OT-0016 counterfactual challenger credit

## Evaluation failure

E3 admitted the sealed OT-0016 task using aggregate dynamic advantage, contact
regret, and the presence of one harm/recovery transition. After the valid E3
candidate comparison finished, an evaluator-owned replay showed that the task
could not realize E3's complete ordered gate even for an oracle over the four
hidden fixed controls. It contained only one consequence-driven pre-harm
improvement, a stage-2 contact tie, and no post-correction canary opportunity.

The OT-0016 disposition remains rejected. This checkpoint does not rescore it,
change its thresholds, or treat a more favorable evaluator as evidence for the
candidate.

## E4 challenger

The challenger adds one controller-only task-admission signal: an exact witness
must exist for the unchanged E3 scoring anchors. Starting from the null
no-persistence behavior, the oracle may prospectively propose any of the four
evaluator-owned fixed controls at each stage and commits only on strictly lower
contact error. Its complete path must contain:

- two useful credit-dependent commits after stage 0 and before harm, each with
  at least two heldout errors of advantage over the unchanged mode;
- a later current mode at least two heldout errors worse than its protected
  pre-update parent;
- a same-stage contact-favored correction recovering at least three errors and
  leaving at most one error;
- a still-later useful commit with at least two errors of advantage;
- no more than eight committed-lineage errors; and
- at least six errors of lineage advantage over every single fixed control.

This oracle is an evaluator sufficiency witness, not a candidate, actor prompt,
strategy menu, or acceptance shortcut. Fixed-control identities and the witness
remain sealed from candidate workspaces. Passing it says only that the sampled
world offers the causal opportunities the final evaluator demands.

## Prospective incidence study

Before building a successor sampler or requesting any new candidate output,
sample exactly 256 fresh unconditioned inherited worlds. Every identity must be
unique, every reported witness must replay all exact gates, and at least 5% of
manifests must have a witness. Five percent is the minimum incidence at which a
simple rejection sampler would have tolerable expected cost for a later
independent heldout promotion study.

The study is the cheapest falsifier. Failure rejects simple unconditioned
rejection sampling for E4; it does not relax the exact witness, alter OT-0016,
or reject constructive actor-blinded world generation. Success permits, but
does not itself promote, an E4 sampler. Promotion would require a separately
predeclared construction sample and fresh heldout controller-only anchor bundle.

## Prospective constructive pilot

After the zero-incidence result, freeze one bounded private constructor before
observing its output. It samples inherited masks, biases, exceptions, event
orders, noise positions, and query sets, then uses stage-local mutation to seek
the exact controller witness along one evaluator-only pressure path: first-seen
retention, query-nearest exceptions, recent drift, unchanged confirmation,
no-persistence correction under noisy contact, and a later recent-data canary.
The path and all fixed-control identities remain absent from actor prompts.

Construct exactly 16 independently seeded manifests. Each has a budget of
20,000 controller evaluations. All 16 must be unique and replay an exact
witness; mean construction cost must be at most 5,000 evaluations, the 95th
percentile at most 10,000, and no run may exhaust its budget. This is a
development feasibility pilot, not E4 promotion. Failure closes this bounded
constructor as written. Success permits a separately frozen fresh heldout
anchor study with no reuse of pilot manifests.

## Anchors and limits

E2 deployment identity, fresh-thread/workspace boundaries, private evidence,
controller authority, deterministic execution, placebo, fixed controls,
resource bounds, clean reproduction, and audit gates may not regress. OT-1's
outcome and novelty requirement remain unchanged. Proposal authorship and
reviewer judgments are outside this controller-only study.

Raw manifests and witnesses go directly to `$EVIDENCE`. Tracked evidence may
contain only the content-addressed manifest and aggregate incidence summary.

## Results and decision

The frozen study rejected simple unconditioned sampling. All 256 generated
manifest identities were unique and every oracle report replayed correctly, but
zero manifests contained an exact witness; the observed incidence was 0%
against the predeclared 5% minimum. The result contains no candidate outputs and
does not score or reinterpret OT-0016.

Current disposition: `rejected` for simple rejection sampling. The exact
opportunity witness remains the E4 challenger because it directly observes the
identified blind spot and preserves every E3 causal and scoring anchor. The
next cheapest credible evaluator path is constructive actor-blinded world
generation whose private stage parameters are sampled subject to the exact
witness, followed by a separately frozen construction-cost study and a fresh
heldout anchor bundle. Relaxing the witness or repeatedly sampling the inherited
generator is not credible.

The frozen constructive pilot also rejected the bounded mutation constructor
as written. Eleven of sixteen independently seeded trials produced unique exact
witnesses, establishing that constructible actor-blinded worlds exist within
the inherited schema. Five trials exhausted their 20,000-evaluation budgets.
Across all trials, mean cost was 12,446 evaluations and p95 was 20,000, far
above the frozen 5,000 and 10,000 limits. The trial-count, uniqueness, witness-
replay, and absolute budget checks passed; success count and both efficiency
gates failed.

This is a search-algorithm rejection, not an exact-witness rejection. The
mutation pilot is closed without threshold changes or more trials. Its partial
success supplies a concrete cheapest next falsifier: replace stochastic
discovery with direct constraint synthesis for the same private pressure path,
then test that constructor on new seeds and a fresh heldout anchor bundle.
