# OT-0208 — cost-corrected pursuit selector

- **Status:** complete; promoted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** objective correction of OT-0207's retained
  `decision-ready-signal` pursuit selector
- **Parent:** exact OT-0207 open successor `a3bcf6b1...`
- **Actor budget:** four independent cost-world authors and four selector
  correctors
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 50 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis and authority

**Hypothesis:** after independent consequence shows that maximizing checkpoint
count consumes more effort than it returns, fresh actors can revise or surrender
the exact selector, and the changed machinery beats its unchanged parent on
sealed portfolios while preserving the completed ledger and inherited floors.

**Cheapest falsifier:** stop and retain the exact parent if any world author or
corrector fails audit, if the unchanged selector is not harmful on every sealed
portfolio, if any corrected selector misses one sealed optimum, or if the
successor loses an inherited floor.

Four fresh world actors first author two five-pursuit portfolios each. Every
candidate exposes checkpoint count, cost per checkpoint, independently
contacted surfaces, reversible branches, and blocked status. Objective net
continuation value is fixed before actors as:

`independent contacts + reversible branches - checkpoint count × checkpoint cost`

Every portfolio must have a unique live checkpoint maximum, a different unique
live net-value maximum, negative net value for the old selector's choice, and
distinct failures for yield-only and effort-only baselines. Accepted worlds are
namespaced by sealed encounter index. The first portfolio from each actor is
public correction contact; the second is hidden consequence.

Four later correctors receive the exact subject, completed-ledger consequence,
retained selector, and all four public portfolio outcomes. Each may retain,
revise, or surrender the selector by authoring one generic bounded arithmetic
score program over the five visible fields. Hidden portfolios and their scores
remain unavailable until the correction is bound.

## Frozen controls and promotion gate

The exact unchanged selector, yield-only, and effort-only programs are frozen
controls. Each must fail every hidden portfolio. Promotion requires:

- all eight fresh actors and audits accepted;
- four independent harmful worlds sealed before correction;
- all four corrected programs select the net-value optimum on 4/4 public and
  4/4 hidden portfolios;
- the exact unchanged selector selects 0/4 hidden optima;
- yield-only and effort-only each select 0/4 hidden optima;
- the corrected selector differs structurally from the inherited selector;
- the OT-0207 ledger capability and completion receipt remain exact;
- route 16/16, identity 18/18, and successor identity conformance pass; and
- the successor remains open for corrected-selector reuse on a subject-authored
  next pursuit.

A pass establishes one consequence-driven correction of pursuit-selection
machinery under a bounded cost regime. It does not establish cross-domain
utility or recurrence of the corrected selector; the next experiment must let
the subject use it to choose and execute another pursuit without an observer-
selected candidate menu.

## Result

All eight fresh actors and audits pass. Four independent world authors seal
four public and four hidden portfolios. The inherited
`decision-ready-signal` selector selects 0/4 hidden net-value optima; yield-only
and effort-only controls also select 0/4.

All four fresh correctors revise the selector to net continuation value and pass
4/4 public plus 4/4 hidden portfolios. The installed correction binding is
`ea5d27fe...`. The exact OT-0207 ledger binding and completion receipt remain
unchanged; route 16/16, identity 18/18, and successor identity conformance pass.

Open successor `ab119b11...` retains the harmful selector in history, installs
the corrected selector, and carries the objective correction receipt. Next let
the subject author a new pursuit portfolio and require the corrected selector
to choose and execute one pursuit outside this cost-calibration encounter.
