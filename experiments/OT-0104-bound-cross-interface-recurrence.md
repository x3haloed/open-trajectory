# OT-0104 — bound cross-interface recurrence

- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** OT-1C recurrence across contact interfaces and an OT-2R foothold
- **Parent:** exact OT-0103 subject at
  `5537d3e7c1e0326fe6bb4140746df2ae9419e99398bb2da09a899365cb47c172`
- **Actor budget:** one fresh contact author and one fresh consequence
  assimilator; no router and no replacement samples
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

The exact parent ends with an actor-authored `allocator-challenge` binding and
an opening that requests four novel allocator frontiers. The fixed driver must
derive its sole action from that retained binding. No researcher selects a
contact interface, runs a routing actor, or supplies a fallback after the run
begins.

The hypothesis passes operationally only if a fresh actor, opened directly on
the bound interface, authors four valid novel frontiers spanning order reversal,
a filtered decoy, the two-floor composition threshold, expansion, regret, and
stable-id ties; the retained allocator agrees with independently executed
reference semantics on every frontier; and a fresh assimilator grounds and
promotes an exact sounding successor with another registered interface bound
and its continuation still open.

The cheapest falsifier is inability to derive the action from the exact subject,
any invalid or incomplete authored frontier suite, retained/reference mismatch,
ungrounded consequence assimilation, machinery-byte change, missing next
binding, or any need for experiment-specific interface selection.

## Frozen machinery, ablation, and stopping

Reuse OT-0103's normalized audit, two-interface registry, allocator contract,
independent scorer, promotion rule, broad-tool containment, and exact subject
projection unchanged. Before actor authorization, run the actual allocator
contact fixtures and verify that exact action extraction returns
`allocator-challenge` from the parent but returns no action from a matched
projection with the final subject-authored binding removed. This deterministic
binding-erasure ablation cannot affect or veto a valid operational successor.

Seal the successor immediately after the fresh consequence and assimilation
pass. Reject if no successor is valid. Do not repair a failed authored contact,
sample a replacement, or substitute the previous joint interface. Stop after
one cross-interface cycle while preserving the final subject's bound next
action. Observer stopping is not subject termination.

Passing would establish that a subject-authored continuation can move one fixed
driver across registered world-contact families after complete actor endings.
It would not establish open-domain interface invention, indefinite recurrence,
or autonomous admission authority. The next decision should be based on the
successor's own bound interface and on whether the two-interface registry has
become the limiting scaffold.
