# OT-0117 — world-grounded selector expansion

- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** improve the exact subject's executable pursuit selector on held-out
  objective portfolios rather than append another synthetic scoring term
- **Parent:** exact OT-0116 open subject at

- **Bound opening:** the subject-selected resilience extension
- **Actor budget:** one fresh selector reviser and, only after passed world

## Frozen hypothesis

The resilience opening can become a change in the machinery that selects future
contacts, not merely another interface name. Public consequences exposing
coordination load, recovery volatility, resilience margin, and realized utility
are sufficient for a fresh successor to revise the inherited allocator. The
bound revision should then outperform the unchanged allocator on new portfolios
derived only after binding.

The cheapest falsifier is failure to load safely, preserve the parent floors,
or materially beat the unchanged selector on the first hidden derivation. No
correction or resampling is allowed in this record.

## Frozen world and actor boundary

Give the actor the exact subject opening, inherited `select(contacts)` source,
a machine-readable selector contract, and six public three-contact portfolios
with independently computed outcome rows. Contact fields include the inherited
validity, reversibility, repetition, completed-floor, expansion, and regret
fields plus `coordination_load`, `recovery_volatility`, and
`resilience_margin`. Outcomes disclose realized utility and failure cost, not
the evaluator source or hidden portfolios.

The actor may change only `selector.py` and must export `select(contacts)`. Bind
the exact source after a clean complete trace, exact truthful change, source
safety, input immutability, valid-id return, and public execution. Only then
derive one private 12-portfolio set from one sealed random seed. Eight stress
portfolios make the inherited composition-first selector prefer a fragile
contact; four floor portfolios have zero resilience shortfall and anchor its
previously useful choice. Option order and ids vary after derivation.

The independent world computes each option's utility from its declared
expansion, regret, completed-floor composition, and positive shortfall of joint
coordination load plus recovery volatility beyond resilience margin. Neither
actor nor selector may alter outcomes or scoring.

## Frozen scoring and promotion

Score revised and inherited selectors on identical hidden portfolios. Require:

- revised correctness on at least 10 of 12 portfolios;
- revised correctness on all four no-shortfall floor portfolios;
- inherited correctness on no more than 6 of 12 portfolios;
- revised total regret at most one quarter of inherited total regret;
- no input mutation, invalid id, exception, or unsafe source; and
- exact reproducibility of every oracle and score row from retained bytes.

After a passing comparison, give one fresh assimilator the exact parent,
selector binding, public observations, and world receipt. It must cite passed
portfolio ids, retain the selector bytes, and author the next continuation or a
substantive surrender. Promotion installs the revised selector as a new
allocation-machinery entry and seals an exact sounding successor before any
interpretive control.

Passing would establish one bounded OT-2 selector improvement under matched
information and resources, plus an operational subject-carried continuation.
It would not establish later corrigibility, recurrence of selector refinement,
cross-domain transfer, frequency, or indefinite continuation. The next regime
must make this improvement harmful or incomplete before corrigibility is
claimed.
