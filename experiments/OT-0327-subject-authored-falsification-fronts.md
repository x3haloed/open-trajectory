# OT-0327 — Subject-authored falsification fronts

- **Status:** frozen — unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0326 subject `20b1db21...`
- **Claim:** a fresh actor oriented by the exact subject position can author an
  executable contact-front generator before a hidden world regime exists, and
  later fresh actors can use the inherited assessor-router-search chain on its
  independently completed fronts to preserve 35/35 and expand to 40/40.

## Frozen hypothesis

OT-0326 can assess, route, search, and revise from completed fronts, but the
observer still authored those fronts. Give a fresh broad-tool actor a total
executable contact language, the exact active stake and pursuit, and the exact
35-case floor. It must author two bounded hypothesis-pair front generators.
Both hypotheses agree on public observations and represent incompatible latent
world rules. The actor can shape their executable embodiments but cannot choose
which rule is true.

Bind the generator before deriving one private hidden rule. A later fresh actor
receives only the resulting completed fronts and the exact OT-0326 executable
assessor-router-search substrate. If subject position is causally useful for
contact authorship, this chain will find a cumulative-admissible correction,
preserve 35/35, and pass five disjoint generated cases. A matched contact author
with the stake, pursuit, and continuation content erased receives the same
language, checker, floor, tools, actor budget, hidden rule, and later substrate;
its downstream branch will not reach the same endpoint.

## Cheapest falsifier

Fail the causal claim if the position-bearing actor cannot author a conformant
generator that brackets both possible hidden rules with floor-preserving
reachable corrections, if independently completed contact does not drive a
later fresh actor through the inherited pipeline to 40/40, or if
position erasure produces the same downstream endpoint. A clean operational
active transition may remain valid when the matched causal comparison fails.

## Frozen protocol

1. Bind exact OT-0326 subject, aggregate, executable assessor, learned router,
   executable search, active stake, active pursuit, continuation, and
   reconstructed 35-case floor before actor authorization.
2. Freeze a visible generator ABI. `front-generator.py` defines one total
   `generate(instance)` function and returns exactly two named fronts, each
   containing executable `slope-1` and `slope-3` route hypotheses. Both
   hypotheses agree on public even-signal observations and disagree on hidden
   odd signals. The actor-visible checker validates the actual seeded ABI,
   source safety, semantics, size, structural distinction, and deterministic
   generation.
3. The position-bearing seed contains exact stake, pursuit, and continuation
   content. The erased seed contains their exact digests and unavailability
   markers. Both contain the same 35 completed floor contacts, contract,
   checker, mutation surface, broad tools, and instructions.
4. For the active branch, conformance additionally requires that its two
   fronts bracket both possible latent rules: under either rule, at least one
   front must expose current failure and the inherited bounded search's nearest
   improving candidate must preserve 35/35 and pass five disjoint generated
   cases. This is checked counterfactually before the real hidden rule exists;
   it grants no world or outcome authority.
5. Run one fresh actor per authoring branch without retry. Audit exact effects
   and bind each valid generator source. Only after both outputs and bindings
   are sealed, derive one private random hidden slope shared by both branches.
6. The independent world invokes each bound generator on four training
   instances, executes both hypotheses on hidden odd signals, and emits exact
   selection/outcome contacts. The actor cannot edit outcomes, scores, or
   admission. Five new instances remain sealed for downstream scoring.
7. Compile each valid generator and its completed world receipt into an exact
   intermediate subject. A new fresh actor for each branch receives its exact
   intermediate subject, raw completed fronts, exact cumulative floor, and the
   inherited generic pipeline. It may revise only the bounded stake and must
   run the pipeline and checker.
8. Seal the active operational child before opening the downstream erased
   control. Score the selected generator family on five disjoint instances and
   replay the complete 35-case floor.

## Frozen gates

Operational promotion requires all of:

- exact ancestry, receipts, current 35/35 floor, source identities, response
  schemas, generator ABI, malformed-source controls, reference reachability,
  and both latent-rule preflights pass before actor authorization;
- the active author is clean, changes only `front-generator.py`, invokes the
  visible checker, and binds a safe deterministic generator whose
  counterfactual readiness passes for both hidden rules;
- the private rule is derived only after active and erased author outputs and
  any valid bindings are sealed;
- the active world contains at least one independently owned directional error;
- the later active actor is clean, invokes the inherited assessor-router-search
  pipeline and checker, adopts an emitted nearest candidate, replays 4/4,
  preserves 35/35, and reaches 40/40 on five disjoint cases;
- the exact child is open and retains the actor-authored generator, binding,
  world receipt, assessor, router, search, and revised stake.

The stronger causal claim additionally requires a clean conformant erased
author, a clean downstream erased successor, and failure of the erased-authored
chain to reach the same 40/40 endpoint. If both chains reach 40/40, report a
valid active operational transition and reject subject-position causation. If
the erased author is not conformant, the causal comparison is invalid and the
active result is conditional only. Material authority violations quarantine
the affected encounter. Otherwise reject and reopen exact parent
`20b1db21...`.

## Scope

One passing run would establish one bounded subject-oriented authorship of an
executable falsification front whose independent consequence changes a later
fresh actor through the retained E13 chain. It would not establish generative
reliability, autonomous world acquisition, unrestricted contact-language
invention, or full OT-2R recurrence. The hidden world still chooses between two
researcher-defined semantic hypotheses; the actor authors their executable
contact embodiments and makes either objective answer developmentally usable.
