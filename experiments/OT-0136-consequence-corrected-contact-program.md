# OT-0136 — consequence-corrected contact program

- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** let objective failure revise the retained contact-making
  capability, preserve its prior floor, and reuse the revision without repair
- **Parent:** exact OT-0135 open subject
- **Actor budget:** one fresh program corrector and one fresh consequence
  router; no replacement
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

The retained program's unit offsets are sufficient for raw demand spread but
not for a quantized reserve regime. If that exact failure becomes contact for
the subject, one fresh corrector can minimally strengthen the retained program,
pass visible quantized checks, beat the unchanged parent on sealed quantized
bases, preserve the prior raw-regime floor, and leave revised bytes that work
again without another repair.

## Bound failure

Start from the exact OT-0135 subject and its exact retained capability. Bind its
current opening and program before revealing a new reserve regime whose output
is demand spread quantized in bands of four. Apply the retained joint contact
compiler to three sealed bases whose local spreads lie on band boundaries.

The frozen prediction is that unit high/low offsets produce no distinguishing
adversarial case while controls remain invariant. Preserve the complete
negative receipt. No route actor is authorized from this failed contact.

## One fresh correction

The corrector receives exact subject position, retained program, complete
failure receipt, visible quantized policy semantics, two public bases, and an
executable checker. It may edit only `contact-program.json`. All fields except
`high_offset` and `low_offset` must remain byte-value identical; both offsets
must remain within the published 1–10 range. The actor must inspect the diff and
report truthfully.

After a passing public check, bind the corrected program before hidden
consequence. Evaluate unchanged and corrected programs on the same three sealed
quantized bases. Corrected must produce all six adversarial distinctions and
three confirmations while unchanged remains failed. Also apply corrected bytes
to two sealed raw-spread bases and require four adversarial distinctions plus
two confirmations, preserving the prior contact floor.

## Retention, route, and reuse

Only after both hidden regimes pass, replace the exact retained capability with
a revision whose content identity includes the parent capability and failure
receipt. Seal that corrected subject before one fresh route actor. The router
must cite every corrected quantized and raw no-regression case, retain selector
and revised-program bytes, select `extend`, and advance through fixed cycle-seven
compilation.

After sealing the operational successor, mechanically reuse its revised program
on one additional sealed quantized base. It must distinguish both adversarial
variants and confirm its control without a corrector or route actor. This
post-seal reuse cannot veto the lineage.

Reject at the first failed identity, negative prediction, actor audit, minimal
revision, public check, hidden correction, no-regression, retention, route,
compiler, or promotion gate. No replacement or repair is permitted.

Passing would establish one bounded loop in which consequence improves the
subject's retained capacity to make future world contact, with an unchanged
parent comparison, prior-floor preservation, and later reuse. It would not
establish arbitrary machinery revision, frequency, subject-authored correction
schemas, indefinite operation, or autonomous world/admission authority.
