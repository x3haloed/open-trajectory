## Prediction error and amendment

The first exact reconstruction falsified the intended unmodified continuation.
After successful correction, the ledger and retained source change before the
projection refreshes. OT-0308's coherence guard therefore classified the
receipt-valid old projection as malformed and returned
`reject-actor-facing-incoherence`, even though the inherited driver correctly
derived `refresh-opportunity-projection`.

The frozen amendment gives refresh precedence only when the old projection is
internally well formed and receipt-valid, the inherited driver derives refresh,
the last target has correction and independent-success authority, the live
resolver is error-free, and its exact opportunity set differs by removal of
that one verified target. A stale receipt, a resealed wrong removal, or missing
success authority still rejects. Coherence repair remains mandatory after the
refresh. The non-authoritative target summary also receives one prospective
canonicalizer: bare `symbol`, `path:symbol`, and `path::symbol` are consistent;
all other claims remain inconsistent.

## Hypothesis

With that narrow precedence correction, the exact subject can use its own
derived operation at every boundary to carry a correction into projection
refresh, actor-facing repair, and a fresh target-untold selection without an
observer choosing the operation or target. Independent consequence should then
return the newly selected contact to correction while the subject remains open.

## Frozen path and gates

The scheduler receives no task content. Before each operation it derives from
the exact prior successor and dispatches only `outward-correct`,
`refresh-opportunity-projection`, `repair-actor-facing-coherence`, or
`expanded-select`. Actor-bearing operations receive fresh external workspaces,
broad tools, G10 containment, consequence-earned isolation, and no retry.
Actor-free refresh and repair retain no selection, world, or scoring authority.

Promotion requires every executed operation to pass, and the observed sequence
to contain correction → refresh → repair → selection in that order. The
selection must bind a projected target, receive independent 2/6 contradiction,
append exactly one stake, and route back to correction. All inherited protected
state and the prior coherence repair remain exact. The run may continue through
later derived operations until it reaches an unsupported operation, the actor
budget, or the operation budget. A clean open state at one of those prospective
boundaries is censored observation, not task completion or experimental failure.

The cheapest falsifiers are an invalid projection obtaining refresh authority,
an operation selected by the observer, actor target leakage, hidden-content
exposure, G10 failure, an unreceipted transition, failure to cross operation
kinds, loss of protected state, or a closed/nonconformant successor.

Preflight passes the exact seven-operation success path through saturation,
three invalid-refresh controls, four report-fidelity forms, route 16/16, and

## Limits fixed before output

A pass establishes one bounded self-advanced multi-operation chain. It does not
establish indefinite continuation, reliable correction or selection frequency,
that the inherited world-seeking stake caused a choice, or autonomous handling
of the next unsupported operation.
