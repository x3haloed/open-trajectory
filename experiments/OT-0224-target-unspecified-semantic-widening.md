# OT-0224 — target-unspecified semantic widening

- **Status:** frozen; not yet run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** subject-directed widening after G7 local saturation
- **Parent:** exact OT-0223 open subject `03b6c4d6...`
- **Actor budget:** one fresh widening actor; no replacement
- **Observer budget:** one bound contact and one independent world

## Frozen hypothesis

When G7 routes a locally saturated subject to `widen`, a fresh actor given one
complete broader repository can select and bind a genuinely new semantic
surface without a researcher choosing its target. Independent consequence on
that surface should enter the exact lineage and leave a mechanically actionable
next operation.

This tests subject-directed choice within a researcher-designed environment,
not autonomous environment construction. The actor sees the full six-target
inventory: the three locally verified scheduling/allocation targets and three
new planning targets in a second module. The prompt and contract name no target,
rank, preferred family, hidden outcome, or expected choice.

## Broader environment

Freeze `operations/planning.py` and an independently owned reference with the
same visible case-object → ordered-identifier-list ABI. The installed module
contains three magnitude-greedy bounded-capacity policies:

- evacuation scheduling over population, delay, hazard probability, and effort;
- inspection prioritization over criticality, exposure, defect probability,
  and effort; and
- backup-power allocation over load, outage duration, failure probability, and
  effort.

The reference maximizes the corresponding consequence-weighted value under
capacity with stable identifier tie-breaking. Each target has six frozen hidden
cases plus the actor's four public cases. The actor may inspect installed and
public reference source and use full ordinary tools, but may edit only its
widening decision. Hidden cases, evidence, evaluator, and admission authority
remain outside its workspace.

## Contract and order

The actor must select a target absent from the exact G7 ledger and bind a
four-case contact under the visible registry. Generalized G6 projection loads
the installed/reference pair named by that registry; G7 must classify the target
`uncontacted` and dispatch `contact`. Existing verified-local targets dispatch
`widen` and cannot satisfy the actor contract.

Freeze this order before live output:

1. verify exact parent, broader registry, installed/reference execution, G6/G7
   fixtures, actor-visible checker, mutation envelope, and inherited floors;
2. open one fresh actor in one fresh workspace with the exact subject position,
   G7 ledger, complete repository, registry, completed-contact registry, and
   target-unspecified contract;
3. bind and audit the decision before hidden consequence;
4. seal an exact intermediate subject carrying the selected new surface;
5. execute the selected target's six hidden cases and retain the complete
   receipt; and
6. transition to `correct` on unresolved consequence, `assimilate` on decisive
   success/surrender, or preserve the intermediate on invalid world execution.

Do not resample the actor or substitute a different target after output.

## Falsifier, controls, and promotion

The cheapest falsifier is failure to bind a new-target G6-valid/G7-contact
package. Static controls require the empty template to reject, each old-target
fresh package to route `widen`, and all three new-target representative packages
to route `contact`. Hidden fixtures require all three installed policies to
differ from their references on four of six cases while remaining valid.

Promote only if:

- the single fresh actor and workspace audit cleanly;
- no target is present in the prompt or contract and the selected target is one
  of the three previously uncontacted surfaces;
- the exact package passes generalized G6 and G7 dispatches `contact`;
- all four public cases execute validly;
- the bound intermediate subject conforms before hidden contact;
- the six-case independent world is valid and its receipt changes the target's
  ledger state to `unresolved` or `verified-local` as warranted;
- the final exact subject remains open with phase `correct` or `assimilate`;
- old pending contact and all historical capabilities/receipts remain exact;
- route 16/16, identity 18/18, reconstruction, and privacy pass.

Passing establishes a second subject-selected semantic widening and the first
one caused by explicit local saturation. It does not establish correction on the
new module, reusable recurrence across generalized paths, autonomous world
creation, or open-ended domain discovery.
