- **Status:** design and executable conformance frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** replace ungrounded capability declarations with behavioral
  signatures and test whether a fresh actor can repair scoped selection from
  those consequences
- **Parent:** exact OT-0182 open successor plus retained OT-0183 mechanism and

- **Actor budget:** one routing repair author, twelve matched active selectors,
  and four prior-regime selectors
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 65 minutes

## Why flat capability labels are insufficient

OT-0184's actor assigned both capability labels to every mechanism. Independent
known behavior also shows that even the intended novel mechanism cannot earn an
unconditional `observed-source` bit: `options ∩ outcome − blocked` fails when
observed outcomes are absent from option enumeration. The missing object is a
scoped behavioral signature, not a stricter declaration checker.

## Frozen behavioral signature matrix

Before actor authorization, run every operative mechanism on public and sealed
disjoint probes from three regimes:

- `observation-led`: observed outcome is not contained in option enumeration;
- `blocked-option`: observed outcome equals option enumeration and contains a
  blocked item; and
- `mixed-observed-blocked`: observed outcome is enumerated, contains a blocked
  item, and option enumeration also contains an unobserved unblocked decoy.

Expected output is independently computed from each regime's established
world rule. Retain exact per-case results and compile a matrix containing only
mechanism id, regime id, pass count, case count, and receipt digest. No actor
declaration can alter earned cells.

The frozen probes predict:

- prediction correction earns observation-led only;
- viable filtering earns blocked-option only; and
- the OT-0183 composition earns blocked-option and mixed-observed-blocked, but
  not observation-led.

This prediction is a conformance fixture, not actor-visible authority until the
world receipts are sealed.

## Actor-owned routing repair

Give one fresh actor the exact parent, OT-0183 and OT-0184 failures, the
nondiscriminating actor projection, the certified public/hidden matrix, the
retained floor ownership, and a complete route schema. It may retain or author
one deterministic mapping from the three regime ids to operative mechanisms.
The public checker enforces complete regime coverage, known ids, one route per
regime, exact surrender/history retention, and substantive change. It does not
prescribe any route or expose the later mixed world.

Bind the repair before later contact. Compile the OT-0183 mechanism, its
authority, the certified signature matrix, and the actor route into a
provisional exact subject.

## Matched comparison and contextual floors

Run six fresh selectors with the complete route and six with exact route-value
erasure. Both receive identical parent, mechanism, authority, signature matrix,
legacy guide, active stake, context id `mixed-observed-blocked`, tools, and
budget. Alternate order and bind all choices before six new mixed cases open.

Require repaired selection/contact 6/6, erased control at most 2/6, and
advantage at least four. Then run two fresh repaired selectors under disjoint
`observation-led` contact and two under disjoint `blocked-option` contact. They
must select prediction correction and viable filtering respectively and pass
completely. The retained identity-extension route must remain 18/18.

Promote only if all seventeen actors pass complete trace and effects audit,
the certified signature fixtures and matched comparison pass, prior contexts
retain their exact floor owners, surrendered/history boundaries remain exact,
and the successor stays open. Install the behaviorally certified matrix and
actor-authored routing repair; do not install OT-0184's ungrounded projection.

Passing would establish one consequence-grounded improvement to selection
machinery after two failed self-corrections. It would not establish later
revision of the route matrix, recurrence across an unenumerated regime, or
eliminate the fixed three-regime probe ontology as researcher structure.
