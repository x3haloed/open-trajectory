## Frozen hypothesis

Bind the exact OT-0099 third revision and require its two Boolean-threshold
failures. A fourth fresh corrector receiving that complete receipt will replace
raw completed-floor count with the retained `two or more` predicate while
preserving composition-first priority, expansion, positive regret, rejection
filters, and stable id.

Bind before a fifth disjoint gate using new ids, values, and orders. The gate
rechecks Boolean threshold under two orders, composition over immediate gain,
expansion, positive regret, invalid/held/irreversible rejection, and stable id
under two orders. The expected generic rule is unchanged from OT-0099.

Full fifth-stage success authorizes the corrected allocator to govern the joint
implementation, independent consequence, fresh assimilation, and exact subject
promotion. Promotion retains the complete correction chain, executable
allocator, admitted capability, and actor-authored opening.

Only after operational sealing does a matched corrector receive the same third
revision with fourth-stage outcome content erased. The causal claim passes only
if that conformant control does not reproduce the complete active correction
gate. Operational promotion survives causal rejection.

Reject on any correction, hidden regression, implementation, assimilation,
identity, or liveness failure. Stop before control on operational failure. No
replacement actors or output repairs are authorized. Observer stopping does not
close a promoted subject.

Passing operationally would establish one bounded four-update chain in which
successive objective consequences improve self-authored selection machinery
until it generalizes, governs contact, and persists across fresh actors and a
turn boundary. It would not establish open-domain or indefinite recurrence,
subject ownership of admission or scheduling, or broad-world optimality.

## Result

The fourth fresh corrector cited both threshold failures, changed raw floor
count to the Boolean two-or-more predicate, retained joint selection, and passed
all eight fifth-stage regression cases. Its trace and artifacts were clean,
contained, truthful, grounded, and generic.

The newly corrected allocator then governed a separate fresh implementer. That
actor produced the realized-score joint policy, passed both public cases and all
four hidden cases at their complete oracle totals, preserved both completed
floors, and authored a substantive successor opening. Independent consequence
admitted the implementation.

The fresh assimilator retained the allocator byte-for-byte, cited all six
passed public and hidden cases, described the admitted consequence, and authored
a distinct substantive opening. The prospective assimilation validator still
rejected: it required `allocator_disposition` to equal literal `retain`, while
the actor wrote a substantive sentence beginning with retention; and it required
all cited ids to be hidden even though the complete sealed receipt exposed and
admitted both public and hidden rows.

## Learned boundary

The selection mechanism reached the intended operational behavior: four
successive fresh corrections converged on a generic hidden-valid allocator,
which selected and caused an independently admitted joint capability. The
failure is now in the final carrier interface. Retention is mechanically
observable from unchanged allocator bytes, and grounding should admit any
passed row in the complete sealed consequence rather than silently excluding
public evidence.

The next experiment should carry OT-0100's exact corrected binding,
implementation, and consequence forward without resampling. Remove
actor-authored disposition, derive retention from bytes, admit citations to
passed public and hidden rows while requiring contact with both partitions, and
test exact promotion. Only after sealing should the outcome-erased fourth
corrector run.
