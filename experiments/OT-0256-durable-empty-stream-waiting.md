# OT-0256 — durable empty-stream waiting

- **Status:** frozen
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0255 subject `d15dbd22...`
- **Fresh actors:** zero

## Frozen hypothesis

When the exact saturated subject requests environment expansion and its provider
has no unseen world, a null pulse can produce durable waiting rather than
failure, closure, or invented contact.

The first invocation must bind an explicit empty-provider observation into a
wait handle, preserve the entire operational subject, keep continuation open,
and spawn no actor. A second process invocation must reload that exact serialized
waiting subject, observe the same empty provider cursor, and leave the subject
bit-for-bit unchanged rather than appending substantive duplicate state.

## Frozen gates

Promotion requires:

- the exact OT-0255 parent and promotion receipt;
- `expand-environment` derived from a null pulse;
- a positive provider control that returns the unseen retained world and the
  live parent returning no world;
- an explicit observation receipt binding the provider catalog, seen-world
  cursor, exact source subject, and empty result;
- one installed wait handle whose resume condition is an unseen world and whose
  operation remains environment expansion;
- no actor, world fabrication, epoch, ledger, pursuit, selector, projection, or
  driver change;
- an open continuation with `awaiting-world-stream-extension` liveness;
- a separate re-observation invocation that reloads the serialized subject and
  produces the exact same subject digest with no second installed wait receipt;
- retained G10 routing and identity floors.

This experiment does not extend the provider or claim resumption. A later
experiment must make a genuinely new world available and resume this exact
waiting subject.
