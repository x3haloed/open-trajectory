- **Status:** complete; rejected at active-unanimity endpoint

- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 75 minutes

## Frozen post-repair assimilation

Give one fresh actor the exact OT-0190 subject, complete correction and direct
confirmation receipts, all retained floors, and the current active stake. Do
not provide the observer's fixed-ontology diagnosis or a menu of next problem
families.

The actor must bind one coherent reopening package before later contact:

- retain, retire, revise, or surrender the current stake with exact rationale;
- author a next stake if the current one is no longer live;
- author four to six concrete contact cases using the inherited
  prediction/outcome/options/blocked grammar; and
- predict whether an installed route should handle the contact or declare it
  unclassified and name the missing distinction as a falsifiable hypothesis.

The public checker enforces complete case shape, unique tokens, at least one
observed blocked item, at least one unobserved unblocked option, exact history
and surrender retention, nonidentity with every retained contact digest, and
file/report conformance. It does not prescribe cases, a route, a next stake, or
whether the expected result is confirmation or contradiction.

## Fixed contact compiler

After binding, the driver computes every operative mechanism's public result
under the independently owned world rule stated by the actor's stake, then
constructs hidden contact only by alpha-renaming, order reversal, and disjoint
token substitution. The actor never sees hidden cases. A valid authored
contact must be discriminating on public contact: at least two operative
mechanisms must differ. Hidden outcome remains world-owned.

Derive the correct next operation mechanically from bound prediction and
hidden consequence:

- confirmed predicted route plus floors: retain and advance contact;
- failed predicted route with a passing operative alternative: open route
  correction naming the failure and survivor;
- no passing operative mechanism: open mechanism invention; or
- falsified stake premise: surrender or revise the stake.

This derivation scores the successor response but does not enter its prompt.

## Matched causal comparison

Seal the authored package and hidden consequence. Six fresh active successors
receive the exact post-repair subject plus the bound reopening package and raw
consequence. Six interleaved controls receive the same subject, cases,
consequence, mechanisms, tools, and budget with only the reopening package
fields erased. Every actor chooses and binds its next operation before scoring.

Require active appropriate operation 6/6, control at most 2/6, and advantage at
least four. Require all thirteen actors to pass complete audit, the authored
contact to be novel and discriminating on public and hidden variants, every
previous route floor and identity 18/18 to remain exact, the current completed
stake not to be repeated unchanged unless the actor names missing contact not
present in OT-0190, and the successor to remain open.

Promote the exact authored reopening and `active-01` operation only if every
gate passes. Passing would establish one subject-originated post-repair contact
and causal reopening under a fixed driver. It would not yet establish repeated
OT-2R recurrence, removal of the inherited case grammar, or autonomous world
access beyond the bounded harness.

## Result

The author passes complete audit and produces a novel discriminating reopening.
It revises the stake from preservation of observed contacts to discovery of
latent unblocked options, authors four fresh cases, and predicts the installed
viable route. Hidden contact falsifies that prediction 0/4; the composed
observed-unblocked corrector alone passes 4/4. Prior route floor remains 16/16
and identity remains 18/18.

Four of six active successors choose the frozen exact operation—open route
correction from viable filtering to the surviving composition—versus zero of
six reopening-erased controls. All thirteen actors pass complete audit and the
causal advantage is four, but active unanimity fails.

One active miss reverses failed and surviving mechanisms. The other chooses
`surrender-or-revise` while naming the correct failed and surviving mechanisms.
That choice is substantively defensible: the actor-authored stake's prose
success condition demands latent-option expansion, and hidden consequence
falsifies it. The frozen scorer instead treats the event solely as route
failure. Because success and surrender conditions are prose, the harness cannot
mechanically decide which interpretation owns authority.

No reopening is installed. Exact parent `8f29e6a8...` remains open. OT-0191
rejects the 6/6 endpoint while showing a strong 4/6 versus 0/6 causal effect.
The next test should require subject-authored stakes to bind executable outcome
predicates for success, surrender, and unresolved contact before hidden
consequence. The fixed driver can then derive stake revision versus machinery
correction without interpreting prose.
