# OT-0133 — transferable contact program

- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** replace one-shot contact cases with an actor-authored contact
  program that transfers to sealed context pairs and demand patterns
- **Parent:** exact OT-0132 open subject
- **Actor budget:** one fresh contact-program author and one fresh consequence
  router; no replacement
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

The OT-0132 subject's new opening asks whether the context-local reserve
boundary transfers to materially different context pairs and demand patterns.
A fresh actor can turn that stake into a compact metamorphic contact program,
check it on visible bases, bind it before hidden consequence, and obtain a
decisive contact when the world applies the same program to unseen bases. The
unchanged route-only carrier can then retain the result in another exact open
subject.

## Contact program

The actor receives a fresh complete capacity repository, two single-context
public bases, `check_program.py`, the exact active subject position, and a
placeholder `contact-program.json`. There is no hidden base, contact catalog,
preferred function, or reference output in the workspace. The actor may edit
only the program.

The program chooses one real policy function and defines a foreign-context
prefix, positive high and low offsets, and a neutral midpoint control. For each
base the public checker derives three mixed-context variants:

1. insert a foreign demand above the local maximum;
2. insert a foreign demand below the local minimum; and
3. insert a foreign midpoint demand.

The declared invariant is that unrelated-context insertion should not change a
context-local result. Public conformance requires both high and low variants to
distinguish the installed result while the midpoint remains equal on every
visible base. The exact conformant program then binds.

## Independent consequence

After binding, apply the same program to three sealed bases with new context
names, event counts, ranges, and insertion positions. The world computes both
installed and context-local reference output. Decisive consequence requires
all six adversarial variants to distinguish and all three controls to confirm.
No hidden value, case, or output may shape the bound program.

Only after decisive consequence authorize one fresh route actor. It must select
`extend`, cite all nine exact generated cases, retain the selector, and author
one new remaining uncertainty. Fixed compiler `ot-0130-v1` derives the cycle-five
action and opening. Reject on the first invalid trace, program, public check,
hidden receipt, route, compiler check, or promotion; authorize no replacement.

Passing would establish one bounded generative contact artifact that transfers
the subject's carried stake to unseen world instances and survives another
actor ending. It would not establish arbitrary contact languages, autonomous
world generation, frequency, indefinite operation, or subject-authored changes
to the carrier itself.
