# OT-0142 — consequence-corrected constitutional selection

- **Status:** completed; promoted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** make choice among materially different self-change rules
  consequence-addressable, correct a myopic inherited selector, and retain the
  selected adaptive rule through later reuse
- **Parent:** exact OT-0141 open subject pointing to q32
- **Actor budget:** two fresh portfolio authors, one fresh selector corrector,
  and one fresh reuse verifier; no replacement
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 120 minutes

## Seed selector and amendment language

Prospectively install a content-addressed constitutional-amendment selector in
the exact OT-0141 subject. Its version-one semantics rank public validity,
smaller derived mutation surface, then stable id. This is disclosed
researcher-designed seed machinery.

Publish one generic amendment language with two representation families, not
two prewritten candidates:

- `absolute`: one symmetric fixed offset, constrained to the least value that
  passes the current public quantum; and
- `quantum-relative`: one symmetric positive factor, constrained to the least
  factor that passes the current public quantum.

A fresh portfolio actor must author exactly one candidate in each family,
including ids, rationale, and surrender condition, and pass an executable
checker on two public bases. Candidate features are mechanically derived:
absolute has mutation surface one and is not scale-invariant; quantum-relative
has mutation surface two and is scale-invariant. Actors cannot author their own
features or scores.

## First selection and retained contradiction

At the subject's q32 opening, bind the first portfolio and apply the inherited
selector before future consequence. The frozen prediction is that both
candidates pass q32, while version one selects the cheaper absolute candidate.

After binding, evaluate both candidates on three sealed q64 bases with
joint-high, joint-low, joint-extremes, and midpoint-control variants. Absolute
offset 32 is predicted to distinguish only three of nine adversarial cases;
quantum-relative factor one is predicted to distinguish all nine, with all
controls invariant. Preserve the complete comparative receipt and install no
candidate. Instead seal the exact subject with the failed constitutional
selection as pending correction.

## Consequence-corrected selector

One fresh corrector receives exact subject position, version-one selector, the
bound portfolio, complete comparative receipt, and an executable retrospective
checker. It may edit only `selector-semantics.json`, which contains only an
ordered permutation of `public_pass`, `scale_invariant`,
`lower_mutation_surface`, and `stable_id`. The actor authors selector semantics
once; parent identity, cause, version, and content digest are compiled
mechanically.

Bind the correction only if it selects the hidden-passing candidate from the
first portfolio while the unchanged selector still selects the failed one.
Install the corrected selector before another portfolio exists.

## Held-out portfolio, matched control, and reuse

Give a second fresh actor the same language at q32 with new public bases and no
access to the first actor's candidate bytes. Bind its conformant portfolio.
Before sealed future consequence, bind selections from both corrected and exact
unchanged selectors under identical information and resources.

On three new sealed q64 bases, corrected selection must choose a passing
quantum-relative candidate while unchanged selection chooses an absolute
candidate that fails. Promote the active branch only after q32 public validity,
q64 hidden transfer, contained actor trace, and exact selector retention pass.
Install the selected strategy as a content-addressed adaptive contact capability
and amend the constitution mechanically to name quantum-relative offsets as the
active strategy. The subject then opens q128.

One final fresh actor must reuse the exact retained strategy and constitution
on three new q128 bases without amendment. Require nine distinctions and three
controls. Seal the operational successor before applying the second portfolio's
absolute strategy as a q128 observational control.

The observer stops after the fresh reuse actor. The exact subject must remain
open and point beyond observation to q256. Erasing the corrected selector from
the post-seal subject must reproduce the unchanged branch's absolute choice on
the held-out portfolio.

Stop at the first identity, schema, public checker, trace, binding, hidden
world, selector correction, matched comparison, installation, reuse, or opening
failure. Never resample an actor.

Passing would establish one bounded case where consequence improves machinery
that selects among different rules for future self-change, and the selected
scale-relative rule widens later contact without another constitutional repair.
It would not establish open-ended amendment languages, subject-authored seed
priorities, autonomous world generation, cross-domain transfer, or indefinite
operation.

## Result

All four fresh actors and contained workspaces passed their exact mutation and
trace audits. Both portfolio actors independently authored one least-passing
absolute amendment and one least-passing quantum-relative amendment inside the
published language.

On the first portfolio, both candidates passed public q32. The inherited
selector preferred the smaller mutation surface and selected absolute offset
32. Sealed q64 consequence produced only three of nine adversarial distinctions
for that choice while the unselected quantum-relative factor-one candidate
produced nine of nine; all controls remained invariant. The exact comparative
failure entered the subject.

One fresh corrector changed only selector priority semantics, moving derived
`scale_invariant` ahead of `lower_mutation_surface`. Parent identity, cause,
version, and content digest were compiled mechanically. Retrospectively the
corrected selector chose the passing first candidate while its unchanged parent
still chose the failed one.

On the second independently authored portfolio, corrected and unchanged
selectors were bound under identical information before sealed q64 consequence.
The corrected selector chose quantum-relative factor one and produced nine of
nine distinctions plus three confirmations. The exact unchanged selector chose
absolute offset 32 and again produced only three of nine plus three controls.

The active branch installed the corrected selector, quantum-relative strategy,
and adaptive constitution. A fourth fresh actor preserved their exact bytes and
reused the strategy at q128, producing nine of nine distinctions and three
controls without another amendment. Only after sealing that operational
successor, the held-out absolute strategy was applied at q128 and produced zero
of nine distinctions while controls remained invariant.

Exact sounding open subject
`a66da59648c69b0b963be36da25e37156ad55f12598d749964c02e5bfb8dce4d`
points beyond observation to q256. Erasing the corrected selector reproduces
the unchanged branch's absolute choice on the held-out portfolio.

This is a bounded matched case where consequence improves machinery selecting
among materially different rules for future self-change, and the chosen rule
expands later contact without another constitutional repair. The amendment
language, initial priority, world family, and admission remain
researcher-designed; cross-domain transfer and subject-authored priorities are
not established.
