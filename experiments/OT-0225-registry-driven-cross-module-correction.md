# OT-0225 — registry-driven cross-module correction

- **Status:** frozen; not yet run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** correction of the exact OT-0224 subject-selected surface
- **Parent:** exact OT-0224 open subject `ade85ab1...`
- **Actor budget:** one fresh corrector; no replacement
- **Observer budget:** one independent six-case correction world plus unchanged control

## Frozen hypothesis

The fixed subject machinery can derive the unresolved target and its
installed/reference files from the exact subject, expose that contradiction to
one fresh corrector, and admit a target-local correction in a module that no
prior correction driver supported. Neither the harness nor prompt may hard-code
the selected target or a planning-specific repair.

This tests registry-driven correction after subject-selected widening. It does
not yet test post-correction reopening or another semantic expansion.

## Contract and order

The harness derives the last pending package, unresolved receipt, target symbol,
target path, and reference path from the exact parent. The actor receives the
complete six-target repository, exact bound contact and receipt, a baseline copy
of the selected source, and a generated correction contract. It may revise only
the selected source file and `correction-decision.json`, or surrender without a
source change. Full ordinary tools remain available.

Freeze this order before live output:

1. verify exact parent and OT-0224 promotion, unresolved G7 dispatch, registry
   resolution, target-local checker, source mutation envelope, follow-up world,
   unchanged control, identity, route, reconstruction, and privacy;
2. open one fresh corrector in one fresh workspace;
3. bind and audit its disposition before follow-up consequence;
4. run six independent follow-up cases against both its revision and the exact
   unchanged source; and
5. seal the correction, world receipt, updated environment, G7 ledger, and next
   `assimilate` phase only when the resulting subject conforms.

Do not resample the actor, replace the target, or substitute a known fixture
repair after output.

## Falsifier, controls, and promotion

The cheapest falsifier is failure to produce a clean target-local revision that
passes the original four public cases. The unchanged control is frozen at 2/6
on distinct follow-up inputs; the conformance fixture reaches 6/6 while changing
only the selected function. The same actor checker must admit the fixture and
reject the untouched template.

Promote only if:

- the harness contains no literal selected target and resolves it from the
  exact pending subject state;
- one fresh actor and workspace audit cleanly;
- the actor revises exactly the registry-selected target function;
- the original public package moves or remains at 4/4;
- follow-up consequence is valid and reaches 6/6 while unchanged stays 2/6;
- the exact pending contradiction is resolved by bound receipts;
- the retained environment installs the admitted patched source;
- G7 marks the target `verified-local` and the driver dispatches `assimilate`;
- the exact subject remains open and conforms; and
- route 16/16, identity 18/18, reconstruction, and privacy pass.

Passing establishes generalized correction on the first subject-selected new
module. It does not establish reusable correction across arbitrary ABIs,
post-correction reopening, autonomous environment construction, or open-ended
domain discovery.
