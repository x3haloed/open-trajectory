# OT-0149 — blind stake world selection

- **Status:** frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** let the continuing subject originate a developmental priority
  before world opportunities exist, then make that priority causally select and
  complete the next useful world contact
- **Parent:** exact OT-0148 open subject
- **Actor budget:** one fresh blind stake author, one fresh selected-world
  implementer, and one fresh consequence assimilator; no replacement
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 100 minutes

## Priority before opportunity

Give the first actor the exact active subject position and a small neutral
developmental-property vocabulary: continuity under reset, expansion of viable
options, and correction from observed error. Do not expose world surfaces,
surface ids, implementations, public cases, hidden cases, or presentation order.

The actor may edit only `next-stake.json`. It must choose one property and author
its own question, rationale, success condition, and surrender condition. Bind
the exact stake and actor patch before constructing any world catalog.

## Compiled world contact

Only after stake binding, materialize three executable JSON-policy worlds, one
per property. Fixed generic machinery maps the bound property to its world; the
researcher does not choose among them after seeing the stake. Record the full
catalog identity and route binding.

Give a second fresh actor only the selected world, public cases, bound stake,
and editable `policy.json` plus `contact-action.json`. It must pass the public
checker with an exactly scoped mutation. Bind its policy before hidden cases.
Independent hidden consequence must pass and add a context-specific capability
without regressing the exact parent.

Give a third fresh actor the exact stake, route, policy, and full consequence.
It may edit only `assimilation.json`. A passed world requires retirement of the
fulfilled stake and a valid next stake choosing a different developmental
property. The next opening is compiled from that actor-authored question.

Seal the successor before two deterministic controls: erasing the bound property
must leave no routable world, while replacing it with a different valid property
must select a different world under the same catalog.

Require all earlier capability and selector-program history, sounding liveness,
and exact actor/tool audits. Stop at the first identity, stake, route, public
contact, hidden consequence, assimilation, control, or opening failure. Never
resample an actor.

Passing would establish one bounded subject-originated priority causing a useful
next-world transition across fresh actors without experiment-specific world
selection after the priority is known. The property vocabulary, compiler, and
world catalog remain researcher-designed; recurrence from the actor-authored
next stake would remain untested.
