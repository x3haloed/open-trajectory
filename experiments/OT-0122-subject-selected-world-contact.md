# OT-0122 — subject-selected sealed world contact

- **Status:** executed; rejected
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** reduce researcher causation over which world surface opens next
- **Claim scopes sought:** bounded `operational-transition` and bounded
  `causal-observation`; no model-specific generative-reproduction claim
- **Parent:** exact OT-0121 open subject with corrected selector
- **Actor budget:** one fresh post-contact router/assimilator
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

The exact OT-0121 subject can use its installed, consequence-corrected selector
to determine which member of a prospectively sealed heterogeneous contact
envelope becomes the next actual world contact. The selected branch's objective
consequence can then determine whether a fresh successor should use, correct,
extend, or surrender the inherited trajectory, without the observer selecting
that branch after seeing the choice.

The immediate pre-correction selector will choose a materially different,
inferior branch from the same envelope. This is the matched causal control for
the effect of the retained OT-0121 correction on the next world surface.

## Sealed contact envelope

Freeze four contacts that all address the active resilience-boundary opening
through different surfaces: a carry-heavy probe, an isolated coordination
boundary, an isolated recovery boundary, and a joint boundary. Every contact
uses the selector's public ABI and declares world validity, reversibility,
predicted expansion, coordination load, recovery volatility, resilience margin,
carry cost, and public regret. Stable ids are only final tie breakers.

Before actor authorization, conformance must establish:

1. the exact corrected and pre-correction selectors load without mutation;
2. selection is invariant under every envelope permutation and a complete id
   renaming;
3. the corrected selector chooses the joint boundary;
4. the pre-correction selector chooses the carry-heavy probe; and
5. the sealed world oracle ranks the joint boundary highest.

The driver binds both selections before revealing any branch consequence. It
then opens only the corrected selector's branch. Unselected branch outcomes may
be scored by the evaluator but are never shown to the successor actor.

## Consequence routing

The sealed world classifies the opened consequence by observable rules:

- `use`: the contact supports the machinery but leaves the current pursuit
  unresolved;
- `correct`: realized ranking contradicts the installed selector;
- `extend`: the contact supports the machinery, settles the current pursuit,
  and exposes grounded remaining uncertainty;
- `surrender`: the pursuit's declared surrender condition is met and no
  supported successor remains.

The fresh router receives the exact subject position, bound selected contact,
only the selected branch consequence, the route semantics, and the immutable
selector. It may edit exactly `route-assimilation.json`,
`successor-opening.json`, and `continuation-action.json`. It must cite every
settled selected-branch case, match the mechanically derived route, preserve
the selector bytes, and bind a valid action and opening before promotion.

## Controls, scoring, and promotion

The target passes only if all of the following hold:

1. pre-actor conformance and both pre-consequence selection bindings pass;
2. corrected and pre-correction selectors choose the prospectively frozen
   different branches stated above;
3. the corrected choice equals the sealed world oracle;
4. the fresh actor's trace is clean, exact, and truthful;
5. its route equals the world's mechanically derived route and cites the exact
   selected-branch case set;
6. the selector is retained byte-for-byte; and
7. the promoted exact child is sounding and preserves an open continuation
   when the route is `use`, `correct`, or `extend`, or a closed continuation
   when it is `surrender`.

The cheapest falsifiers are selection instability, no choice difference from
the pre-correction control, a non-oracle corrected choice, route disagreement,
ungrounded citations, tool-trace rejection, selector mutation, or failure to
reopen the exact promoted subject. Stop at the first unavailable causal link;
do not authorize replacement actors.

## Researcher-causation inventory

- **Researcher-owned:** the finite envelope, hidden branch fixtures, route
  semantics, evaluator, admission, isolation, and observation stopping.
- **Subject-owned:** the installed selector, the actual branch choice, the
  consequence disposition, the next action, and the successor opening.
- **Removed here:** researcher selection of one world regime after inspecting
  the subject's state or selector.

Passing would establish one bounded subject-caused world-surface choice whose
consequence governs a fresh successor transition. It would not establish
subject authorship of the envelope, repeated autonomous scheduling, open-domain
world discovery, or indefinite continuation.

## Result

The bounded causal-selection portion passed. The exact corrected selector chose
`joint-boundary` invariantly; the immediate pre-correction control chose
`carry-heavy-boundary`; and the sealed world oracle ranked `joint-boundary`
highest. The one fresh router had a clean, exact, truthful trace, retained the
selector bytes, cited all four selected-branch cases, and correctly classified
the consequence as `extend`.

The operational transition is nevertheless rejected after evaluation-regime
audit. Although the actor grounded a new resource-scarcity uncertainty in its
assimilation, action rationale, expected information, and continuation after
contact, its authoritative `successor-opening.next_opening` exactly repeated
the parent's opening and did not name resource scarcity. The frozen harness
mistakenly accepted non-surrender as sufficient evidence of extension. Preserve
the raw aggregate's apparent promotion as evidence of that gate defect; no
subject checkpoint is published. The next test must retain the exact selection
and world receipt while requiring the authoritative next opening to encode the
newly grounded uncertainty.
