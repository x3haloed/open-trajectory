## Why the evaluator must change

creates valid unseen contact while the waiting subject remains exact. It cannot
distinguish three materially different achievements:

1. valid operational novelty;
2. a subject-carried condition causally changing which move occurs; and
3. a falsifiable subject-originated stake causally shaping contact and then
   remaining answerable to independent consequence.

Repeated same-language renewal has saturated the first signal. Treating it as a
proxy for all three would make arbitrary novelty look self-directed.
