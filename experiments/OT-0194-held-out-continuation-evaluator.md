# OT-0194 — held-out continuation evaluator

- **Status:** design frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** prospectively validate the corrected definition of useful pursuit
  continuation on a fresh cohort, then install the direct transition only if it
  causally carries a stake into real next contact
- **Parent:** exact OT-0190 open successor plus retained OT-0191–OT-0193
  candidate evidence
- **Actor budget:** six active continuation authors and six matched controls
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Evaluation checkpoint

OT-0193's frozen evaluator `E0193` counted a continuation only when it preserved
the new stake, authored novel targeted public/hidden-discriminating contact,
and predicted the mechanism that survived the previous contact. Raw evidence
showed the final clause was blind to the desired invariant: a new falsifiable
hypothesis need not reuse the prior survivor.

Freeze challenger `E0194` before this held-out cohort. It requires:

- exact inheritance of the transitioned active stake;
- a valid and distinct 4–6 case contact;
- observed blocked evidence plus an unobserved unblocked option in every case;
- candidate discrimination on public and sealed hidden transformations; and
- a valid installed-mechanism prediction or explicit unclassified distinction.

The hidden world may confirm or kill the hypothesis. Either result preserves
continuation value. `E0193` is also computed on the same outputs for comparison
but has no promotion authority.

## Corrected direct transition

Reconstruct OT-0192's exact predicate binding and evaluated operation. Seal a
candidate child carrying the predicates, evaluation receipt, direct transition,
active OT-0191 stake, and open continuation. Do not create a global
`active_developmental_mechanism`: the prior survivor belongs to the completed
encounter, not every future hypothesis.

Active actors receive that child and the evaluated receipt. Interleaved
controls receive exact parent and an erased receipt. Raw reopening, consequence,
predicates, and candidate mechanisms are identical across branches. Actors
author next contact; they never vote on the settled operation.

## Frozen promotion gate

Require `E0194` active 6/6, control at most 2/6, advantage at least four, all
twelve complete audits, deterministic corrected transition, route 16/16,
identity 18/18, exact retained history, and an open successor. Also record
`E0193` counts, but do not require it to pass.

On passage, advance the evaluation epoch, install the corrected direct subject,
and retain active-01 reopening plus sealed consequence. On failure, preserve
exact parent and both evaluators' scores.

Passing establishes one direct subject-authored pursuit transition that causes
fresh actors to carry its stake into falsifiable later contact. The following
experiment must resolve that retained consequence through a second update;
OT-0194 alone does not establish recurrence.
