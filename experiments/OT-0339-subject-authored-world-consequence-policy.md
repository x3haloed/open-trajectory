## Evaluation checkpoint

The first world-level consequence signal observed only eligible-target count
and baseline matches. The exact requested pair tied at two viable-looking 2/6
surfaces each; the prior actor then selected Morrowmere for the missing third
receipt. Observer inspection suggests Morrowmere may expose three such surfaces,
but promoting “more failures is better” after seeing that shape would be a
gameable post-hoc evaluator change.

Before the third receipt is compiled, a fresh successor will author a bounded
lexicographic policy over three normalized signals: viable non-regressive
contact count, mean current match rate, and minimum current match rate. Every
candidate must preserve the inherited floor and every counted contact must pass
the complete existing offer, public-execution, independent-outcome, and open-
correction fixture. The policy chooses metric order and higher/lower direction;
it cannot name a world or inspect sealed material. This is a scoped challenger
for world consequence, complementary to E13 rather than a replacement for its
all prior routing and cumulative-floor anchors.

## Frozen causal comparison

The active author receives the exact developmental stake, pursuit, world-
seeking question, OT-0338 extension, two tied outcome receipts, and public
three-world catalog. The matched actor receives identical mechanics, receipts,
catalog, tools, and task, but the subject-priority projection is erased. Neither
receives Morrowmere's targets, cases, references, viability result, evaluator
fixtures, sibling output, or expected policy.

Both actors write only one policy file under broad-tool G11 audit. The policy
must be syntactically valid, require floor preservation and all counted contacts
to be viable, provide a complete metric permutation and direction map, and keep
ties open. Bind the active policy before deriving the third receipt. Then apply
the same viability evaluator to all three retained worlds and run the bound
policy. Policy erasure has no selection authority. Altered packages, failed
contact fixtures, floor regression, missing binding, and fabricated outcome
authority reject.

The active policy is additionally evaluated on a hidden anchor bundle whose
world ids, order, and viable counts differ from the live catalog. Match rates
are held equal so the anchor isolates expansion: the policy must prefer the
floor-preserving world with more fully viable contacts in every case and must
reject a higher-count regressive decoy. This anchor predates the live third
receipt and is not exposed to either actor.

## Promotion and interpretation

Operational promotion requires a clean active actor, pre-consequence binding,
full passage of the hidden expansion anchor, three valid world receipts under
one viability regime, deterministic policy application, exact stake retention,
and an open successor. If the policy finds
a unique better world different from the inherited descriptor selector, the
child may route consequence-grounded stake revision; if it ties, it must remain
open without revision. The priority-erased comparison determines only the
stronger causal claim that inherited subject priorities changed the policy or
downstream selection; it cannot veto an otherwise valid operational child.

This experiment does not assume Morrowmere wins, does not treat actor rationale
as outcome evidence, and does not establish global world fitness. It asks
whether the subject can author what its next consequence should mean before
that consequence arrives.

## Result

Before the third consequence was compiled, the active actor authored a valid
policy that lexicographically maximizes viable non-regressive contact count,
then worst and mean match rate. It passed the hidden expansion bundle 5/5. All
three retained worlds then passed the complete viability and inherited-floor
fixtures. Morrow Quay and Harbor each exposed two viable 2/6 contacts;
Morrowmere exposed three. The bound policy selected Morrowmere, disagreeing
with the inherited descriptor selector's Harbor choice and routing an open,
consequence-grounded stake-revision decision. Exact operational child
`32ac5ab0...` is valid.

The priority-erased actor independently authored the same substantive metric
order and directions, also passed 5/5, and made the same Morrowmere selection.
The raw aggregate's `priority_causal_claim_supported: true` is therefore wrong:
its scorer counted differing policy ids/rationales as causal even though every
operative field and downstream decision matched. OT-0340 freezes an exact
interpretation correction. The raw aggregate `521ac8ca...` is retained; its
operational result stands, while its priority-causal flag has no authority.
