# OT-0082 — subject-owned world routing

- **Status:** unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** OT-2R researcher-causation reduction
- **Claim scopes sought:** `operational-transition`, `causal-observation`
- **Parent:** exact OT-0081 open subject
- **Actor budget:** at most three routing actors, three implementation actors,
  and one post-seal control implementation actor
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Hypothesis

After its promoted challenge machinery is independently confirmed as saturated,
the exact OT-0081 subject can use its inherited consequence-compiled
developmental selector to choose among materially different admissible tool
world contacts, bind that choice before outcome, produce a world-valid
intervention through a fresh implementation actor, and reopen from the admitted
consequence without a researcher choosing the surface.

The narrower causal prediction is that applying the inherited selector to one
bound environmental assessment chooses a new joint capability, while a
selector-erased stable-order replay of the same assessment chooses a held
primitive repetition and fails developmental admission.

## Cheapest falsifier

Reject operational routing if the fixed driver cannot derive saturation,
routing, implementation, admission, denial retry, and reopening from the exact
subject plus sealed receipts; if no admitted contact is produced within three
routes; if a chosen intervention alters undeclared authority; or if the exact
successor is not sounding and open.

Reject the bounded selector claim if the full and selector-erased routes receive
different environmental information; if the full selector does not choose an
admitted surface; if the erased route does not choose a materially worse
surface under the same hidden world and admission rules; or if removing the
selector's actionable role does not remove the difference.

An operationally admitted full successor is sealed before the control. A failed
control narrows the selector claim and cannot veto that successor.

## Starting subject and saturation

verifies byte identity, internal seal, sounding runtime, challenge machinery

It first executes the inherited version-3 challenge generator against the
inherited executable on frozen fresh cases `(3109, 8)`, `(3203, 10)`, and
`(3301, 12)`. All must be deterministic, prefix-consistent, seed-diverse, and

Confirmation compiles a saturation receipt and mechanically exposes the

`tool_world_scheduler.open = inspect-and-select-environmental-intervention`.

## Frozen tool world portfolio

The router receives read-only copies of all three surfaces, their public tests,
and their interface descriptions. The identifiers and presentation order are
fixed as follows:

1. `surface-17` — categorical cadence continuation. Implement a missing
   next-tone operation from repeating event categories. It can pass independent
   hidden cases, but repeats the subject's already held score-listening
   primitive and is classified `world-valid-held-primitive-repetition`.
2. `surface-42` — route/score bridge. Compose a provided ordered-route counter
   and a provided score-event listener behind one missing joint operation.
   Hidden graph and event cases require both completed floors and classify a
   passing patch `world-valid-novel-composition`.
3. `surface-68` — mutable registry acceleration. Improve batch resolution while
   preserving registration after warm-cache use. Hidden interleavings detect
   stale caches or destroyed extension hooks. A passing, extension-preserving
   patch is a valid novel primitive; a regression is
   `irreversible-closure` and is rejected.

Each surface is a small isolated Python package. Public tests and source are
actor-visible. Hidden tests, expected outputs, evaluation code, other actor
workspaces, repository state, and acceptance remain outside every actor
workspace. Fixture bytes and conformance results are sealed before actor
authorization.

## Bound routing operation

A fresh routing actor may inspect every surface with ordinary broad tools, but
may edit exactly `route.json`. It must assess every surface exactly once using:

- `axis_chord` in `[0, 4]`: composition of independently completed floors;
- `reversibility` in `[0, 4]`;
- `immediate_gain` in `[0, 4]`;
- `axis_lumen` in `[0, 4]`: coherent future affordance;
- the booleans `collision`, `held_repeat`, `irreversible_closure`, and
  `world_invalid`; and
- a bounded rationale and implementation opening.

The actor does not name the selected surface. After audit and binding, the
driver applies the exact inherited selector policy:

1. reject an assessment with any inherited reject flag;
2. require `axis_chord >= developmental_selector.threshold`;
3. rank descending by `axis_chord`, `reversibility`, `immediate_gain`, then
   `axis_lumen`; and
4. break ties by `surface_id`.

This makes surface choice an executable consequence of inherited subject state
and actor-observed environment rather than an in-flight researcher decision.

## Contact, admission, and retry

The chosen surface alone is copied into a fresh implementation workspace with
the bound route and exact subject projection. The actor may edit only the
surface's declared implementation file. Its patch is bound before hidden tests.

- A contaminated or world-invalid encounter is quarantined. The observer stops
  and preserves the exact last valid open subject.
- A world-valid held repetition is denied developmental admission. The denial
  joins the exact subject, and the fixed scheduler may open a fresh router on
  the remaining surfaces.
- A world-valid novel primitive or composition is admitted only if every public
  and hidden test passes with no inherited-floor regression. The exact patch,
  world receipt, routing receipt, and capability entry join the subject.

At most three routes may be attempted. Surface identifiers already denied by
world-valid consequence are unavailable on retry. The researcher may not pick
a retry, repair a patch, change ordering, alter classifications, or amend tests
after actor output.

Promotion reopens at `inspect-and-select-environmental-intervention`, retains
the prior challenge opening as a reachable operation, and increments the
subject's tool-world capability ledger by exactly one.

## Post-seal selector ablation

After a valid full successor is sealed, replay the same bound three-surface
assessment through a selector-erased operation that preserves every assessment
byte and resource but ignores `positive_gradient`, `saturated_areas`,
`held_repeat`, and the learned rank order. It rejects only `world_invalid` and
selects the lowest stable identifier. A fresh control implementation actor then
receives that selected surface under the same tools, mutation boundary, public
tests, hidden tests, and implementation budget.

The control is observational. It cannot mutate, delay, or veto the full
successor. The claimed selector effect passes only if the full route yields an
admitted capability and the erased route yields a world-invalid intervention or
a world-valid intervention denied by the frozen developmental gate.

## Researcher-causation inventory

- **Researcher-owned:** the bounded portfolio, public and hidden fixtures,
  isolation, actor budgets, world execution, outcome receipts, and the frozen
  admission envelope.
- **Subject-owned:** inherited floors, saturation history, developmental
  selector and threshold, reject flags and ranking, interpretation by fresh

  and next opening.
- **Ablated here:** experiment-specific researcher selection of the next world
  surface, plus the actionable role of the inherited selector in that choice.

The catalog is still external and bounded. Passing OT-0082 would not establish
open-world search, cross-repository transfer, generative frequency, or that the
subject can redesign its own admission authority.

## Observation and subject stopping

The observer stops after one admitted full successor and its control, three
exhausted routes, material contamination, or 60 minutes. Only an independently
supported admitted subject state may close the subject. Every other endpoint
retains the exact last valid state as open or quarantined from its valid parent.
