# OT-0319 — Cumulative stake integration

- **Status:** completed — rejected
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0318 subject `21e90d47...`
- **Claim:** jointly replayable retained consequences enable one cumulative
  selector correction instead of alternating regime-specific repairs
- **Actors:** full-history reviser first, earlier-outcome-erased reviser second;
  one draw each, no retry

## Prediction error and revised hypothesis

OT-0318's promoted stake scores 5/5 in the reversed regime and 0/5 in the
earlier regime; its predecessor has the complementary scores. The first OT-0319
design assumed no bounded global item-weight vector could preserve both and
therefore proposed a catalog-level program language. Candidate-free exhaustive
conformance falsified that premise before freeze: a legal bounded vector can
fit all six retained contacts. Machinery-language expansion is not yet earned.

The narrower live hypothesis is that the missing input was joint access to the
authenticated alternatives and their consequences. A fresh actor receives the
exact current subject plus a named projection of three OT-0314 contacts whose
materiality was reconstructed by OT-0315 and three OT-0318 contacts. An updated
training-only workbench evaluates any actor-proposed legal stake over every
authoritative row that is present. If this permits one successor to preserve
both distinctions prospectively, the lineage has accumulated rather than
merely flipped.

## Cheapest falsifier

The cheapest falsifier is one clean full-history actor that cannot produce a
legal changed stake scoring 6/6 on revealed contacts and 10/10 on a disjoint
mixed private trajectory, while exact current and prior stakes each score only
5/10. That ends the claim for this action surface without adding a new carrier.

## Frozen interface and authority

Only `stake-revision.json` is mutable under the already public OT-0314 contract.
`evaluate_revision.py` reports per-row availability and replay fitness for the
current candidate; it exposes no private seed, held-out catalog, target vector,
search result, or preferred edit. `check_revision.py` is the corrected public
checker. Each actor has ordinary G10 tools in a fresh isolated workspace, must
successfully run both tools, and must inspect its exact diff.

The cumulative contact projection binds the exact current subject, OT-0315 and
OT-0318 aggregate receipts, and all six selection/outcome receipts. The actor,
candidate code, and substrate have no world, score, evidence, or admission
authority. The observer reconstructs retained artifacts, generates the private
trajectory after freeze, scores it, audits effects, and seals a valid successor
before the control.

## Frozen worlds, branches, and order

The revealed full-history branch contains the exact three earlier contacts and
three reversed contacts. The earlier-outcome-erased branch has byte-identical
catalogs, selections, tools, current subject, and resource budget; only the
three earlier independently owned outcome bodies are replaced by explicit
same-shaped erasure receipts. Its three current outcomes remain executable.

After candidate-free conformance, implementation, and this record are committed
and pushed, generate one private seed. Derive five earlier-regime and five
reversed-regime held-out catalogs using the already frozen world generators.
Candidate runs first and is sealed before the erased control. One draw per
branch; no retry, repair, reseed, or endpoint selection. The
observer budget is two actor calls and twenty wall-clock minutes; exhaustion
ends this observation, not the open subject.

## Frozen score and promotion gates

Before actors, exhaustive search over the complete bounded global coefficient
surface must demonstrate at least one 6/6 training witness. The exact current
and prior stakes must each score 3/6 revealed and 5/10 on fixture and private
mixed trajectories. A fixed representative legal witness must score 6/6 and
10/10 only as a candidate-free reachability fixture; it is never placed in an
actor workspace.

Operational promotion requires a clean changed stake, actor-executed checker
and workbench, 6/6 independently replayed training fitness, and private 10/10
versus both exact baselines at 5/10. The child must retain exact ancestry, the
cumulative projection, the actor-authored stake, its replay receipt, private
score, open continuation, and no world/scoring/admission authority.

The stronger causal label additionally requires a clean earlier-erased actor
whose workbench sees exactly three available current outcomes and does not
produce a changed 10/10 stake. A surprising 10/10 control narrows the causal
claim but cannot retroactively erase an already sealed operational successor.
Wrong receipt binding, hidden-data access, immutable mutation, missing tool
execution, evaluator disagreement, G10 contamination, malformed output, tie,
or any baseline outside 5/10 rejects the relevant claim.

## Limits and next dependency

A pass would establish one bounded cumulative selection improvement caused by
jointly actionable retained consequence. It would not establish a new machinery
language, reliability, cross-domain transfer, autonomous world invention, or
removal of the researcher who chose this discrepancy and comparison. The next
experiment should reduce that remaining causal role rather than repeat these
catalogs.

## Result

The full-history actor was clean, ran both required tools, and correctly
observed that all six outcomes were available while the exact current stake
passed only 3/6. It nevertheless retained the stake unchanged. Its decisive
reason was a false claim that every legal fixed linear weighting must choose the
same side in both regimes. Candidate-free exhaustive conformance had already
falsified that claim with a legal 6/6 witness, and that witness scores 10/10 on
the frozen mixed family. The live actor did not test any successor proposal.

The matched earlier-outcome-erased actor was also clean. It observed three
available current outcomes, passed all three with the incumbent, and retained
unchanged. Both exact current and prior baselines scored the frozen private
trajectory at 5/10. No candidate reached training or private scoring, no
operational child was produced, and exact parent `21e90d47...` remains open.
Rejected aggregate receipt is `03492917...`.

Both actors first attempted unavailable `rg`, disclosed the failure, and used
available shell tools successfully. This did not change actor information,
authority, scoring, branch comparability, or the result.

OT-0319 rejects the claim that jointly visible consequence plus a
candidate-only evaluator is sufficient for cumulative correction. The observed
missing edge is proposal search or counterexample generation: the actor could
evaluate a candidate but incorrectly declared the reachable legal surface
exhausted without exploring it.
