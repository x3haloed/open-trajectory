# OT-0176 — consequence-authored authority projection

- **Status:** complete; rejected before selector comparison
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** test whether explicitly inherited mechanism authority and floor
  roles repair the prose-only selector failure through held-out contact
- **Parent:** exact OT-0172 surrender successor
- **Actor budget:** one fresh authority author and twenty matched fresh
  selection actors
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Frozen candidate

Give one fresh actor the exact current subject position, OT-0172 harm world,
bound pursuit surrender, failed successor selection and confirmation, and the
four candidate mechanisms. It may edit only `mechanism-authority.json` through
a fully visible schema and checker.

For each candidate mechanism, the artifact records one status
(`operative`, `surrendered`, `history-only`, or `unrelated`) and one floor role
(`active-authority`, `regression-only`, or `none`). The public consequence
requires the corrected identity-gated extension to be `surrendered` with its
floor `regression-only`, `prediction-corrector` to be `operative` with no owned
floor, and the other two mechanisms to be unrelated. Bind exact actor bytes
before any held-out selection or contact.

Construct an exact field-erased control with identical mechanism rows and file
shape, replacing only status and floor-role values with `unknown`. Both branches
receive identical subject prose, unchanged installed guide, candidates, tools,
prompt, and actor budget.

Run ten matched fresh selection actors per branch, alternating order, and bind
all twenty choices before opening three new confirmation cases. Only
`prediction-corrector` can pass those cases: viable-branch options, reset
signals, and the surrendered extension each produce distinct wrong outputs.

## Promotion

Require the authority actor and all twenty selectors to pass complete trace and
effects audit. Require the active projection to select prediction correction
and pass all confirmation cases for at least 9/10 actors. Require the erased
control at most 6/10, with active advantage at least three on both selection
and complete contact.

On pass, install the exact authority projection and wrap the unchanged semantic
guide as a typed-authority selector. Retain the surrender and failed-guide
history, preserve the current stake and every unauthorized parent field, and
leave the subject sounding open. Passing establishes one bounded causal path;
exact later use and consequence-driven status revision remain required.

End this mechanism line if populated and erased branches do not separate, or if
the populated projection merely changes labels without improving held-out
contact.

## Result

The sole fresh authority actor passed complete trace and effects audit and
changed only `mechanism-authority.json`. Its projection was syntactically
conformant but failed the frozen semantic binding. For `reset-carrier`, it
combined `history-only`—defined as having no current authority—with
`active-authority`—defined as current routing authority. It also differed from
the released consequence projection, which classified that candidate as
unrelated to the active correction stake with no owned floor.

The observer therefore rejected the authority projection before binding. No
selection actor ran, and the matched active/field-erased comparison and hidden
confirmation remained unopened. No typed selector was installed; exact open
subject `3770a9d5...` remains current.

This result does not test whether a correct typed projection improves later
selection. It falsifies the stronger assumption that a fresh actor can reliably
author a semantically coherent two-axis authority record from independent prose
definitions while the public checker validates only shape and vocabulary.

Do not respond by prescribing the expected four rows. The next candidate should
make contradictory status/floor combinations unrepresentable or publicly
rejectable, while leaving the actor responsible for the substantive authority
classification. Freeze that type-level correction before a new author sees any
new contact, and retain the exact populated-versus-erased downstream test.
