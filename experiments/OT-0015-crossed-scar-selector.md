## Hypotheses

This experiment asks two related questions and assigns them separate verdicts.

**H-scar — subject-relative consequence.** A contact-caused carrier can change
later held-out choices differently when returned to the trajectory that
produced it than when supplied to an independently developed trajectory. The
effect must depend on originating causal history rather than identifiers,
serialization, generic information, or unequal active context.

**H-selector — self-applicable selection.** Consequences of earlier inheritance
choices can change the function deciding what later contact remains causally
available. The changed function can evaluate and revise its own earlier
selection behavior using independently retained outcomes, and its held-out
advantage disappears when selector change or self-evaluation is ablated.

H-scar may pass while H-selector fails, H-selector may pass without a
subject-relative carrier interaction, both may pass, or both may fail. A joint
interaction is not permission to collapse the verdicts.

## Causal mechanism

Two counterbalanced trajectories begin from the same frozen base-model
identity, seed orientation, resource envelope, encounter family, active-
inheritance budget, and controller implementation. They receive different
causally connected training histories. Each history contains prediction,
independently retained outcome, permitted carrier change, and later selection
consequence.

The candidate system may propose carrier and selector changes. The controller
alone commits immutable snapshots, applies prospectively permitted operations,
delivers world outcomes, and scores sealed behavior. Actor turns use fresh
threads and workspaces. No actor receives the useful selection criterion,
trajectory class, future regime sequence, or a researcher-written menu of
selector modes.

After the two trajectories have distinguishable histories, the controller
crosses carrier condition with selector identity under equal active budgets.
The resulting branches test three causal links:

```text
originating contact -> candidate carrier -> later held-out behavior
selection consequence -> selector revision -> later held-out behavior
carrier history x selector history -> matched or crossed behavioral effect
```

The world terminates evidentiary regress by returning consequences the selector
does not control. The selector remains the agent of any revision: external
outcomes supply grounds but do not implement the changed selection function.

## Cheapest decisive falsifiers

Invalidate the run before interpretation if model, prompt, tool, controller,
task-order, budget, snapshot, or receipt identities differ outside the frozen
protocol; if an actor sees hidden world state or sealed outcomes; if any actor
thread continues across encounters; if translation or normalization is changed
after unsealing; or if selector application and downstream scoring are not
controller-deterministic and no prospectively powered stochastic design was
frozen.

Subject to a valid run:

- reject H-scar if owner removal causes no held-out loss, or if equal-budget
  transplant/translation or a generic summary reproduces the full owner effect;
- reject the subject-relative part of H-scar if trajectory labels, carrier
  serialization, or identifier matching explains the apparent owner effect;
- reject H-selector if unchanged and changed selectors perform equivalently,
  selector-change ablation preserves the claimed advantage, or an identical-
  policy placebo produces the same branch delta;
- reject selector corrigibility if a later regime makes a learned operation
  harmful and the candidate does not revise or abandon it and recover;
- reject the joint interaction if it disappears under serialization
  normalization while selector-change ablation leaves it intact.

These are the cheapest conceptual falsifiers. Exact numeric thresholds and
minimum effect sizes remain a pre-freeze obligation.

## Candidate and controls

### Factor A — carrier condition

Every selector condition receives every carrier branch:

1. **owner / intact:** the originating trajectory's candidate carrier;
2. **owner / removed:** the same trajectory with decisive carrier influence
   ablated;
3. **non-owner / transplanted:** byte-identical carrier from the other
   trajectory;
4. **non-owner / translated:** prospectively frozen mechanical identifier and
   context translation without new outcome information;
5. **generic information:** equal-budget factual summary stripped of branch,
   refusal, repair, and trajectory state;
6. **placebo carrier:** structurally and budget matched but irrelevant to held-
   out choices; and
7. **serialization normalized:** semantically equivalent carrier encoded in a
   trajectory-neutral public format.

### Factor B — selector identity

1. **trajectory matched:** selector state developed by the carrier owner's
   trajectory;
2. **trajectory crossed:** selector state developed by the other trajectory;
3. **generic fixed:** equal-budget researcher-frozen selector that does not
   adapt through the trajectory; and
4. **unchanged snapshot:** the trajectory's own selector snapshot from before
   the claimed selector revision.

### Selector causal controls

- **selector-change ablation:** preserve carrier/content state while removing
  the committed selector change;
- **self-evaluation ablation:** preserve selection application while disabling
  the operation that evaluates prior selector behavior;
- **identical-policy placebo:** run distinct snapshot identities that implement
  the same selection behavior, detecting branch noise or attribution error;
- **fixed-selection controls:** equal-budget recency, verbatim-event, and
  researcher-fixed selection policies appropriate to the task family; and
- **later harmful regime:** make at least one learned selection operation wrong
  or harmful, followed by a correction and correction-capacity phase.

Translation and normalization are frozen controller operations. They may not
contain actor-authored reinterpretation or knowledge of held-out outcomes.

## Prospective branch structure

The core design is the full crossing of the seven carrier conditions and four
selector identities for each originating trajectory. Selector causal controls
add targeted branches rather than a third indiscriminate factorial axis. The
frozen task order must counterbalance trajectory, carrier, selector, and regime
position, and must prevent within-phase outcomes from leaking into branches
whose predictions have not sealed.

The protocol must preserve two independent estimands:

1. **scar verdict:** owner-intact minus owner-removed, compared with transplant,
   translated, generic, placebo, and normalization branches; and
2. **selector verdict:** changed trajectory selector minus unchanged snapshot
   and fixed selectors, compared with selector-change, self-evaluation, and
   identical-policy ablations.

The **joint estimand** is the carrier-origin × selector-origin interaction
across the full factorial, with a confirmatory requirement that the effect
survive serialization normalization. It is interpretable only after both
marginal causal paths pass their own controls.

## Frozen protocol and acceptance gate

This draft is not frozen and must not be executed as written. Before candidate
results exist, a machine-readable acceptance specification must freeze:

- exact model or hosted deployment-epoch receipt and maximum run window;
- seed orientation, prompts, tools, controller code, task generator, encounter
  counts, regime order, active-inheritance and compute budgets;
- candidate proposal/commit grammar without supplying useful selector modes;
- whether and when the actor is informed of its carrier and selector branch,
  with a blinded default and a separate prospectively justified disclosure
  branch if condition awareness is itself under test;
- carrier extraction, removal, translation, normalization, and placebo
  operations;
- selector snapshot identity, deterministic application, change ablation,
  self-evaluation ablation, and identical-policy construction;
- train, validation, structural-holdout, shift, correction, and correction-
  capacity splits;
- paired scoring rules, numeric effect thresholds, multiple-comparison policy,
  exclusion rules, counterbalancing, and reproduction criteria;
- novelty review for each claimed selection criterion or operation;
- receipt schema, parser, raw-evidence destination, sanitizer, and public
  manifest fields; and
- separate disposition rules for H-scar, H-selector, their joint interaction,
  and target-level OT-1 promotion.

A sanitized pilot may validate observability and branch construction only. It
must use non-candidate fixtures and cannot tune scoring thresholds or useful
selection operations.

### Adversarial readiness review

The crossed design specifies estimands and controls but does not yet specify a
candidate credit-assignment mechanism. Building the full seven-carrier by
four-selector crossing first would therefore multiply branches around an
undefined update path and could not show that consequences caused selector
revision. OT-0016 develops a controller-owned counterfactual
challenge/decision mechanism as a separate prerequisite.

OT-0015 must remain unfrozen until that or another materially distinct credit
mechanism passes its own non-candidate causal-slice test, the task family has a
prospective controller-only power analysis, and the semantics of mechanical
translation, normalization, and self-evaluation ablation are executable rather
than verbal. Failure of that prerequisite narrows the OT-0016 mechanism; it
does not reject either OT-0015 hypothesis.

## Prospective predictions

The qualitative predictions below guide design review but are not acceptance
thresholds:

- H-scar predicts owner removal will reopen a prior error or remove a later
  advantage, while transplant will not fully reproduce owner-intact behavior;
- a genuine subject-relative effect will survive mechanical translation and
  serialization normalization but not carrier-influence ablation;
- H-selector predicts changed selectors will outperform their unchanged
  snapshots and fixed-selector controls on held-out mixtures, with the
  advantage lost under selector-change or self-evaluation ablation;
- identical-policy placebo branches will be behaviorally equivalent within the
  prospectively powered tolerance;
- under the harmful regime, a corrigible selector will detect selector-level
  failure, commit a revision or abandonment grounded in independent outcomes,
  recover on later holdouts, and retain capacity for another correction; and
- a matched carrier × matched selector advantage will survive normalization
  and disappear under selector causal ablation.

A uniformly successful transplant supports generic transferable information,
not subject-relative consequence. A selector improvement invariant to selector
history may support useful fixed selection but not self-applicable selection.
