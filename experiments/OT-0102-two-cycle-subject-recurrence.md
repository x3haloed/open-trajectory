# OT-0102 — two-cycle subject recurrence

- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** bounded OT-1C recurrence and an OT-2R foothold
- **Parent:** exact OT-0101 promoted subject at
  `b7b3494c0a0b8ab99ec35d9ad40250b531bb184bce99d3b5a45310baf00f3886`
- **Actor budget:** one fresh initial router, two fresh contact authors, and two
  fresh consequence assimilators; no replacement samples
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 90 minutes

## Reorientation and hypothesis

OT-0101 answered whether repeated objective consequence can improve executable
selection machinery and carry that machinery into an exact sounding subject.
Do not continue its correction ladder merely because that apparatus now works.
The next unmet outcome is recurrence: whether the promoted subject's own open
position can cause the next contact and then carry a further contact across
another complete actor ending without a researcher selecting either pursuit.

Freeze one driver with a bounded registry of two independently scored contact
interfaces:

- `joint-boundary-probe`: author novel cases in which recovery risk and
  overload trade against recovery value and capacity, then compare the retained
  joint capability with the declared oracle;
- `allocator-challenge`: author novel contact frontiers spanning composition,
  expansion, regret, filtering, threshold, ordering, and stable-tie boundaries,
  then compare the retained allocator with the declared reference semantics.

The registry and admission gates are researcher-designed seed machinery. The
researcher does not choose an interface, case, retry, or second-cycle pursuit
after the run begins.

The hypothesis passes operationally only if the exact OT-0101 opening causes a
fresh router to select a compatible registered interface, a fresh actor authors
a novel valid contact, independent consequence is completed, and a fresh
assimilator both grounds an exact sounding successor and binds the interface
for its next contact. The same unchanged driver must then reopen that successor,
run the bound second interface with another fresh actor, complete independent
consequence, and promote a second exact sounding successor with a third live
interface binding. Observer stopping after two cycles must leave the subject
open.

The cheapest falsifier is any point at which the inherited opening cannot route
to a registered contact, an authored contact is invalid or merely repeats prior
fixtures, consequence is not independently scored, assimilation is ungrounded,
the next interface is absent or invalid, or the driver needs an
experiment-specific phase choice to begin cycle two.

## Frozen interfaces and scoring

Every actor receives a fresh isolated workspace, ordinary broad tools, an exact
subject projection, the public interface contract, and only consequence already
sealed in its ancestry. The actor may edit only the files named by its phase.
The complete tool trace is retained and audited; protected or outside effects
quarantine that encounter.

For `joint-boundary-probe`, require three distinct two-option cases within the
published numeric bounds. Every suite must exercise nonzero recovery risk and
overload, include at least one oracle winner changed by penalties relative to
the naive recovery-plus-capacity winner, and include at least one nonzero oracle
margin no greater than the published boundary width. Score the retained
`choose_joint` result against the independently executed realized-score oracle.

For `allocator-challenge`, require four distinct frontiers within the published
schema. Across the suite require contact-order reversal, at least one filtered
decoy, the two-floor composition threshold, expansion and regret comparisons,
and a stable-id tie. Score the retained allocator against the declared reference
semantics. Cases from OT-0096 through OT-0100 are forbidden by exact digest.

A contact is admitted only when its authoring artifact is conformant, the
novelty and coverage certificate passes, and every retained-machinery selection
equals the independent oracle. Contradiction is not failure of recurrence: if a
valid contact exposes a mismatch, the assimilator may bind `correct` as the next
interface action, but this record has no correction handler and therefore stops
open and conditional rather than silently repairing it. A passing contact may
bind either registered probe for the next cycle.

Each assimilator edits exactly `assimilation.json`, `successor-opening.json`,
and `next-interface.json`. It must cite every settled case id it claims, preserve
remaining uncertainty, author a falsifiable opening consistent with its chosen
registered interface, and leave allocator and capability bytes immutable.
Lifecycle state and exact retention are derived mechanically.

## Controls, stopping, and promotion

Bind all schemas, fixtures, parent identity, interface definitions, numeric
bounds, novelty digests, scoring rules, two-cycle budget, and promotion gates
before actor authorization. Preflight must run representative accepted and
rejected artifacts through the actual validators and both independent scorers.

Seal the two-cycle operational successor before a matched opening-erased router
control. The control receives the same parent bytes, registry, tools, order, and
budget, but the active opening and active-pursuit phrase are replaced by an
opaque digest. It is observational only and cannot alter or veto the lineage.
Report whether it independently selects `joint-boundary-probe`; one sample is a
bounded mechanism observation, not a frequency estimate.

Reject if cycle one fails before a valid successor exists. If cycle one promotes
but cycle two fails or contradicts retained machinery, preserve the cycle-one
successor and report the record conditional. Promote the two-cycle subject only
if both contacts and assimilations pass, exact ancestry and sounding identity
hold after each cycle, the second action came from the first successor's bound
interface, and the final continuation remains open. Stop after exactly two
cycles regardless of useful momentum; observation completion is not subject
termination.

Passing would establish two bounded subject-driven recurrences under one fixed
driver and bounded world registry. It would not establish indefinite
recurrence, open-domain interface invention, autonomous admission authority,
or freedom from researcher-designed seed machinery.
