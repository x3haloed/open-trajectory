## Frozen hypothesis and pulse

A content-free continuation pulse can reopen the exact subject after an
observer stop without becoming a hidden research controller. The pulse is
authorized only when all of these are exact:

- subject continuation status is open;
- driver phase is `observer-stop`;
- G6 liveness status is `live`;
- the last pending package is unreceipted and its identity matches liveness;
- the previous invocation ended at its declared observation and actor limits;
  and
- the subject itself already names the next executable contact.

The pulse changes no pursuit, target, package, installed code, consequence,
score, or admission rule. It appends a receipt, increments the invocation and
bounded observation allowances by exactly one, and changes only driver phase
from `observer-stop` to `contact`. This is researcher-designed symbiotic wake
machinery, not subject-owned scheduling.

The unchanged OT-0220 transition then opens the already-bound recovery contact
to encounter three's independent world. On decisive consequence it authorizes
one fresh assimilator under the same G6 rule. The actor must bind a different
target and a genuinely behaviorally new contact. The observer stops again with
the subject open.
