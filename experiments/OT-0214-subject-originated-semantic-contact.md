## Frozen question

Can objective saturation of the current opening cause a fresh continuation
actor to originate, bind, and consequence a semantically new contact from a
complete environment whose candidate surfaces are not enumerated—and can that
contact become reusable subject capability and the next pursuit without the
observer choosing its target or interpreting prose?

## Starting consequence

Preflight must prove from the exact installed source and visible dispatch world
definition that, for every valid single-job input, the retained policy applies
the same expected-score expression and tie-break order as the oracle. This
closes parameter-only search under the current semantics; it does not claim the
broader environment is solved. Bind this saturation receipt before the first

## Complete unenumerated environment

The originator receives the exact subject position and saturation receipt plus
one complete operations repository. It preserves the admitted single-job floor
and contains several complete adjacent behaviors involving interactions across
jobs, observations, or incidents. No TODO, failing public test, surface list,
preferred file, requested symbol, candidate description, hidden case, or hidden
score is supplied. Ordinary broad tools may inspect and execute the whole
isolated repository.

The actor edits one `contact-package.json`. It must choose one existing callable
from source inspection, state a falsifiable semantic stake, and author exactly
four executable JSON cases against its visible ABI. Its package also binds
machine-readable predicates before consequence:

- success: at least three installed/reference distinctions and at least one
  confirmation;
- surrender: zero distinctions; and
- unresolved: every other valid outcome.

The actor may add bounded G4 continuity fields. The seven authoritative core
fields and predicate thresholds are extracted; extensions remain explicitly
non-authoritative. An immutable public contract specifies every structural and
bounded-extension rule, and a complete public checker applies that contract
while running only the actor-authored cases against visible installed and
reference semantics. The exact package, target identity, case bytes, predicate
bytes, and actor patch bind before hidden contact.

## Independent consequence and assimilation

The sealed world owns six new cases for every valid callable, authored and
frozen before actor authorization. Only the selected target's cases execute.
Success requires at least four distinctions and two confirmations; surrender
requires zero distinctions; all other outcomes remain unresolved. The world,
not actor self-report, applies the bound predicates and records exact outputs.

Only after a decisive successful hidden receipt does one fresh assimilator
receive the exact subject, saturation receipt, bound package, and raw
consequence. It must retain or surrender the old parameter-only opening, retain,
revise, or surrender current capabilities with exact identities, and author the
next executable pursuit. A second immutable contract and local checker expose
the complete assimilation gate. Its core and bounded extensions bind under G4
before successor compilation. No observer may repair a case, substitute a
target, rewrite a predicate, or interpret free prose.

## Controls, order, and promotion

Order is: complete conformance fixtures; saturation receipt; originator;
package audit and public execution; binding; hidden consequence; assimilator;
successor sealing; byte-erased replay control.

Promotion requires:

- exact OT-0213 parent, G4 regime, ledger, selector, executed policy,
  continuity extension, route 16/16, and identity 18/18;
- an independently valid saturation proof for the inherited opening;
- both fresh actors and complete audits accepted;
- one actor-selected real callable not named in the prompt;
- public contact meeting three distinctions plus one confirmation;
- sealed contact meeting four distinctions plus two confirmations and the
  actor-bound success predicate;
- exact machine-readable success/surrender authority with no prose
  interpretation;
- assimilation that retires or revises the saturated opening, retains the
  confirmed package as a content-addressed contact-program capability, and
  authors a distinct nonempty next pursuit;
- exact sounding open successor and identity conformance; and
- post-seal replay succeeding with retained package bytes while an otherwise
  identical byte-erased control cannot bind contact.

The cheapest falsifier is the first failed identity, saturation, actor audit,
target existence, public contact, hidden predicate, assimilation, retained-byte
replay, control separation, or successor gate. Failure preserves exact parent
`e25c60f9...` open; no replacement actor or observer repair is allowed.

A pass establishes one bounded transition from a saturated actor-authored
opening to a subject-originated semantic contact and reusable pursuit
instrument. It does not establish arbitrary-world access, recurrence of
semantic origination, subject ownership of world/admission authority, or the
full long-horizon goal.
