# OT-0216 — contact-bearing continuation liveness

- **Status:** promoted; G5 active prospectively
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** prospective G4 → G5 evaluation-regime transition
- **Construction evidence:** OT-0215 assimilation accepted lexical novelty while
  probably repeating completed recovery-capacity contact
- **Parent:** exact operational OT-0215 successor `f0e044c2...`

## Transition reason

G4 correctly separates a fixed authoritative core from bounded
subject-authored non-authoritative extensions. For continuation liveness,
however, the current core asks only that `next_pursuit` be nonempty and not
exactly equal to the inherited string.

Expected: an assimilator that retires completed contact opens a distinct missing
stake. Observed: the OT-0215 assimilator passed by writing a near-paraphrase of
the 4+2, 6/6 recovery-capacity encounter it had just received. The operational
move and artifact-causality comparison remain valid. Only the claim that its new
phrase denotes live missing contact is unsupported.

## Incumbent G4

G4 retains an experiment-specific authoritative decision core and admits up to
eight bounded additional fields as non-authoritative continuity content. For
OT-0215, liveness was:

```text
nonempty(next_pursuit)
and next_pursuit != inherited_opening
```

Every G4 hard anchor remains non-negotiable: exact ancestry, fresh isolated
actors, bind before consequence, independent world authority, immutable sealed
receipts, contained-effects audit, G4 extension bounds, route and identity
floors, privacy, and no retrospective rescoring.

## Challenger G5

G5 keeps the descriptive `next_pursuit` but does not derive liveness from its
wording. A continuation is operationally live only when it also binds one
`next_contact` package containing:

- a content identifier, existing callable identity, stake, and exact ABI;
- exactly four executable cases with canonical inputs;
- fixed machine predicates allowing success, surrender, and unresolved
  consequence; and
- a package digest for which no independent consequence receipt exists in the
  current subject.

Case IDs are local labels. Duplicate detection canonicalizes target, ABI, and
case inputs while excluding IDs and prose. At least two canonical inputs must
be absent from completed contact packages for that target. Renaming old cases,
rewriting the stake, changing order, or paraphrasing the pursuit cannot create
liveness.

The package may be actor-authored and may have been tested locally. That does
not complete it: only a separately authorized world receipt resolves the
contact. G5 grants the package no world, scoring, or admission authority.

## Construction and held-out validation

Use OT-0215 only to construct the challenger and construction fixtures. Freeze
the G5 compiler before opening a separate, deterministically generated held-out
bundle. The bundle must contain, in counterbalanced order:

- genuinely unreceipted executable contacts with at least two new canonical
  inputs;
- the same live packages with valid bounded G4 extensions;
- renamed and reordered duplicates of completed contact;
- already-receipted package digests;
- prose-only paraphrases without executable contact;
- packages with only one new canonical input;
- missing, excessive, deep, broad, invalid-key, and oversized forms; and
- invalid callable, ABI, case-count, or predicate shapes.

The held-out generator seed `216503911`, expected labels, and score are frozen
before the evidence run. The bundle contains sixteen counterbalanced fixtures.
G5 must accept every live package and reject every stale or malformed package.
G4 must retain its historical exact-core and extension behavior; its inability
to distinguish the new stale/live package pairs is the identified blind spot,
not a retrospective failure. Preflight expects G5 16/16 and observes the
incumbent liveness rule at 7/16; the official run must reconstruct those exact
fixtures and digests.

## Promotion

Promote G5 only if:

- all prior G4 hard-anchor fixtures remain exact;
- every held-out live/stale/malformed label is correct;
- renamed or reordered completed contact cannot manufacture liveness;
- a bounded valid extension remains admitted but non-authoritative;
- the exact OT-0215 subject compiles without changing its operational
  capabilities or receipts;
- its current prose-only reopening is marked `liveness-unresolved`, not erased
  or retrospectively rejected; and
- route 16/16, identity 18/18, reconstruction, and privacy pass.

The prospective compiled subject may add only the transition receipt and a
`continuation_liveness` record. Every pre-existing operational field, exact
continuation phrase/status, capability, and receipt must remain byte-equivalent,
and the resulting subject must satisfy identity conformance.

On promotion, G5 governs future continuation admission. Historical G4
operational outcomes stand. Historical claims that relied only on lexical
opening distinctness become stale when next decision-relevant; they are not
eagerly rescored.

## Claim boundary

A pass establishes a better prospective distinction between a turn ending and
the subject's available music ending: executable unreceipted contact can cross
the boundary even when prose is poor, while novel prose cannot cross alone. It
does not prove that the contact is important, semantically expansive, safe in
an arbitrary world, or ultimately successful. Independent consequence and
later correction remain required.

## Result

The frozen transition passed. G5 classified all 3/3 construction fixtures and
16/16 seeded held-out fixtures correctly. The incumbent lexical rule
distinguished only 7/16 held-out live/stale pairs. G5 rejected renamed and
reordered completed cases under the same canonical identity, rejected a package
with only one new canonical input, rejected prose-only and malformed forms, and
accepted both genuinely unreceipted packages including the valid G4-extension
variant.

Every OT-0215 operational field, capability, receipt, continuation phrase, and
status remained exact. Route stayed 16/16, identity stayed 18/18, and exact
successor `b579ce63...` conforms. It adds only the G5 transition receipt and a
`continuation_liveness` record. The subject remains operationally `open`, while
the inherited prose opening is correctly marked `unresolved` because it carries
no executable unreceipted contact.

G5 is active for future continuation admission. No historical operational
result was rescored. The next experiment should give a fresh actor the exact
subject and a complete environment, require it to bind a G5-valid contact
without an observer-selected target, then let independent consequence—not the
package or prose—decide whether that contact joins the subject.
