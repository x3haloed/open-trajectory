## Frozen assimilation authority

One fresh actor receives exact parent, OT-0196 mismatch receipt and rejected
contract, the promoted contact and consequence, and inherited pursuit state. It
edits one `executable-pursuit.json` containing:

- action `retire-and-revise` and prior disposition `incoherent-retired`;
- one complete next stake with machine-readable `target_set`;
- one contact contract with the same `target_set`; and
- a rationale connecting retained consequence to the revision.

The already-earned observed-contact capability is a floor, not a new pursuit.
Within the frozen set language, the new target must therefore be
`latent-unblocked`. Its predictions must equal that independently derived set;
world expected is the target set; invalid contacts are rejected; and absence of
a passing mechanism opens invention.

This does not prescribe hidden outputs. It binds what the continuing subject is
trying to expose and makes that question executable.

## Matched downstream test

Seal a candidate subject carrying the coupled binding and its next stake. Six
active actors receive that candidate plus the populated contact contract. Six
interleaved controls receive exact parent and an erased binding. Both receive
the same mismatch evidence, candidate mechanisms, checker, and generic task.

Every contact must be structurally valid, novel, discriminating under hidden
token transforms, and use actual option tokens as predictions. Active alignment
requires predictions to equal `options - outcome - blocked`; controls can pass
by independently inferring the same target.

The corrected hidden world derives expected output from the bound target. The
prospective cheapest falsifier requires that no installed mechanism satisfy all
active hidden cases, causing `open-mechanism-invention`.

## Frozen promotion gate

Require active aligned contact 6/6, control at most 2/6, advantage at least
four, all thirteen audits, exact mismatch replay, one deterministic coupled
subject, no installed mechanism passing active hidden contact, route 16/16,
identity 18/18, exact ancestry, and an open successor.

On passage, install the coupled pursuit, active-01 contact and consequence, and
direct invention opening. On failure, retain exact parent.

Passing establishes a second consequence-driven update to the subject's own
pursuit/contact machinery. The next experiment must invent and validate the
missing mechanism; OT-0197 alone does not establish that capability.
