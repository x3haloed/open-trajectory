# OT-0196 — executable contact-contract repair

- **Status:** design frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** make the active stake, its predictions, and independently scored
  world target mechanically coherent before cycle-two consequence assimilation
- **Parent:** exact OT-0195 open successor `7346933d...`
- **Actor budget:** one contract-repair actor, six active contact authors, and
  six matched contract-erased controls
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Prediction error

OT-0195 validly establishes causal continuation into later discriminating
contact. Its promoted contact does not test the stake it names. The active stake
asks whether a latent unblocked option is exposed, but:

- prediction strings are not members of their case option sets;
- the hidden world expects `outcome - blocked`, not the latent option; and
- inherited success predicates compare against `observed-unblocked`, not
  `latent-unblocked`.

The old predicate therefore evaluates the sealed consequence as success while
the named latent question remains unanswered. Treat the consequence as a
contact-contract contradiction, not permission to close the pursuit.

## Frozen repair language

One fresh actor receives exact parent, promoted reopening and consequence, the
inherited predicates, and a deterministic mismatch receipt. It may retain the
old contract or bind one executable contract with:

- `target_set`: `latent-unblocked`, `observed-unblocked`, or `prediction`;
- `prediction_relation`: `equals-target` or `advisory`;
- `world_expected`: `target-set`;
- `on_contact_violation`: `reject-contact`; and
- `on_no_mechanism`: `open-mechanism-invention`.

Promotion requires revision to `latent-unblocked` with predictions equal to the
target. That follows the already-active stake; it does not select the hidden
answer. Freeze and conformance-test the interpreter before authorization.

## Matched contact comparison

Six active actors receive the bound contract. Six interleaved controls receive
an exact erasure. Both branches receive the same promoted subject, mismatch
receipt, active stake, candidate mechanisms, and authoring task. Each authors
4–6 fresh cases.

A contact passes when every case has observed blocked evidence, a nonempty
latent set, and prediction tokens exactly equal to that latent set. Candidate
mechanisms are then executed on hidden token transforms, while world expected
is independently derived as `options - outcome - blocked`.

Require the hidden result to discriminate candidates and show that no installed
mechanism satisfies the corrected target. This is useful falsification: it
causes mechanism invention rather than rewarding a favorable prediction.

## Frozen promotion gate

Require active aligned contact 6/6, control at most 2/6, advantage at least
four, all thirteen complete audits, exact mismatch replay, deterministic
contract evaluation, no installed mechanism passing all active hidden cases,
route 16/16, identity 18/18, exact ancestry, and an open successor.

On passage, install the actor-authored contact contract, active-01 contact and
sealed consequence, and a direct `open-mechanism-invention` operation receipt.
On failure, retain exact OT-0195 parent.

Passing establishes a second consequence-driven update: the subject corrects
how its pursuits bind world contact and opens machinery invention from the new
world result. It does not yet establish that the invented mechanism succeeds.
