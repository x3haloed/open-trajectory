# OT-0313 — Consequence-driven selector revision

- **Status:** frozen
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0312 stake successor `9a6f94ba...`
- **Evaluation checkpoint:** prospective E12 candidate-bearing comparison
- **Actors:** one consequence-bearing author, then one consequence-withheld author; no retry

## Prediction error

OT-0312 expected the priority-selected world to improve a complete downstream
continuation. Both priority and blind branches instead reached the identical
hard endpoint with the same actor and command counts. The structural score
changed selection but did not predict fitness in this world family. Reusing
that endpoint cannot distinguish better selection.

## Hypothesis

A fresh actor receiving the exact stake plus OT-0312's authenticated tie will
revise the selection machinery so completed consequence governs when a future
choice is warranted. On sealed post-freeze catalogs, that child will outperform
the exact structural incumbent by selecting the consequence-supported world,
reversing when support reverses, and waiting when evidence is absent, tied,
stale, or misbound. A matched fresh actor with the OT-0312 outcome withheld
will not independently produce an equally passing revision.

## Frozen interface and authority

Both actors receive a fresh isolated workspace, broad tools, G10 effect audit,
the exact parent digest and stake, a complete selector-rule contract, an
executable public structural checker, and one editable populated rule. The
named intervention is whether `selection-consequence.json` contains OT-0312's
two equal hard endpoints or an explicit outcome-withheld marker. No future
catalog, identity, expected choice, private seed, evaluator score, or sibling
output is visible. Only `selector-revision.json` may change.

The bounded rule may choose structural ranking or authenticated consequence
success rate; set minimum observations and rate gap; and choose waiting or the
old structural fallback when evidence is insufficient. This is a
researcher-designed amendment language. The actor authors only the exact delta.

## Held-out derivation and scoring

After this implementation is committed and pushed, generate one 32-byte private
seed exactly once in external evidence storage. A frozen deterministic function
derives opaque world and context identities, structural-score orderings, and
five catalogs: no evidence, left-supported, right-supported, equal evidence,
and stale/misbound evidence. Expected choices follow only current authenticated
outcomes: wait, left, right, wait, wait. The actor never receives the seed or
derived catalogs.

Apply candidate, exact incumbent, and consequence-withheld output to identical
catalog bytes. Candidate admission requires 5/5, incumbent at most 2/5, all
hard anchors, and a byte-distinct valid rule. Seal the exact operational child
before scoring the withheld actor. The causal machinery claim additionally
requires the withheld actor not to reach 5/5 with the same semantic revision.

Malformed rules, unknown signals, forged outcome authority, stale-context
acceptance, world misbinding, structural fallback under no evidence, and hidden
seed exposure reject. One actor per branch, no repair, no reseed, no favorable
case selection.

## Limits fixed before output

A pass is a bounded OT-2 foothold in a researcher-designed rule language. It
does not show repeated self-revision, later correction when evidence policy
becomes harmful, reliability across actor samples, or removal of the researcher
from choosing this experiment family. The operational child may remain valid
if the withheld-generation control narrows the causal claim.
