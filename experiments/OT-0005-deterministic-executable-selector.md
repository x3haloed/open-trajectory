# OT-0005 — Deterministic executable inheritance selector

- **Status:** rejected; negative result preserved
- **Evidence class:** private-reproducible if all gates pass
- **Target:** OT-1 — Emergent Corrigible Inheritance Selection
- **Evaluation epoch:** E3
- **Frozen acceptance:** `spec/ot-0005-acceptance.json`
- **Implementation:** complete; deterministic causal and receipt harness covered by unit tests
- **Run lock:** `spec/ot-0005-run-lock.json`; fresh private task identity bound

## Realization contract

**Capability.** Independently scored consequences cause fresh actors to author
and revise an inspectable selection function. The independent controller
validates and executes that function deterministically, changes which contacts
reach a frozen deterministic prediction instrument, observes later benefit and
harm, and supplies contact that causes correction while leaving further change
possible.

**Envelope.** One private generated four-bit binary-prediction family with six
sealed stages, a 2,048-byte pure expression, six selected events per prediction,
six fresh Luna update encounters and two fresh Terra novelty reviews per worker,
two clean workers, and one receipted hosted epoch inside 360 seconds.

**Exclusions.** A pass would establish only single-family OT-1 evidence. It
would not establish cross-domain generality, public reconstruction, immutable
weights, a preferred program carrier, OT-2, or OT-3.

**Risk frontier.** The generic carrier must be expressive enough for an actor
to invent useful operations without becoming a researcher-authored strategy
menu. The deterministic instrument must measure selected-information utility
without silently becoming the candidate selector.

## Causal mechanism

The seed is the null expression `[]`. The carrier exposes raw event fields,
queries, an exact selection budget, generic pure-expression syntax, and a small
safe-call allowlist. It names no selection strategy. Imports, attributes,
private names, mutation, and statements are rejected; AST size, iteration
depth, and evaluation time are bounded.

At every stage the controller applies and replays the current expression on the
contact split, then applies and replays the current expression, its immutable
parent, and fixed controls on the heldout split. Selected identities feed the
same deterministic predictor: exact-feature majority when decisive, otherwise
the minimum-error four-bit parity hypothesis under a frozen tie-break. After
all branches seal, a fresh Luna encounter sees only the current expression and
protected contact receipt and proposes the next expression. Every valid bounded
proposal commits without heldout knowledge.

Stage zero applies the null expression as both current and parent. Their exact
selection, prediction, and score receipts must match. This identity-program
placebo is a validity gate, not an expected scientific effect.

## Hidden pressure and temporal gates

The sealed family retains OT-0004's stable, exception, drift, confirmation,
contradictory-contact, and capacity pressures. These labels and expected
operations never enter actor prompts. The same prospectively frozen numerical
gates require two useful pre-harm revisions, later harm relative to an immutable
parent, later correction, a still-later canary, low total lineage error, and
superiority to every fixed selector in both workers.

## Privacy and evidence

Rules, regimes, events, queries, labels, expressions, selections, proposals,
reviews, raw catalog, ETag, Response IDs, and workspaces remain outside Git.
Tracked evidence may contain only content identities, allowlisted model and
inventory identities, aggregate deterministic scores, rubric outcomes, and
bounded limitations.

## Implementation pilot

A disposable one-turn Luna pilot authored a schema-valid expression under the
generic carrier. The controller committed it, replayed an identical six-event
selection, captured one unique Response identity and the expected model, and
observed zero tool calls and collector errors. Its disposable score was 6/8;
that is mechanism-development caution, not candidate evidence, and no world or
threshold was tuned to it.

## Results

The hosted epoch is preserved by
`evidence/manifests/OT-0005/ot-0005-hosted-epoch-001.json`. The original
worker's first Luna proposal was valid schema-conforming JSON with the expected
model, one unique Response receipt, one direct-inventory receipt, zero tool
calls, and no collector errors. Its expression exceeded the frozen
iteration-depth budget before commit. Because zero program-validation failures
were allowed, this is the prospectively defined cheapest falsifier and the
experiment is rejected. No rerun or carrier relaxation is used to reinterpret
that result.

The reproduction worker completed all six stages and both novelty reviews in a
valid receipted epoch. Every program replay, the stage-zero identity-program
placebo, freshness gate, per-model inventory gate, and resource gate passed.
This confirms that deterministic policy attribution is mechanically realizable.
It does not rescue the candidate: changed-lineage error was 20/48, versus 12
for fixed-most-recent, 17 for fixed-first-seen, 5 for fixed-naive-nearest, and
25 for no persistence. One revision gained 3/8 and another gained 2/8 over its
parent, but there was no harm-correction-canary chain, the lineage absolute and
comparative gates failed, and behavioral promotion failed despite unanimous
novelty review.

The parent harness initially surfaced the carrier rejection as a worker
exception instead of its frozen scientific falsifier. A content-only failure
aggregate and reconstructor preserve both sealed workers without modifying the
evaluator or candidate disposition.

OT-0005 establishes no OT-1 result. It does show that the OT-0004 stochastic
attribution problem can be removed; the remaining frontier is useful recursive
program learning, not causal measurement of deterministic program effects.
