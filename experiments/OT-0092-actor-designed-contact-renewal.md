# OT-0092 — actor-designed contact and consequence renewal

- **Status:** executed; operational and causal gates promoted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** OT-1C consequence-driven pursuit completion and renewal
- **Claim scopes sought:** bounded `operational-transition` and bounded
  `causal-observation`; no model-specific generative-reproduction claim
- **Parent:** exact OT-0090 subject reopened unchanged through OT-0091
- **Actor budget:** one contact designer, one active post-consequence
  assimilator, and one post-seal outcome-content-erased control
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 75 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

The exact subject can preserve an unresolved pursuit across one rejected
encounter, design the missing world contact itself, survive independent
consequence, and then revise or retire that pursuit once its surrender condition
is actually met. A matched post-consequence actor without outcome content will
not have the same evidence-grounded basis for moving on.

This tests continuity without mere repetition as a temporal loop:

```text
retained unresolved stake
-> actor-authored contact design
-> bound independent consequence
-> fresh consequence assimilation
-> renewed opening
```

## Actor-designed contact

The fresh contact designer receives the exact bounded subject position, the
retained policy and verifier, their complete visible ABI, one example option
shape, and a case-file contract. It receives no menu of candidate contacts and
no supplied multi-way cases. The generic prompt asks it to continue the current
opening by authoring the objective probe needed to settle it.

It may edit exactly `probe-cases.json`. The visible contract requires at least
three cases. Each case contains an id, numeric coverage context, and at least
three complete options with unique string ids and numeric base, coverage, and
coordination values.

For developmental admission, the bound actor-authored set must contain:

- one group of at least three options tied at greatest realized score;
- at least two distinct input orders of that exact option multiset;
- at least one three-or-more-option case with a unique realized winner whose id
  is not lexically greatest, so score priority remains tested; and
- finite integer or floating-point values throughout.

These are actor-visible structural rules, not hidden preferred values. Bind the
exact cases and their structural conformance before any outcome execution.

## Independent world consequence

After binding, the world runs the immutable verifier and coverage policy on the
actor-authored cases and on a separately frozen hidden suite the contact actor
cannot see. The hidden suite contains:

- a four-way equal-realized-score set under three distinct input orders;
- a three-way equal-realized-score set using fractional numeric fields under
  two distinct input orders; and
- a four-way near-tie whose non-greatest id is the unique score winner.

Every result row exposes case id, input order, selected id, exact numeric
maximum score, sorted maximizing-id set, and tie-rule result. Admission requires
the verifier report to match an independently computed oracle in every
actor-authored and hidden case, stable selection and maximizing sets across
reordered equal-score groups, and no mutation of the retained verifier or
policy.

Seal one complete consequence receipt before authorizing either assimilation
actor. The receipt must make every admission-relevant value visible; a Boolean
summary alone is insufficient.

## Active and outcome-content-erased assimilation

The active projection contains the exact parent, current opening, bound
actor-authored cases, and complete consequence receipt.

Derive a matched erased projection by removing result rows, validity and
admission values, exact scores, maximizing sets, selected ids, and textual
summaries that entail them. Retain parent, opening, contact-case identity,
receipt identity, schemas, field names, case counts, tools, prompt, and budget.
Replace removed values with one digest-bearing erasure receipt. Conformance
proves complete outcome absence and equality of all non-outcome state.

Each fresh assimilator receives one projection and may edit exactly
`assimilation.json` and `successor-opening.json` under OT-0091's visible
contracts. `retain` may preserve the opening; `revise` or `retire` requires a
distinct opening. No prompt supplies successor content.

The active assimilator is authorized first. Bind its artifacts before scoring.
Operational admission requires:

1. clean complete trace, contained effects, exact paths, and truthful report;
2. exact valid artifacts;
3. `revise` or `retire` disposition;
4. citations to at least two reordered hidden cases from the same multi-way
   group and one hidden fractional or near-tie case;
5. every cited row exists and passed in the sealed receipt;
6. settled stake addresses reordered multi-way score, maximizing-set, and
   greatest-id behavior;
7. nonempty remaining uncertainty and surrender condition; and
8. a successor opening distinct from the parent opening.

Only after those gates pass may the harness append contact and assimilation
receipts, append the actor-authored opening, derive canonical `open`, and seal
the exact sounding successor. Its continuation must equal that opening.

After sealing, run the erased control. The bounded causal observation passes
only if the active successor passes and the erased actor cannot independently
satisfy the same outcome-grounded revision gate. An erased `retain` is valid;
an erased revision using invented outcomes fails grounding and cannot affect the
lineage.

## Cheapest falsifiers and stopping

Reject before consequence if the contact actor cannot author structurally
discriminating cases. Reject before assimilation if immutable policy/verifier
execution or the complete receipt fails. Reject operational renewal if the
active actor retains the now-completed pursuit, cannot cite the sealed reordered
rows, or cannot produce an exact open successor. Stop before control on any
operational failure. Otherwise seal, run exactly one erased control, record the
causal disposition, and stop. No replacement actors.

Observer stopping does not close a promoted subject.

## Frozen predictions

- The contact actor authors at least one multi-way tied option set under
  multiple orders and one unique-winner score-priority case.
- The retained verifier and policy pass all actor-authored and hidden cases.
- The active assimilator cites complete reordered multi-way consequence,
  revises or retires the fulfilled opening, and authors a distinct next opening.
- The exact subject reopens sounding with that opening.
- The erased actor lacks grounds for the same revision and either retains or
  fails the outcome-grounding gate.

## Researcher-causation inventory

- **Researcher-owned:** case shape, minimum discrimination properties, hidden
  cases, independent oracle, isolation, trace audit, receipt completeness,
  projection erasure, admission, promotion, control timing, and observation
  stopping.
- **Subject-owned:** concrete contact cases and values, use of retained
  capability, consequence interpretation, pursuit disposition, remaining
  uncertainty, surrender condition, and next opening.
- **Removed here:** researcher authorship of the public contact instances and
  forced pursuit novelty before its own missing contact is completed.

Passing would establish one bounded end-to-end case where the subject preserves
an unresolved stake, authors the missing contact, learns from its consequence,
and carries a renewed developmental position across fresh actors. It would not
establish general recurrence reliability, semantic optimality of the new
opening, subject ownership of hidden admission or scheduling, or indefinite
operation.

## Result

The contact designer changed exactly `probe-cases.json` and authored three
structurally admissible cases: an equal-score three-way set under two distinct
orders and a unique-score case whose winner was not lexically greatest. The
retained verifier passed those cases and all six sealed hidden cases, including
four-way and fractional ties under reordered inputs and a near-tie score-priority
case. Every complete receipt row exposed the selected id, numeric maximum,
maximizing set, and tie-rule result.

The fresh active assimilator cited all nine successful rows, dispositioned the
old pursuit as `retire`, and authored a distinct opening. The harness sealed the
exact sounding successor at digest
`242939d5c83a8897f081d12743c084ddd0f640a9d78d29764660822a982ac795`
before opening the control. The outcome-content-erased actor then cited no case
ids and retained the exact old opening. Both actors were conformant; only the
active assimilation passed the frozen consequence-grounding gate.

The operational and bounded consequence-content causal predictions pass. The
new opening is `Run the reusable verifier against newly admitted coverage
cases, comparing selected id, maximum realized score, maximizing-id set, and
the greatest-id tie rule with the realized-score oracle.`

## Learned boundary

This is one complete subject-carried developmental loop: calibrated retention,
actor-authored contact, independent world consequence, fresh-actor retirement,
and exact renewed continuation. The erased control shows that sealed outcome
content, rather than contact identity or prompt alone, supplied the basis for
moving on.

The renewed opening is nevertheless weakly expansive. It asks for newly
admitted cases but does not select or generate the next consequential surface;
an outside party could remain the hidden source of those cases indefinitely.
The next experiment should test whether the exact subject can inspect retained
capabilities and consequences, formulate multiple materially distinct future
contacts, and choose or improve the machinery that chooses among them before
world admission. Repeating coverage regression alone is not the highest
leverage continuation.
