# OT-0090 — confirmation-driven pursuit renewal

- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** OT-1C continuation through confirming world contact
- **Claim scope sought:** bounded `operational-transition`; no causal-control or
  model-specific generative-reproduction claim
- **Parent:** exact OT-0089 open subject
- **Actor budget:** one fresh successor
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

The exact OT-0089 subject can follow its own opening through objective contact
that confirms rather than contradicts its current policy, convert the resolved
uncertainty into a reusable verification capability, and author a distinct next
opening. The harness can then carry that new opening across the actor ending
without forcing a policy mutation or treating local confirmation as subject
closure.

This is a test of renewal after success. It is not a test that the inherited
opening causally beats an erased control, and it does not reopen OT-0089's
rejected target-selection claim.

## Complete verification world

Freeze a small complete repository containing the exact retained coverage
policy, its realized coverage scoring function, public cases, a public
observation command, an actor-visible verifier ABI, and public floor tests.
Public contact must include:

- one case with a unique realized-score winner;
- one equal-realized-score tie whose correct choice is determined by the
  retained option-id tie break; and
- reversal of input order so list position cannot explain the tie result.

Freeze hidden cases before actor authorization. They must include a two-way tie
with unseen ids, a three-way tie, a near-tie where realized score must dominate
id, and changed input orders. The hidden evaluator checks both the immutable
coverage policy and the actor-authored verifier against the visible ABI. No
hidden field name, input shape, return key, or tie convention may be omitted
from the actor-facing repository.

Conformance runs the unchanged policy and a reference verifier across all
public and hidden cases before authorization. It proves order invariance,
unique-winner behavior, two- and three-way tie behavior, exact ABI visibility,
mutation isolation, and rejection of the placeholder verifier.

## Fresh successor encounter

The fresh successor receives the exact bounded subject position and complete
public repository. The generic prompt asks it to continue the subject's current
opening through world contact, leave a reusable verifier, preserve the policy,
author the next substantive opening, run useful checks, and inspect the exact
diff. It receives ordinary broad tools in an isolated workspace.

It may edit exactly:

- `verify_coverage.py`; and
- `successor-opening.json`.

The verifier ABI is actor-visible and requires one function that accepts a
context and option list, calls the immutable coverage policy, computes realized
scores, and returns the selected id, sorted ids sharing the maximum realized
score, and whether the retained tie rule selected the greatest id among those
maxima. The exact JSON-compatible return keys and types are stated in the
source contract.

The successor opening uses OT-0089's five substantive fields and contains no
actor-authored lifecycle status. It must be non-placeholder, grounded in the
observed contact, and have `next_opening` distinct from the inherited tie
opening. Bind exact verifier source and opening before hidden execution.

## Admission and promotion

Hidden admission requires:

1. exact changed paths and truthful report;
2. clean complete tool trace and contained external effects;
3. unchanged coverage-policy digest;
4. verifier compilation and public-floor success;
5. exact valid verifier reports on every hidden case;
6. unchanged policy choices matching the realized-score oracle, including the
   frozen tie rule; and
7. a valid distinct substantive successor opening.

Only after all gates pass may the harness add the verifier as a retained
environmental capability, append the sealed confirmation receipt and
actor-authored opening, derive canonical `continuation.status = open`, and
seal the exact successor. Its continuation must equal the actor's newly bound
`next_opening`, not the inherited tie opening or a harness fallback.

## Cheapest falsifier and stopping

Reject if the actor changes the policy, fails the visible verifier ABI, cannot
generalize to any hidden tie or near-tie, authors no distinct valid opening, or
the exact promoted subject cannot reopen sounding with that opening. Stop at
the first material failure, after one promoted successor, at actor-budget
exhaustion, or after 45 minutes. No replacement actor is allowed.

Observer stopping does not close a promoted subject. A correct result may end
this experiment while leaving the exact subject open for the next encounter.

## Frozen prediction

The fresh successor will preserve the admitted coverage policy, implement a
hidden-valid generalized verifier, resolve the tie uncertainty through
confirming contact, and author a distinct actionable next opening. The exact
subject will reopen sounding with that new opening and one additional retained
verification capability.

## Researcher-causation inventory

- **Researcher-owned:** seed verifier ABI, public and hidden cases, isolation,
  trace audit, admission, canonical lifecycle derivation, and observation
  stopping.
- **Subject-owned:** current tie pursuit, verifier implementation, contact
  interpretation, surrender content, and next opening.
- **Removed here:** a researcher-chosen policy repair and the assumption that
  only contradiction or mutation can justify another turn.

Passing would establish one bounded confirmation-to-renewal transition. It
would not establish indefinite recurrence, causal advantage over erased
pursuit, subject ownership of scheduling or admission, or the usefulness of
the next opening until later world contact enacts it.
