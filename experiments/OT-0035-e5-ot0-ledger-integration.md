## Hypothesis and complete causal path

OT-0033 learned a task-specific weight relation and corrected it twice, but its
downstream predictor was a public deterministic fixture. OT-0035 places that
same generic learner directly before the promoted OT-0014 substrate and the
same fresh hosted-model boundary:

```text
current selector snapshot
→ one-of-two choice for each completed raw observation
→ independently released observation outcome
→ task-specific weight update
→ revised selector chooses the eighty observations that remain available
→ OT-0014 discrepancy-gated version ledger observes only those choices
→ exact 96-byte ledger projection crosses a complete context reset
→ fresh receipted Luna instance predicts structural holdouts
→ independently sealed held-out error
```

The final task seed is derived mechanically from the future clean implementation
commit. It fixes a selector criterion and an eligible parity rule; the later
regime uses the rule and criterion complements, and the third uses fresh
contacts under the original pair. Candidate code, prompts, and workspaces do
not receive the seed, criteria, rules, future sequence, or controls.

## Branches and attribution

For each regime, the candidate and unchanged branches copy the exact same
parent selector and ledger. The candidate learns from the completed receipt,
reselects the completed raw observations, and commits them to its ledger. The
unchanged branch applies the parent selector to the same observations and
updates an otherwise identical ledger. Credit-neutralized replay must preserve
the exact parent snapshot.

Nine prewritten equal-budget fixed lineages—zero and both signs of every unit
axis—carry their own copies of the same OT-0 ledger through all regimes. A
stronger prospective control learns the first selector, then freezes that
snapshot and its ledger through both later regimes. A final candidate
projection ablation supplies no ledger state while preserving the final canary.

The hosted application is stochastic, so two complete workers repeat every
branch under one directly receipted deployment epoch. Corresponding candidate
and unchanged predictions must match exactly across workers. Every turn uses a
fresh ephemeral thread and fresh workspace. The twelve branch orders are
rotated and reversed so every condition has mean serial position 5.5.

Invalidate on any task, lock, freshness, model, catalog, ETag, response,
inventory, parse, tool, counterbalance, resource, evidence, test, or privacy
failure. Otherwise reject unless both workers independently obtain zero
candidate errors in all three eight-query canaries; unchanged selection loses
at least three initially and eight in both later regimes; every fixed lineage

Promotion is limited to private, time-bounded, single-domain OT-1 evidence. It

unqualified public reproduction. The one E5 authorization is consumed at first
