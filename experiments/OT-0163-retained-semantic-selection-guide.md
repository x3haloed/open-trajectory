## Frozen artifact

Give one fresh guide author the exact subject position, four candidate
mechanisms, all twenty-eight now-open selection cases, and the sealed OT-0160,
OT-0161, and OT-0162 consequences. It may change only `selection-guide.md`.
Require a nonempty guide of at most 5,000 UTF-8 bytes, a one-file clean complete
audit, and truthful output. The guide may preserve lineage-specific semantic
knowledge; it may not see the new portfolio.

Bind the exact guide bytes and consequence ancestry before materializing ten
new selection requests: the exact carried stake, four unseen composed-extension
paraphrases, and five ordinary hard negatives. Each request varies candidate
order and omits its expected answer from actor-visible files.

For every request run a matched pair of isolated fresh selection actors:

- active receives the exact bound guide;
- erased receives no guide;
- both receive the same subject position, request, candidates, tools, response
  contract, and instruction to satisfy the full substantive stake;
- each may change only `choice.json` and must return one presented id.

Alternate active-first and erased-first order across pairs. Bind all twenty
audited choices before scoring any expected answer.

## Promotion

Require every guide and selection actor to pass complete trace and effects
audit. Active must score 10/10, including all five dependency cases and all five
ordinary hard negatives. Erased must score at most 8/10 and active advantage
must be at least two cases.

Install the exact guide binding as the active developmental mechanism selector,
append semantic-guide and selection-capability receipts, retain its failed
lexical parent as correction ancestry, and preserve the exact active stake,
continuation, and every unauthorized parent field.

Passing would establish one causal artifact effect on fresh model-mediated
semantic selection and operational routing of the current stake. It would not
yet establish exact later reuse under a new scheduler encounter, revision after
later semantic-guide harm, or selection among newly invented mechanisms.

`selection-guide.md`. The exact 2,745-byte guide distinguishes ordinary direct
routes from dependency on admitted/corrected composed machinery plus its earned

`semantic-choice-01-active` as a seed parent, while the actor runtime owns that
same label as its evidence directory and raised `FileExistsError`. No choice was
generated or scored, no hidden comparison opened, and the subject is unchanged.

This is an apparatus staging-namespace failure, not semantic-guide evidence.
Preserve the exact guide and audit. The next record should reconstruct its
binding without another guide actor, stage choice seeds under disjoint names,
