# OT-0130 — route-only recurrent subject

- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** cleanly reproduce three recurrent subject-selected contacts from
  one actor-authored developmental representation
- **Claim scopes sought:** bounded recurrent `operational-transition` and
  bounded recurrent `causal-observation`; no model-specific reproduction claim
- **Parent:** exact OT-0124 open subject
- **Actor budget:** exactly three fresh route actors
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 100 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

Across OT-0128 and OT-0129, fresh actors reliably interpret consequence,
ground remaining uncertainty, choose a route, and cite exact cases. Reliability
fails only when they independently restate that route into action or opening
representations. If the grounded route is the sole actor-authored developmental
state and the substrate compiles every downstream continuation representation,
three fresh cycles can recur without loss or intervention.

## Route-only substrate

Each fresh actor may edit only `route-assimilation.json`. It must author exact
case citations, consequence summary, remaining uncertainty, route,
selection-rule disposition, and surrender condition while preserving the exact
selector. After the route passes and binds, compiler `ot-0130-v1` derives:

- a `registry-extension` action;
- a unique target `continuation-<cycle>-<first 12 hex characters of the SHA-256
  of remaining_uncertainty>`;
- expected information equal to exact `remaining_uncertainty`;
- rationale from route plus selected contact and consequence summary;
- action surrender equal to route surrender; and
- the exact OT-0129 canonical opening projection from route and compiled action.

The compiler must be deterministic, its target must be new and structurally
valid, and the exact uncertainty must appear unchanged in action expected
information, opening continuation, next opening, and unresolved.

## Clean recurrence

Restart from exact OT-0124. Use the exact three frozen nodes, worlds, selector
controls, continuation-owned scheduler, and contextual-transfer equivalence.
At each node bind selection before consequence, reveal only the selected branch,
authorize one fresh route actor, bind the exact accepted route, compile and
validate action/opening, promote the sounding child, and select the next node
only from the compiled authoritative opening.

Stop at the first failure and authorize no replacement or later actor. The
target passes only if exactly three fresh routes and three promotions occur,
all corrected selections equal their sealed oracles, all exact pre-correction
controls choose their decoys, and the final subject remains sounding and open
on contextual transfer.

Passing would establish one clean three-cycle reproduction where a compact
artifact makes a single grounded developmental judgment persist causally across
fresh actors and turns. It would not establish open-ended scheduling, arbitrary
world generation, subject-authored compiler evolution, or indefinite operation.
