# OT-0204 — subject-authored pursuit selector

- **Status:** design frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** recurrent renewal that improves the subject's capacity to select
  future pursuits rather than merely adding another object-level target
- **Parent:** exact OT-0203 open successor `cd611836...`
- **Actor budget:** one selector-renewal actor, six active portfolio authors,
  and six matched selector-erased controls
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 65 minutes

## Frozen renewal authority

One fresh actor receives the exact completed subject and completion receipt. It
must assimilate completion and author:

- one new scalar world-measured dimension over candidate pursuits;
- the dimension's meaning and whether higher or lower is preferable;
- fixed missing, blocked, and tie policies;
- one representative portfolio of four to six complete candidate stakes; and
- the selection predicted by its executable selector.

The observer supplies only the generic scalar comparison carrier. It does not
choose the dimension, its meaning, direction, candidate pursuits, measurements,
or selected stake. Old object-level source and target names are forbidden as
dimension names. The selector excludes blocked pursuits, rejects missing
measurement, and preserves every tied extreme.

## Matched downstream test

Compile a candidate subject carrying the selector renewal. Six active portfolio
actors receive its populated selector binding; six interleaved controls receive
an exact shape-preserving erased binding. Both branches author novel portfolios
of complete candidate stakes and report their predicted selection. The public
checker enforces structural validity; hidden evaluation applies the renewal
actor's selector and an affine score transformation that preserves ordering but
changes every magnitude.

## Frozen promotion gate

Require all thirteen audits, valid completion assimilation, active exact
selection 6/6, controls at most 2/6, advantage at least four, hidden affine
stability, route 16/16, identity 18/18, canonical selected active stake, and an
open successor. On passage install the selector capability and active-01's
selected pursuit. On failure retain exact parent.

Passing establishes one bounded recurrence where the subject changes how later
moves are selected. It does not establish that the chosen dimension predicts
real utility; the next experiment must expose its selections to independent
world consequence and permit correction or surrender.
