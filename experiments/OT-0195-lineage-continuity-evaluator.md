# OT-0195 — lineage continuity evaluator

- **Status:** design frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** validate pursuit continuity as exact retention or
  consequence-grounded revision of the live lineage, without rewarding stale
  repetition
- **Parent:** exact OT-0190 open successor plus retained OT-0191–OT-0194
  candidate evidence
- **Actor budget:** six active continuation authors and six matched controls
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Frozen evaluator

`E0195` counts a structurally valid, audited, novel, targeted, public- and
hidden-discriminating continuation when either:

1. its next stake exactly equals the stake installed by the direct transition;
   or
2. its action is `revise`, its next stake differs from the actor's actual
   current stake, and it preserves the transitioned stake's property.

The second branch permits a changed question, success condition, and mechanism
hypothesis. Hidden consequence may later confirm or kill that hypothesis. A
control that retains its older current stake satisfies neither branch.

`E0194` exact-stake scores are retained on the same fresh outputs for comparison
but have no promotion authority. OT-0194 outputs are construction evidence only:
the new rule replays 6/6 active versus 1/6 control there, but no OT-0194 subject
is retrospectively promoted.

## Candidate and comparison

Reconstruct OT-0194's corrected direct subject exactly: actor-authored pursuit
predicates and evaluation receipt install the latent-contact stake, with no
global future-mechanism authority. Six active actors receive that subject and
the evaluated receipt. Six interleaved controls receive exact parent and an
erased receipt. Both branches receive identical raw reopening, consequence,
predicates, and candidate mechanisms. Actors author next contact and do not vote
on the settled transition.

## Frozen promotion gate

Require `E0195` active 6/6, control at most 2/6, advantage at least four, all
twelve complete audits, deterministic corrected transition, route 16/16,
identity 18/18, exact retained rejection chain, and an open successor.

On passage, advance the evaluation epoch to `E0195`, install the corrected
direct subject, and retain active-01 reopening plus its sealed consequence. On
failure, preserve exact parent and all raw outputs.

Passing establishes one direct pursuit transition whose unresolved lineage
causes fresh successor contact without requiring verbatim stake repetition.
It does not establish a second update; the promoted sealed consequence must be
resolved next under fixed machinery.
