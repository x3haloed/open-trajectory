# OT-0131 — subject-originated contact beyond the closed graph

- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** originate, bind, consequence, and retain one useful contact outside
  the OT-0130 three-node world graph
- **Claim scopes sought:** bounded `operational-transition` and bounded
  pursuit-conditioned `causal-observation`
- **Parent:** exact OT-0130 open subject
- **Actor budget:** one fresh contact author, one fresh consequence router, and
  one post-seal matched contact-author control
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

The OT-0130 subject's carried opening can orient a fresh actor inside a complete
but unenumerated environment strongly enough to originate a decisive new world
contact. Binding that actor-authored contact before independent consequence and
returning the receipt through the unchanged route-only carrier will produce one
exact open successor without an observer choosing the contact surface or
repairing the transition.

The narrower causal prediction is that opening content shapes which valid
contact is authored. After the operational successor is sealed, an otherwise
matched fresh control carrying a recovery-latency opening will target recovery
latency rather than the active subject's demand-volatility transfer surface.

## Complete contact environment

Each contact actor receives a fresh repository containing a complete
`capacity/policy.py`, two passing single-context public observations, and no
TODO, failing public test, proposal catalog, preferred function, hidden oracle,
or hidden score. Both complete policy functions accept a mixed event history
and a requested context. Their present implementation uses the whole history.

The actor may inspect and execute the repository, but may edit only
`contact-proposal.json`. It authors exactly three bounded cases against exactly
one real function. Every case supplies a requested context and a mixed event
history; at least two are intended adversarial interleavings and at least one is
a same-behavior control. The harness derives contact identity from the exact
proposal. The actor does not author a trusted surface label or select from a
researcher catalog.

The sealed world independently compares installed behavior with a
context-local reference for the selected function. A contact is decisive when
all cases execute, at least two distinguish installed from reference behavior,
and at least one confirms equal behavior. The exact proposal binds before any
reference output is revealed.

## Consequence and continuation

Only after a decisive active contact, expose its complete receipt to one fresh
route actor. Keep the OT-0130 selector immutable and retain its route-only ABI:
the actor authors consequence summary, exact case citations, disposition,
remaining uncertainty, and surrender condition once. Fixed compiler
`ot-0130-v1` derives action and canonical opening as cycle four.

Promote only if the route is `extend`, every case is cited exactly, the selector
bytes remain exact, the accepted uncertainty is new, compilation is
deterministic, and the resulting subject is sounding and open. No replacement
actor, proposal repair, hidden resampling, or between-stage contract change is
allowed.

## Matched post-seal control

After and only after sealing an operational successor, run one fresh contact
author in a fresh copy of the same repository with equal tools, contract,
budget, and subject state except that the inherited opening asks whether
recovery latency responds to regime shift. Bind and score the control proposal
identically. It cannot mutate, delay, or veto the sealed lineage.

The pursuit-conditioned causal prediction passes only if both contacts are
decisive, the active proposal targets `reserve_for_context`, and the matched
control targets `recovery_window`. Operational promotion does not depend on
that stronger comparison.

## Gates and stopping

Reject before consequence if the active proposal or trace is invalid. Reject
before promotion if the active contact is not decisive or route assimilation
fails. Stop immediately and authorize no later actor after an operational-path
failure. Stop after the post-seal control otherwise. Preserve the exact last
valid subject open.

Passing operationally would establish one bounded continuation in which the
subject's inherited developmental position leads to actor-originated contact
outside the fixed OT-0130 graph and returns through the compact carrier. It
would not establish open-domain search, arbitrary-world validity, contact
frequency, indefinite operation, subject-authored compiler evolution, or
subject ownership of world and admission authority.
