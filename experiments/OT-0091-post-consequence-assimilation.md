- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** OT-1C consequence-driven pursuit revision
- **Claim scopes sought:** bounded `operational-transition` and bounded
  `causal-observation`; no model-specific generative-reproduction claim
- **Parent:** exact OT-0090 open subject
- **Actor budget:** one active assimilator and one post-seal
  consequence-content-erased control
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

OT-0090's next opening was authored before hidden consequence and became
partly stale when that consequence verified the very multi-way and order
behavior it asks to test. A fresh actor that receives the exact subject and
sealed consequence can retire the satisfied uncertainty, preserve what remains
unresolved, and author a genuinely later opening. A matched actor without the
consequence content will not have the same basis for retirement.

The mechanism under test is post-consequence assimilation, not another policy
repair or target-selection contest.

## Active and consequence-content-erased projections

Derive both projections mechanically from the same exact parent and receipt.
The active projection contains complete receipt content.

The erased projection removes public and hidden outcome rows, validity and
admission values, and any textual summary that entails them. It retains the

This control does not own, mutate, or veto the active lineage. It opens only

## Fresh assimilation encounters

Each fresh actor receives one projection in an isolated workspace with ordinary
broad tools. There is no new policy world and no editable capability code. The
identical generic prompt asks it to assimilate available consequence into the
current pursuit, distinguish settled from unresolved stakes, write one bounded
assimilation record, author the substantive successor opening that should now
be carried, run local validation, and inspect the exact diff.

Each actor may edit exactly:

- `assimilation.json`; and
- `successor-opening.json`.

The visible assimilation contract has exact fields:

- `disposition`: one of `retain`, `revise`, or `retire`;
- `settled_case_ids`: a list of actor-cited receipt case ids;
- `settled_stake`: what consequence settled;
- `remaining_uncertainty`: what still matters after that contact;
- `receipt_use`: how the available receipt changes or fails to change pursuit;
  and
- `surrender_condition`: what later evidence would reverse the assimilation.

The successor opening uses the five substantive fields from OT-0089 and has no
actor-authored lifecycle status. `retain` permits carrying the same opening;
`revise` or `retire` requires a distinct `next_opening`. No prompt or contract
supplies the next opening's content.

Bind each exact assimilation and opening before scoring. Self-report alone is
not evidence that a receipt supports it; scoring uses the sealed receipt and
literal case identities.

## Operational admission and promotion

The active successor passes only if:

1. tool trace, contained effects, exact changed paths, and report are
   conformant;
2. both artifacts have exact visible fields and no placeholders;
3. disposition is `revise` or `retire`, not `retain`;
4. `settled_case_ids` includes `hidden-three-way` and at least one hidden
   reordered or tie case actually present in the receipt;
5. the settled stake addresses multi-way or reordered tie verification;
6. the remaining uncertainty is nonempty and does not claim the cited receipt
   failed; and
7. the successor `next_opening` differs from the stale parent opening.

After binding, the harness verifies every cited id and claimed successful
receipt fact against the sealed OT-0090 rows. Only then may it append an
assimilation receipt, append the actor-authored opening, derive canonical
`open`, and seal the exact successor. Its continuation must equal that opening.

## Frozen causal comparison

After active promotion, run the erased actor. The bounded causal prediction is
that the active actor validly revises or retires the stale opening using actual
hidden case ids, while the erased actor retains it or fails evidence-grounded
assimilation because the decisive outcome content is absent.

The causal observation passes only if the active operational gate passes and
the erased result does not independently satisfy the same receipt-grounded
revision gate. A valid erased `retain` is acceptable. Any erased revision based
on invented outcome values fails its own grounding audit rather than counting
as evidence against the active subject.

No semantic quality score, researcher preference over the new opening, or
post-result reinterpretation enters admission. The usefulness of the revised
opening remains for the next world encounter.

## Cheapest falsifiers and stopping

Reject operational assimilation if the active actor retains the stale opening,
cannot ground retirement in the sealed rows, loses all remaining uncertainty,
or fails to produce an exact sounding successor. If active promotion fails,
stop before the control. If it passes, seal first, run exactly one erased
control, record the causal disposition, and stop. No replacement actors.

Observer stopping does not close the promoted subject.

## Frozen predictions

- The active actor cites the hidden three-way result, revises or retires the
  stale tie-verification opening, and authors a distinct next opening.
- The exact subject reopens sounding with that revised opening.
- The erased actor lacks evidence for the same grounded retirement and either
  retains the opening or fails the receipt-grounding gate.

## Researcher-causation inventory

- **Researcher-owned:** receipt projection and erasure, artifact schemas,
  case-identity grounding, isolation, trace audit, promotion, control timing,
  and observation stopping.
- **Subject-owned:** consequence interpretation, pursuit disposition, retained
  uncertainty, surrender condition, and next opening.
- **Removed here:** an observer-authored repair for the stale pursuit and the
  assumption that a pre-consequence opening remains current after consequence.

Passing would establish one bounded case where sealed world consequence changes
what the subject carries next across a fresh actor boundary. It would not
establish general assimilation reliability, semantic optimality of the new
pursuit, indefinite recurrence, or subject ownership of admission and
scheduling.
