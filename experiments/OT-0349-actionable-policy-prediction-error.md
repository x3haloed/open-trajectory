# OT-0349 — Actionable policy prediction error

- **Status:** frozen — awaiting live actors
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Operational parent:** exact OT-0347 child `708ea5a3...`; OT-0348 made no
  successor
- **Fresh actors:** at most four: discrepancy-bearing corrector, matched
  linkage-erased corrector, held-out selector, and selected-contact actor

## Hypothesis and cheapest falsifier

OT-0348 showed that complete raw delayed consequence beside an inherited policy
did not make its proxy failure actionable. The hypothesis is that the missing
substrate is a subject-held prediction error: an exact causal link between the
policy's declared continuation objective, the proxy it optimized, the selected
world's realized reopening, and an admissible counterexample. Adding only that
linkage should cause a fresh actor to revise the executable policy; the exact
revision should beat its unchanged parent on a disjoint post-binding catalog
and yield a mechanically reopenable downstream frontier.

The cheapest falsifier is active retention, an equivalent operative change
under linkage erasure, a revision that does not improve the completed training
consequence, failure on the post-binding held-out catalog, exact-machinery
erasure not restoring the old choice, hidden contact failure, floor regression,
or a successor that needs private observer state to reopen.

## Frozen substrate intervention

Reuse OT-0348's exact private training derivation and observer-owned consequence
without resampling. A deterministic compiler consumes only the exact incumbent
source, its actor-authored policy rationale, and those public consequence rows.
It records:

- the policy source and consequence identities;
- the policy's declared continuation objective and operative immediate-count
  proxy;
- the selected world's proxy value and verified downstream yield;
- the best admissible counterexample's corresponding values;
- the violated monotonic relation and unresolved correction operation.

The active actor receives this record inside `subject-position.json` plus the
unchanged raw consequence. The matched actor receives the same subject, raw
consequence, tools, ABI, and prompt, but `active_prediction_error` is `null`.
Thus no outcome information is withheld from the control; only the derived
policy-to-outcome linkage is ablated. Both may revise or retain and edit only
`policy.py` and `decision.json`. The checker enforces deterministic,
order-invariant, id-generic, floor-preserving execution without encoding a
desired metric or choice.

If and only if the active fresh actor cleanly changes the policy and improves
verified training yield, bind its exact source. Only afterward create one new
private seed. The disjoint catalog uses fresh ids, order, cutoffs, immediate
counts, and downstream frontiers: six-to-zero, four-to-one, and two-to-four.
Neither corrector can see it before binding.

The held-out selector runs the bound source and chooses only a current contact
inside the selected world. A separate fresh contact actor repairs that contact
against public cases; independent hidden consequence then releases the selected
world's complete downstream public frontier into the subject. Broad tools,
fresh workspaces, and G13/G11 auditing remain unchanged.

## Controls, score, and promotion

Primary score is verified downstream contact count after one equivalent current
contact. Changed and exact unchanged sources receive identical held-out rows.
Exact machinery erasure replaces only the bound source with the incumbent.
The linkage-erased actor determines whether raw consequence plus the generic
correction setting produces an equivalent revision. Inadmissible high-yield
decoys remain blocked.

Operational promotion requires clean active correction, binding before hidden
derivation, clean selector and contact actors, 3/3 public and 5/5 hidden contact
success, exact 40/40 floor preservation, and subject-only materialization of the
new frontier. The bounded substrate-causal and machinery-refinement claim also
requires changed held-out yield strictly above unchanged, different world
selection, machinery erasure restoring the unchanged choice, and no equivalent
operative revision in the linkage-erased branch.

This tests one researcher-designed seed mechanism for making contradiction
actionable. It does not establish generation reliability, cross-domain fitness,
autonomous discrepancy-language invention, or full OT-2R. A later harmful
regime must test whether the learned delayed-yield rule can itself be corrected.

## Budgets and stopping

At most four fresh actors, no retries, no post-output repair, one exact retained
training derivation, one post-binding held-out derivation, and forty observer
minutes. Stop after deterministic controls and subject disposition are sealed.
Observation completion does not close an open subject.

## Result

The active fresh actor treated the subject-held prediction error as a
falsification of the incumbent proxy. It changed the exact policy from immediate
viable-contact count to independently verified reopened-contact count, passed
the generic checker with a clean three-command G13/G11 trace, and improved the
retained training consequence from zero to three downstream openings. Its
source was bound before one new private held-out seed was generated.

On fresh ids, order, cutoffs, and counts, the bound program selected the
two-current/four-downstream world. Exact machinery erasure selected the
six-current/zero-downstream world. A fresh selector enacted the bound choice;
a fourth fresh actor repaired the chosen contact and passed 3/3 public plus 5/5
hidden cases. Exact 40/40 floor state remained unchanged. Candidate subject
`d80ac19e...` contains the four public downstream contact specifications and
materializes its active opening without private state.

The stronger substrate-causal claim fails. The linkage-erased actor, which
still received the raw outcome rows and was externally placed into the same
correction task, independently authored an operative delayed-opening policy.
Its source differs in tie-breaking and outcome qualification, but chooses the
same worlds as the active source on all six frozen equivalence anchors. Thus
the active prediction-error record did not cause discovery once the observer
had already chosen to open a correction actor.

Aggregate `01b8deab...` is conditional operational evidence, not a promoted
prediction-error mechanism claim. A protocol-order limitation also remains:
the linkage-erased actor ran before the active binding was sealed, although its
output was isolated and not consulted by binding or held-out generation. A
prospective exact reconstruction must settle the operational child without
resampling. The next causal test should give the same fixed driver active and
linkage-erased subject states: only the active prediction error should derive
`resolve-selection-prediction-error`; the erased branch should follow its
unchanged policy-reuse operation. That tests whether the artifact causes the
correction turn to happen, rather than whether it changes an already assigned
correction task.
