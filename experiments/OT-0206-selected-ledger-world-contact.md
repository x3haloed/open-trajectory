## Frozen authority and order

**Hypothesis:** the exact subject-selected stake induces a reusable ledger
program that preserves independently authored observed-unblocked contact and
revoked-identity correction across hidden cases and fresh replay actors.

**Cheapest falsifier:** one accepted suite, program, or replay omits a usable
observed contact, admits a blocked or stale-prediction contact, mislabels an
identity, changes order, or fails exact replay. Stop at the frozen actor budget
and retain the parent.

Four fresh world actors first author mixed active/revoked contact suites. Each
suite has eight ordered cases; both its public and sealed halves contain active
and revoked identities, stale prediction conflicts, blocked outcomes, and at
least one usable observed contact. Their accepted outputs are sealed before any
ledger actor runs.

Four later ledger actors receive the exact subject-selected stake and only the
public half of every suite. Each may author one generic declarative ledger
program choosing ordering, contact source, blocking, prediction, and identity
policies. The evaluator then executes it on all 32 sealed cases. No ledger actor
can read hidden contact or evaluator authority.

Five deterministic negative controls are frozen before actors: prediction-copy,
options-copy, no blocked-contact exclusion, reversed order, and marking every
identity corrected. Each must fail both the fixture and accepted world bank.
These controls validate the evaluator's discriminating surface; they do not
replace a matched selector comparison, which is outside this experiment's
operational completion claim.

If all four programs pass, install the first exact program and expose the full
sealed bank plus that program to two fresh replay actors. Each replay actor must
produce the exact same ordered ledger using the executable carrier in its own
fresh workspace.

## Frozen consequence and promotion gate

The selected portfolio assigned this pursuit eight units of
`decision-ready-signal`. Before output, this experiment binds that value to
eight independently observable checkpoints:

1. four independent contact suites seal cleanly;
2. four ledger programs and audits are structurally valid;
3. every public observed-unblocked contact is preserved;
4. every hidden observed-unblocked contact is preserved;
5. no blocked contact enters the ledger;
6. every revoked identity receives the correction marker and no active identity
   does;
7. every ledger entry preserves the sealed sequence order; and
8. two fresh replay actors reproduce the exact complete ledger.

Promotion requires all eight checkpoints, all ten audits, exact parent/stake/
selector identity, four-of-four hidden program generalization, replay identity,
all five negative controls failing, route 16/16, identity 18/18, and an
identity-conformant open successor. Failure retains the exact OT-0205 parent and
localizes the first failed checkpoint. Actor order, the eight Boolean scoring
rule, and the all-of-gates promotion decision are fixed here before unsealing.

A pass establishes successful independent consequence for the selected
pursuit, not that eight is globally calibrated or that this selector beats a
different selector on utility. A later encounter must still compare predictive
value or provide contradiction capable of correcting or surrendering the
selector.

## Result

All four independent contact actors produce individually valid suites with
clean complete traces and accepted audits. The run nevertheless rejects at the
first checkpoint before any ledger actor is authorized. Contact actors one and
four independently choose the same valid local `suite_id`,
`preserved-contact-ledger-01`; the harness's unpublished global-uniqueness
condition rejects their composition.

This is apparatus invalidity, not evidence against the selected pursuit. An
isolated actor cannot coordinate a globally unique label with actors whose
outputs it cannot see, and neither the actor-visible checker nor schema assigns
global naming authority. Exact OT-0205 parent `3e06b644...` remains open.

Prospectively namespace each already-valid suite by its sealed encounter index
when composing the world bank. Preserve actor-authored local IDs inside the
namespaced record, rerun fresh actors under a new experiment ID, and change no
ledger semantics, checkpoints, negative controls, or promotion gates.
