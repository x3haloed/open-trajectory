# OT-0200 — subject-authored target-language expansion

- **Status:** design frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** post-capability pursuit renewal without an observer-selected target
- **Parent:** exact OT-0199 open successor `08c877ff...`
- **Actor budget:** one renewal actor, six active contact authors, and six
  matched renewal-erased controls
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis and cheapest falsifier

After direct success completes the inherited `latent-unblocked` pursuit, a
fresh actor can assimilate that completion and author a genuinely new,
executable pursuit. Because the old set vocabulary is exhausted, the actor may
name one new set-valued world source, define its meaning, compose it with the
existing set operations, and bind the result to a contact contract.

The cheapest falsifier is failure to produce one valid nonrepeating renewal, or
failure of the resulting binding to align fresh contact authors substantially
better than a binding-erased control. No observer supplies a next property,
source name, target expression, or semantic direction.

## Frozen renewal interface

The renewal actor receives the exact promoted subject, completed pursuit,
installed mechanism, direct success receipt, the three earned target
signatures, and the bounded expression grammar. It edits one artifact carrying:

- completion assimilation and exact receipt identity;
- one next stake with success and surrender conditions;
- one new set-valued source name and world meaning;
- one target expression using that source;
- one matching contact contract; and
- four to six representative cases.

Every representative target must be nonempty, be a subset of options, exclude
blocked members, and differ from observed-unblocked, latent-unblocked, and
their viable union. The new source must be used by the expression and vary
across cases. Prediction tokens must equal the independently executed target.
These conditions prevent renaming an earned pursuit while leaving the content
of the new distinction to the actor.

## Matched downstream test

Compile a candidate subject carrying the renewal. Six fresh active actors
receive its populated binding; six interleaved controls receive the same exact
completed parent and a shape-preserving erased binding. Both branches receive
the same generic contact carrier and checker. Each authors four to six novel
structural cases. The public checker enforces carrier validity only; hidden
evaluation applies the renewal actor's source and expression.

An aligned contact uses the selected source identity, makes prediction equal
the executed target in every case, and preserves the same nonrepetition and
safety conditions. Token-renamed hidden cases must preserve alignment.

## Frozen scoring and promotion

Require the renewal actor and all twelve contact actors to pass complete audit,
active alignment 6/6, control alignment at most 2/6, advantage at least four,
representative and hidden execution checks, exact completion receipt, installed
route 16/16, identity 18/18, and an open exact successor.

On passage, install the post-completion assimilation, target-language
extension, active-01 contact and consequence, and a direct opening to invent a
mechanism for the subject-authored target. On failure, retain exact parent.

Passing establishes one bounded subject-authored expansion of what the lineage
can make contactable. It does not establish that the new distinction is useful
outside this set world, that a mechanism exists, or that renewal recurs.
