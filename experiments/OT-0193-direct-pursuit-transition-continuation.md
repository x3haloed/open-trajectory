# OT-0193 — direct pursuit transition and continuation

- **Status:** design frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** let an evaluated subject-authored pursuit predicate change the
  subject directly, then test whether fresh actors continue from that changed
  position without voting on the settled operation
- **Parent:** exact OT-0190 open successor plus retained OT-0191 and OT-0192
  candidate artifacts
- **Actor budget:** six active continuation authors and six matched controls
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Prediction error under test

OT-0192 expected executable pursuit consequence to determine the next
operation. It instead gave successors the predicate projection, raw consequence,
and only the digest of the evaluated result, then asked them to derive the
operation again. One of six active actors selected the opposite branch.

The smallest uncertain assumption is whether a fresh actor belongs between an
already-evaluated subject predicate and its authoritative transition.

## Frozen direct transition

Reconstruct the accepted OT-0192 predicate binding and deterministic evaluation
from retained artifacts. Conformance must reproduce the exact
`retain-and-advance` operation, surviving
`observed-unblocked-contact-corrector`, nonoverlap, source subject, and receipt
digests.

Without an actor vote, seal a direct child that carries:

- the actor-authored executable pursuit predicates;
- their exact evaluated consequence receipt;
- a direct transition receipt naming the operation and surviving mechanism;
- the OT-0191 candidate stake as the active developmental stake; and
- its question as the open continuation.

This is a candidate transition, not a promoted subject until the complete gate
passes.

## Matched continuation comparison

Six active actors receive the directly transitioned subject. Six interleaved
controls receive the exact parent. Both branches receive identical raw OT-0191
reopening, consequence, candidate mechanisms, and OT-0192 predicates. Active
actors receive the evaluated transition receipt; controls receive an exact
erasure with no operation authority.

Actors do not select the settled operation. Each authors one next reopening:
a stake, 4–6 concrete contact cases, and a routing hypothesis. A continuation
counts when it:

- keeps the installed latent-contact stake;
- predicts the installed surviving mechanism;
- authors contact distinct from OT-0191;
- gives every case an observed blocked contact and a still-unobserved unblocked
  option; and
- discriminates candidate mechanisms on both public and sealed hidden contact.

The predicted mechanism may pass or fail hidden contact. Falsification is a
valid continuation; the experiment measures whether the lineage produces a
real next encounter, not whether it protects a claim.

## Frozen promotion gate

Require active continuation 6/6, control at most 2/6, advantage at least four,
all twelve complete actor audits, deterministic direct-transition replay,
installed route floor 16/16, identity 18/18, exact retained history, and an open
successor. On passage, promote the direct child plus active-01 reopening and its
sealed consequence. On any failure, retain exact parent `8f29e6a8...` and all
negative evidence.

Passing establishes one subject-authored pursuit consequence installed and
used to originate later world contact without successor reinterpretation. It
does not establish a second consequence-driven update, repeated recurrence, or
open predicate-language expansion.
