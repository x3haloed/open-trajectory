# OT-0023 — Actor-authored contrast-portfolio feasibility

- **Status:** failed at frozen decision-carrier gate; portfolio untested
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target authority:** none; development feasibility only
- **Predecessor:** OT-0022 failed single-challenger reproduction

## Hypothesis

OT-0022's failed actor over-selected positive discrepancy examples and erased
the contrast required by the deterministic predictor. A single free-form
challenger makes that one hypothesis the entire causal opportunity. A bounded
portfolio authored within one fresh encounter may preserve competing contrasts
long enough for the same actor's prospective rule to choose from independently
measured consequences.

This is not repeated actor sampling. One actor authors all three expression-
distinct alternatives and one rule before any comparison exists. The controller
evaluates each against the same current selector and contact, executes the rule,
and commits at most one. It supplies no strategy names, alternative templates,
ranking, semantic compiler, or choice.

## Cheapest falsifier, controls, and scoring

Before hosted output, reject the carrier if any known generic three-alternative
output cannot validate, replay, produce at least two distinct selection sets,
gain at least four errors on the fresh public split, choose a useful alternative
under true credit, choose current when all error credits are neutralized, and
commit the exact actor-authored identity. Reject any prompt leak of the sealed
split or any non-contiguous, tampered, or over-budget consequence ledger.

If structural checks pass, run exactly two fresh Luna encounters. Each must
author exactly three distinct bounded selector expressions and one bounded
prospective choice rule. At least two deterministic selection sets must result.
The chosen alternative must change selection and gain at least four errors over
the null current selector. Complete credit neutralization must change the choice
to current. Both encounters must pass parse, tool, freshness, exact model,
inventory, Response identity, ETag, collector, token, time, test, audit, and
evidence gates.

## Results and decision

OT-0023 failed during the first actor encounter, before any portfolio
comparison or commit. The actor returned the exact schema, three textually
distinct selector expressions, and a prospective decision expression with zero
tools. All three selectors passed the depth-8 carrier and deterministic
execution; the programs had three distinct identities and produced two distinct
selection sets on the sealed public split.

The decision expression was 388 bytes but contained 65 AST nodes against the
inherited 64-node limit. The controller rejected it exactly as frozen, did not
evaluate the portfolio, and did not release the second actor. All evidence,
deployment, and verification available before that rejection is preserved.

Final disposition: `failed`. This is a carrier-capacity failure, not a result
about portfolio usefulness. OT-0023 is not rescored or repaired. A separately
numbered experiment may prospectively give the three-way decision carrier a
128-node limit while preserving its 512-byte limit, portfolio size, prompt,
credit topology, +4 performance gate, and every authority boundary; it must use
a fresh public task and fresh actors.
