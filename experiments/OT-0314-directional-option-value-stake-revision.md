## Motivation and hypothesis

OT-0312 produced a downstream tie. OT-0313 correctly refused to infer a
selector repair from that nondirectional result. OT-0314 therefore supplies the
missing causal signal: three prospective selections where the exact incumbent
commits before independent consequence, and the unchosen alternative preserves
more executable future routes.

The active stake says branches and comparisons help preserve distinctions, with
compactness only a penalty. Its current byte coefficient nevertheless dominates
those positive terms. The hypothesis is that repeated exact failures will cause
a fresh actor to revise that bounded machinery, and that the revised stake will
beat its exact parent on disjoint private held-out catalogs. Erasing outcome
content from the same training contacts should remove the useful revision.

## Frozen world and order

After this implementation is committed and pushed, generate one private
32-byte seed in external evidence storage. The fixed generator derives opaque
world identities for three author/tune catalogs and five disjoint held-out
catalogs. Every catalog has one compact constant-route policy and one larger
conditional policy. Public descriptors are measured from their source ASTs.
The incumbent ranks first from those descriptors and binds its choice before a
separate outcome world executes both policies on sealed future contexts.
Option value is the number of distinct valid routes actually produced, not a
restatement of syntax counts. The outcome authority, not the actor, computes
and receipts it. The incumbent must choose the lower-option world in every
training row or the experiment stops before actors.

The consequence actor receives the exact active stake plus all three bound
selections and outcomes. The erased actor receives identical public catalogs
and selection receipts with option counts and preferred identities removed.
Neither receives held-out identities, seed, cases, scores, sibling output, or
evaluator instructions. Both use fresh isolated workspaces, broad tools, and
G10 effect audit. Only a populated `stake-revision.json` may change.

The actor may alter weights, minimum score gap, and rationale. Stake identity,
question, support/contradiction operations, anchors, and all other semantics are
immutable. The exact machine-readable contract and executable structural
checker are actor-visible.

## Frozen score and gates

Candidate, exact incumbent, and erased output are applied deterministically to
the same five held-out catalogs. A row passes only when the rule supports and
selects the unique maximum-option world. Operational admission requires a clean
changed candidate scoring 5/5, exact incumbent at most 1/5, valid ancestry,
three directional training errors, and all hard authority and identity floors.
Seal that exact child before opening the erased actor.

The machinery-refinement claim additionally requires the erased actor not to
produce any changed 5/5 stake. Wrong outcome authority, selection/outcome
misbinding, outcome-before-selection, hidden seed exposure, malformed stake,
immutable-field change, or a held-out tie rejects. One private seed, one actor
per branch, no repair, retry, reseed, or favorable endpoint selection.

## Limits fixed before output

A pass is one bounded weight-level OT-2 foothold in the existing
researcher-designed descriptor language. It does not establish repeated
self-revision, cross-language transfer, later correction, generation
reliability, or researcher-optional experiment invention. The current local
`expanded-select` opening remains intact while the revised world-selection
machinery waits for its next environment boundary.

## Result and protocol deviation

The substantive comparison passed. Three independently executed training
worlds each exposed the same directional error: the incumbent selected a
one-route compact policy over a four-route conditional policy. The fresh actor
changed only `source_bytes` from -1 to 0, explicitly citing all three outcomes,
and scored 5/5 on private held-out identities while the exact incumbent scored
0/5. The outcome-erased actor retained the incumbent byte-exactly and scored
0/5. Both traces are G10-clean. Candidate operational digest is `0a48ab16...`;
raw aggregate receipt is `945bd6b5...`.

Promotion is nevertheless invalidated at the frozen interface gate. The
actor-visible `check_revision.py` constructs its empty-dictionary default as a
set containing a dictionary and raises `TypeError: unhashable type: 'dict'`.
Both actors reported this failure and manually verified the fully visible
machine-readable contract. The harness's internal validator accepted the same
semantics, so the candidate bytes and held-out comparison remain useful exact
evidence, but preflight failed to exercise the actual seeded checker and the
declared executable-checker claim is false.

Do not resample either actor or regenerate the private world. The next record
should fix only that checker expression, run the repaired checker against both
exact retained workspaces, reconstruct the aggregate from the same seed and
actor bytes, and decide whether this interface defect was immaterial to the
bounded causal comparison. Until then exact parent `9a6f94ba...`, not the raw
aggregate's auto-labeled child, remains authoritative.
