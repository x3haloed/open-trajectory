# OT-0307 — Consequence-routed correction recurrence

- **Status:** promoted operational transition; actor-facing wording nonconformance disclosed
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0306 open subject `2ce0d208...`
- **Invocation:** up to four identical content-free openings
- **Actor budget:** up to three fresh correction actors; no resampling

## Hypothesis

One recurrent entry can follow the exact subject through correction, failed-
revision or surrender consequence, successful admission, and a separate
actor-free opportunity refresh without an observer choosing the target, phase,
or next response. The active consequence—not an inherited module patch—must
select what a later actor receives.

This tests a more general continuation mechanism than one-shot repair. It does
not retry OT-0305's rejected priority claim and does not claim that the current
stake caused the world or target choice.

## Frozen mechanism

Each invocation contains no instruction beyond reopening the exact subject.
Derivation permits only `outward-correct` or
`refresh-opportunity-projection`. Every correction encounter gets a fresh
external workspace and broad tools under unchanged G10 containment.

An initial actor receives the selected public surface and four public cases. If
an independently scored revision remains unresolved, the successor retains the
failed candidate and one canonical counterexample. If an actor truthfully
surrenders, the sealed world evaluates only the unchanged 2/6 policy; no
candidate or success authority exists, and one canonical mismatch becomes
non-authoritative developmental feedback. The next seed is routed by the exact
active receipt: failed revision exposes the failed candidate, while surrender
exposes the surrender receipt and no candidate. Each consequence can reveal at
most one of the two hidden input classes.

A 6/6 revision against unchanged 2/6 is admitted. Its exact successor must
derive refresh. A later identical, actor-free opening performs that refresh,
exposes the two remaining surfaces, and derives `expanded-select`. The observer
does not collapse success and refresh into a manually chosen phase.

## Frozen gates

Promotion requires:

1. exact OT-0306 parent and promoted receipt, open correction derivation, and
   conformant identity;
2. byte-exact preservation of the world-seeking stake, OT-0305 same-world
   negative comparison, provider/wait receipts, developmental stakes, and
   invalidity lineage;
3. no target literal in the harness and exactly two target-scoped hidden input
   classes before correction;
4. prospective success for all seven histories of zero, one, or two prior
   consequences: failed revision, surrender, and both mixed orders;
5. exact consequence-specific seed routing with sealed sources and undisclosed
   cases absent from both projections;
6. surrender grants no candidate, success, or earned authority; six authority
   controls reject; surrender after both hidden classes are disclosed fails
   closed;
7. every admitted branch reaches independent 6/6 against unchanged 2/6,
   derives refresh, then reaches a conformant two-opportunity successor deriving
   selection;
8. at most three fresh correction actors and four total content-free openings,
   with no retry after an invalid encounter; and
9. route 16/16 and identity 18/18 remain exact.

The cheapest falsifiers are wrong receipt projection, invalid or contaminated
actor output, any non-2/6 unchanged control, exhausted or fabricated feedback,
loss of protected lineage, observer-selected phase or target, failure to derive
refresh/selection, or actor/invocation budget exhaustion.

Preflight passes every frozen branch and control. Receipt `03e0da4e...`. Live
output remains sealed until this design, apparatus, and passing preflight are
committed and pushed.

## Limits fixed before output

A pass establishes one bounded recurrence in which retained consequence routes
both later actor context and an actor-free next phase. One live trajectory does
not estimate reliability across models or worlds, prove open-ended development,
show self-redesign of the recurrence machinery, or reverse OT-0305's negative
priority verdict.
