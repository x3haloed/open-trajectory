- **Status:** frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** complete the exact active stake, then test whether a deterministic
  developmental transition projection causally improves post-completion
  pursuit assimilation over the same raw evidence alone
- **Parent:** exact OT-0181 open operational successor
- **Actor budget:** one completion selector, eight assimilation actors, and
  eight successor selectors
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 75 minutes

## Interpretation repaired before contact

OT-0180 confirmed correction of authority under later blocked-outcome harm. It
did not complete the older revoked-identity pursuit. OT-0181's refined stake is
therefore not treated as fulfilled merely because its wording resembles prior
history.

First give one fresh actor the exact OT-0181 subject and require it to bind a
mechanism for the active correction stake. Open only after binding:

- the exact three revoked-identity cases named by the stake;
- three disjoint revoked-identity cases where only world-grounded prediction
  correction returns the complete observed set; and
- the exact accumulated 18-case floor.

Require prediction correction, exact contact 3/3, disjoint contact 3/3, and
floor preservation 18/18. Seal a completion receipt before any assimilation
actor runs. Failure stops the experiment and leaves the parent unchanged.

## Candidate and matched control

Compile a deterministic developmental delta from the bound stake and sealed
completion receipt. It records the prior stake digest, selected mechanism,
exact and disjoint scores, retained-floor score, and the frozen evaluator's
criterion status. It contains no next stake, property, mechanism recommendation,
or opening.

Run four fresh assimilation actors with that populated delta and four with an
exact field-erased delta. Both branches receive the exact completed subject,
raw completion receipt, candidates, authority, tools, budget, and mutation
envelope. Actors may `retire-and-renew` with a different visible property or
`refine-current` with the same property and a materially changed named missing
contact. Bind all eight outputs before opening any next-contact world.

For every valid output, compile its authored stake into a provisional subject.
A fresh successor selector then binds a mechanism before the fixed hidden
world selected by the actor-authored property opens. Require all eight
successors to choose the unique matching mechanism and pass 3/3.

## Frozen interpretation and promotion

Count retirement of the completed correction property, not verbal agreement:

- **delta-causal:** populated delta retires in at least 3/4 actors, erased
  control retires in at most 1/4, and the advantage is at least two;
- **raw-sufficient:** both branches retire in at least 3/4 actors; the smaller
  raw-receipt path is preferred and the delta is not installed;
- otherwise the assimilation comparison is inconclusive and no authored next
  stake is installed.

The completion receipt is sealed before this comparison and cannot be vetoed
by later controls. If an assimilation regime passes, install the first valid
retired successor in fixed actor order from the preferred regime, together
with its bound selection and contact. If neither passes, retain the completed
stake and open continuation for another assimilation mechanism.

All seventeen actors must pass complete trace and effects audit for the
assimilation claim. Passing establishes one exact completion and at most one
post-completion transition. It does not establish recurrent self-directed
development or eliminate researcher-authored contact families.
