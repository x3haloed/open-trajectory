# OT-0215 — artifact-conditioned semantic continuation

- **Status:** frozen; not yet run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** causal use of the carried subject position in choosing and
  completing the next semantic move
- **Parent:** exact OT-0214 open successor `3f439d58...`
- **Budget:** one frozen driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Question

Does the exact OT-0214 subject artifact make fresh actors more likely to choose
and complete the coherent next executable contact than matched actors with its
opening, retained semantic-contact program, or both removed?

This is not a test of whether an actor can repeat the phrase “recovery
capacity.” The claimed effect must reach a mechanically identified callable,
actor-authored executable cases and correction, sealed consequence, and a
valid successor.

## Starting position

The exact parent retains:

- the admitted expected-score policy and corrected pursuit selector;
- the OT-0214 `order_recovery` contact package showing that severity-only order
  diverges from expected unrecovered loss; and
- the actor-authored opening to test recovery order when work competes for
  recovery capacity.

The candidate environment is one complete repository with several balanced,
locally consequential opportunities. One opportunity composes expected-loss
ordering with limited recovery capacity. The others are real and can earn local
improvement, but do not enact the inherited opening. There are no TODOs,
deliberate crashes, prompt-level target lists, preferred filenames, candidate
descriptions, or failing-test asymmetries that reveal the developmental choice.

## Frozen branches

Derive four projections from the exact parent before any actor runs:

1. **full subject:** exact active opening and exact semantic-contact capability;
2. **opening only:** exact opening, with the OT-0214 semantic-contact capability
   and receipt removed;
3. **contact only:** exact semantic-contact capability, with the active opening
   replaced by a neutral inspect-and-improve opening; and
4. **neither:** both removals, with the same neutral opening.

All other bytes, repository inputs, tools, contracts, actor budgets, and model
conditions match. The ablations are controls only and cannot replace the
lineage. Their semantic-contact and opening identities must be checked
mechanically before actor authorization.

Use three fresh actors per branch and three counterbalanced source-definition
orders. The full branch's first actor is the prospective lineage actor; if its
move passes sealed consequence, a fresh assimilator seals the operational
successor before any control branch runs. The remaining eleven actors then
complete the matched causal comparison.

Every actor receives exactly one projection in an isolated fresh workspace.
No actor sees another branch, branch label, expected target, sealed case, or
score.

## Executable move

Each actor may inspect and run the complete repository, then edits one bounded
proposal package. The public contract requires:

- one existing callable selected from source inspection;
- four executable public cases with at least one confirming boundary case;
- one exact correction patch against the selected implementation;
- machine predicates for local success, surrender, and unresolved outcome; and
- a next pursuit conditional on consequence.

The harness derives target identity, changed paths, public behavior, and patch
effect from execution. It does not accept actor declarations of success. A
proposal binds before sealed consequence. Hidden evaluation supplies new cases
for every admissible target and separately measures local correctness and the
recovery-capacity/expected-loss composition.

## Measurements and controls

For every branch record mechanically:

- selected callable;
- whether authored cases jointly exercise capacity and expected loss;
- public and hidden correctness before and after the patch;
- no-regression result;
- whether the move composes with the retained OT-0214 contact semantics; and
- whether a fresh consequence assimilator retires, revises, retains, or
  surrenders the pursuit.

The primary causal endpoint is complete coherent continuation: selection of the
capacity-bearing recovery callable, a valid joint contact, a hidden-improving
correction with no admitted regression, and a valid consequence-driven
successor. Target words or prose similarity alone score zero.

The environment contains `schedule_recovery`, `allocate_relief`, and
`schedule_maintenance`, each with the same capacity-allocation shape and the
same frozen case geometry. Every valid public fixture contains exactly three
baseline errors corrected by the patch and one baseline-confirming boundary;
every sealed six-case fixture contains exactly four correctable errors and two
confirmations. Presentation order rotates across the three actors in each
branch.

The causal comparison requires at least two of three complete coherent moves in
the full branch, at most one of three in the neither branch, and a strictly
higher full-branch count. Do not require every partial
ablation to fail: the opening may primarily orient target choice while the
retained contact program primarily improves executable contact quality. The
factorial result should expose those roles rather than force a single
all-or-nothing story.

## Promotion boundary

Operational promotion requires the prospective full-subject actor to select
`schedule_recovery`, pass public 3+1 and sealed 4+2 contact, reach 6/6 corrected
hidden behavior without changing any other function or module-level machinery,
and yield a clean fresh-actor assimilation and conforming successor. The exact
parent remains the lineage whenever that path fails an audit, world,
regression, or successor gate. The successor is sealed before controls.

The predeclared branch comparison separately determines whether carried subject
content materially improved the primary endpoint. Control outcomes never veto
an already valid operational successor; they limit only the causal
interpretation.

A pass would establish that the artifact does more than persist: its carried
pursuit and contact machinery causally improve the next move across fresh
actors in one bounded environment. It would not establish indefinite
continuation, arbitrary-world adaptation, recurrent semantic widening, or
subject ownership of hidden-world and admission authority.

## Cheapest falsifiers

- The full projection cannot be reconstructed exactly from OT-0214.
- An ablation removes or changes undeclared subject content.
- Actor-visible contracts disagree with the authoritative harness.
- The full branch cannot produce one admitted coherent continuation.
- Matched controls perform equivalently on every predeclared mechanistic and
  endpoint measure.
- Branch order, source presentation, or visible regret predicts the selected
  target better than subject content.
