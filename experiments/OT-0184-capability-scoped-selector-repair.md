# OT-0184 — capability-scoped selector repair after novel-mechanism rejection

- **Status:** design frozen; executable conformance pending; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** test whether exact OT-0183 selection failure lets a fresh actor
  repair the selector and scope a novel mechanism without erasing the prior
  prediction and viable routes
- **Parent:** exact OT-0182 open successor plus retained rejected OT-0183
  contact, correction, selection, and floor receipts
- **Actor budget:** one repair author, twelve matched active selectors, and four
  prior-context selectors
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Prediction error

Expected: a valid candidate declaration plus active authority would let the
inherited selector choose an actor-authored novel mechanism.

Observed: OT-0183's correction actor authors a semantic composition that passes
the common hidden world 4/4 and gives it active authority, but the inherited
selector follows its fixed property guide back to viable filtering and scores
0/4.

Uncertain: whether the missing structure is a typed statement of what the
active stake requires, a typed statement of what each mechanism supplies, or a
different selector rule joining those declarations.

Evidence: exact OT-0183 contact, correction, authority, active choice, common
world, and prior-floor results.

## Frozen repair envelope

Give one fresh actor the exact parent and complete OT-0183 failure. It may
retain the selector or author one capability-scoped selector projection. The
visible vocabulary contains only two semantic capabilities:

- `observed-source`: output is grounded in observed outcome rather than option
  enumeration;
- `blocked-exclusion`: blocked items cannot survive output.

The actor declares capabilities for all operative mechanisms, requirements for
the exact active stake, and a deterministic selection rule from:

- exact capability match, then active authority, then stable mechanism id; or
- minimal capability superset, then active authority, then stable mechanism id.

It must also classify the retained prior correction-from-error and
option-expansion contexts. Public validation enforces complete declarations,
known vocabulary, deterministic tie-breaking, exact retention of surrendered
and historical boundaries, and substantive change when repair is claimed. It
does not prescribe declarations, rule choice, or the selected mechanism.

Bind the repair before opening any new world. Compile it with the exact
OT-0183 actor-authored mechanism and authority into a provisional subject.

## Frozen matched comparison

Run six fresh selectors from the repaired candidate and six from an exact
selector-repair-erased control. Both receive the same parent, OT-0183 failure,
mechanism candidates, authority, active stake, tools, and budget. Alternate
branch order and bind all twelve choices before opening six new mixed-contact
cases. The actor-authored composition alone passes the mixed world.

Require repaired selection/contact 6/6, erased control at most 2/6, and
advantage at least four. All selector decisions are scored by executable output,
not explanation or declared capability labels.

## Contextual no-regression and promotion

Run two fresh repaired selectors on disjoint prior observation-led contexts and
two on disjoint prior blocked-option contexts. The former must select prediction
correction and pass completely; the latter must select viable filtering and
pass completely. Replay the retained identity-extension floor 18/18 through
its own route. The novel mechanism need not globally pass contexts outside its
declared scope.

Promote only if all seventeen fresh actors pass complete trace and effects
audit, the matched advantage and all contextual floors pass, the exact
surrender/history boundaries remain unchanged, and the successor stays open.
Install the actor-authored mechanism, its scoped selector projection, corrected
authority, OT-0183 failure, and OT-0184 confirmation.

Passing would establish one consequence-driven joint repair of mechanism scope
and selection machinery. It would not establish recurrence under a later scope
contradiction, eliminate the fixed two-capability vocabulary, or make the
researcher optional for generating the next world family.
