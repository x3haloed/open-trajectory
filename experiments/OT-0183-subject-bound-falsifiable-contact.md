# OT-0183 — subject-bound falsifiable contact and machinery composition

- **Status:** design frozen; executable conformance pending; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** test whether the subject can choose contact that falsifies its
  current route, use that consequence to author a more general mechanism, and
  beat a property-only friendly-contact control on the same later world
- **Parent:** exact OT-0182 open successor
- **Actor budget:** one contact author, two correction authors, and two fresh
  successor selectors
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 55 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis and cheapest falsifier

The active option-expansion stake distinguishes contacts actually observed in
the world from merely enumerated options, while retaining blocked-result
exclusion. The installed viable-branch mechanism sees options and blocked
items but not observation; prediction correction sees observation but does not
exclude blocked items. A composition using observed outcomes with blocked
items removed should cover both established regimes.

The cheapest falsifier is that a fresh subject actor cannot bind a valid
contact containing both boundaries, or that the resulting consequence does not
cause a correction branch to outperform the property-only control. Do not
resample a failed contact author.

## Subject-bound contact

Give one fresh actor the exact parent, active stake, prior completion receipt,
candidate mechanisms, and an actor-visible contact schema. It authors four to
six concrete cases using only typed `outcome`, `options`, `blocked`, and
prediction fields. Admission requires at least one case with an unblocked
enumerated option absent from the observed outcome and at least one case with a
blocked item present in the observed outcome. These requirements are derived
from the active stake's two claimed boundaries; the actor chooses all case
contents, ordering, combinations, and rationale.

Bind the exact contact before hidden consequence. A deterministic sealed
compiler renames tokens and permutes list order without changing membership
relations. Score the installed viable mechanism, prediction correction, and
the uninstalled observed-minus-blocked composition on both authored and hidden
cases. Expected output is independently computed as observed outcome minus
blocked items; the actor cannot author scores or outcomes after binding.

The matched property-only branch receives the exact OT-0182 favorable
option-expansion world and no authored contact. Its viable route passes 3/3.
Seal both receipts before correction.

## Consequence-driven correction

Give one fresh correction actor per branch the same parent, primitive
vocabulary, candidate menu, tools, budget, and mutation envelope. The only
difference is the sealed contact receipt. Each may retain current machinery or
author one executable mechanism from:

- source `outcome` or `options`;
- optional intersection with observed outcome; and
- optional exclusion of blocked items.

It also authors the corresponding authority projection. Public checks enforce
shape, executable semantics, coherent authority/status pairs, exact retention
for unchanged components, and a real change when revision is claimed. They do
not prescribe an expression, mechanism id, decision, or hidden result.

Bind both corrections before opening confirmation. Compile valid revised
mechanisms into provisional exact successors. One fresh selector per branch
then chooses from the resulting candidate/authority state before the common
hidden world opens.

## Frozen worlds, controls, and promotion

The common hidden world contains four new mixed cases. Every case includes an
unblocked unobserved decoy and a blocked observed item. Only semantic
observed-minus-blocked composition passes 4/4. Also replay:

- the OT-0178 and OT-0179 prediction-correction floors;
- the OT-0180 harm and confirmation viable-filtering floors; and
- the retained 18-case identity-extension floor under its own contextual
  route.

Promote only if:

- all five actors pass complete trace and effects audit;
- authored public and hidden contact both expose failure of the installed
  viable route and prediction correction, while the composed semantic control
  passes completely;
- the active correction authors an executable composition, its successor
  selects it, and it passes the common hidden world 4/4;
- the property-only branch scores at most 1/4 on that world;
- the active branch passes every prior prediction and viable floor, while the
  identity-extension floor remains 18/18 through its retained route; and
- the exact successor remains open with the failure receipt, actor-authored
  mechanism, correction, selection, confirmation, and prior floors retained.

The property-only correction is not required to retain; its actual response is
part of the control. Passing establishes one bounded chain from subject-chosen
falsification through machinery composition and later advantage. It does not
establish recurrence across another uncurated contact or eliminate the fixed
typed contact language and sealed compiler as remaining researcher structure.
