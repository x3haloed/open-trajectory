# OT-0143 — cross-domain constitutional selector transfer

- **Status:** frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** test whether OT-0142's consequence-corrected preference for
  scale-invariant self-change transfers from reserve contact to a materially

- **Parent:** exact OT-0142 open subject carrying corrected selector and

- **Actor budget:** one fresh recovery-amendment portfolio author and one fresh

- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## New world and amendment language

Retain the exact corrected and reconstructed unchanged selectors from OT-0142.
Publish a distinct recovery-cadence amendment language:

- `fixed-delay`: one least public-passing absolute retry delay; and
- `latency-relative`: one least public-passing factor over the maximum observed
  recovery latency.

Derived features preserve only the abstract relation learned in OT-0142:
fixed-delay has smaller mutation surface and is not scale-invariant;
latency-relative has larger mutation surface and is scale-invariant. No reserve
quantum, demand spread, foreign context, or offset program is exposed to the
actor.

One fresh actor receives exact subject position, corrected selector, recovery
cases, and an executable checker. It must author exactly one candidate in each
family with ids, rationale, and surrender condition. Both must schedule the
correct delay on two public cases whose maximum observed latency is 32.

## Matched transfer comparison

Bind corrected and exact unchanged selector decisions on the same portfolio
before hidden consequence. Corrected is predicted to select latency-relative;
unchanged is predicted to select fixed-delay.

The sealed recovery world contains three shifted histories with required delays
64, 96, and 128 plus two stable controls requiring 32. A candidate passes a
history only when its scheduled delay exactly equals the maximum observed
recovery latency. Latency-relative factor one should pass all shifted histories
and controls. Fixed delay 32 should fail all three shifted histories and pass
both controls.

Before promotion, replay the exact retained quantum-relative reserve capability
at q256 and require nine adversarial distinctions plus three confirmations.
Only after matched recovery success and reserve no-regression may the active
recovery strategy become subject capability and amend the constitution to name
latency-relative cadence as active.

## Later reuse and correction reserve

One fresh actor must reuse exact retained recovery-strategy and constitution
bytes on three new latency histories requiring 160, 192, and 224 plus one stable
control. Seal a sounding open successor before applying the held-out fixed-delay
strategy as an observational control.

The subject must carry both reserve and recovery capabilities, remain open, and
point to another recovery-cadence encounter. Erasing only the corrected selector
must reproduce the unchanged fixed-delay choice on the actor-authored portfolio.

Stop at the first identity, actor schema, checker, trace, binding, matched world,
reserve floor, installation, reuse, or opening failure. Never resample an actor.

Passing would establish one bounded cross-domain transfer of consequence-
improved constitutional selection. It would not establish that scale invariance
is universally correct, open amendment-language invention, autonomous world
choice, or subject-authored priorities. A later contradictory regime must still
be able to correct or surrender the transferred priority.
