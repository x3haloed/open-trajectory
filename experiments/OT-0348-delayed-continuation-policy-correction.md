## Hypothesis and cheapest falsifier

The exact subject asks to test its retained world-consequence policy on a fresh
catalog. That policy currently treats immediate viable-contact count as its
primary continuation signal. The hypothesis is that independently completed
downstream consequence can make this rule visibly harmful, cause a fresh actor
to revise the executable selection machinery, and let the changed machinery
beat its exact unchanged parent on a disjoint catalog created only after the
revision is bound. A later fresh actor must complete the selected contact and
leave a subject-held, mechanically materializable frontier.

The cheapest falsifier is retention of the harmful rule, the same operative
revision without the consequence, failure to bind before held-out derivation,
agreement with the unchanged rule, no held-out continuation-yield advantage,
loss of the exact 40/40 floor, hidden contact failure, or a final opening that
cannot be materialized from the subject alone.

## Frozen world and information boundary

One private training seed derives three opaque worlds in opaque order. Their
fixed structural roles separate immediate contact count from verified
post-contact continuation: an immediate-rich world has five current contacts
and zero verified downstream contacts; a balanced world has three and one;
and a continuation-rich world has two and three. Every current contact is
floor-preserving and independently viable. Applying the exact incumbent policy
must select the immediate-rich world and realize zero downstream yield. The
observer-owned receipt also records the two unchosen counterfactual outcomes;
it is completed consequence, not actor self-report.

The active corrector receives the exact policy source, exact subject position,
all three completed consequence rows, and the incumbent selection. The matched
control receives the same files, tools, mutation authority, and current
selection, but downstream consequence fields are erased. Each may revise or
retain. It may edit only `policy.py` and `decision.json`; the policy ABI is the
general operation `select_world(rows) -> world_id | None`. The checker enforces
only syntax, determinism, admissibility, permutation stability, and source-
independent ids. It does not encode the desired metric or selection.

If the active actor cleanly revises and its public replay improves realized
continuation yield, bind its exact source. Only then generate one new private
held-out seed. It derives new ids, order, contact counts, cutoffs, and downstream
frontiers for analogous but numerically distinct roles: immediate-rich is six
to zero, balanced is four to one, and continuation-rich is two to four. Neither
corrector can see this catalog or seed.

The held-out selector receives the bound source and complete public consequence
rows, chooses only among the program-selected world's current contacts, and has
no hidden cases, role labels, control result, or evaluator. A final fresh actor
receives only that bound contact and repairs it. Hidden consequence then reveals
the selected world's downstream public frontier and compiles it into the
subject. All actors have ordinary broad tools in isolated workspaces under the
prospectively retained G13/G11 effect audit.

## Controls, score, and promotion

The primary score is independently verified downstream contact count after one
equivalent current contact. Apply changed and exact unchanged policies to the
same held-out rows. Machinery erasure is exact replacement of the bound source
with the incumbent source while retaining all information and resources. A
regressive high-yield decoy must remain inadmissible. The consequence-erased
actor is the causal ablation for whether completed delayed consequence, rather
than the correction prompt or ABI, produced the operative change.

Operational promotion requires clean active correction, pre-heldout binding,
a clean held-out selector and contact actor, 3/3 public plus 5/5 hidden contact
success, exact 40/40 floor preservation, and an open subject whose downstream
frontier materializes without private state. The machinery-refinement claim
additionally requires changed held-out yield strictly above unchanged yield,
different selected worlds, exact machinery erasure reproducing the unchanged
choice, and no equivalent operative revision from the erased actor.

This is a bounded synthetic test of correcting one selection rule. It does not
establish natural-distribution fitness, generation reliability, autonomous
world invention, or full OT-2R. The observer still defines the world family,
independent outcome, and admission envelope; the subject owns whether and how
to revise within the exposed executable ABI.
