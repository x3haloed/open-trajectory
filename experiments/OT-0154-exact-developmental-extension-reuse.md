- **Status:** frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** test whether the exact OT-0153 subject can route and execute its
  retained new property and operation in a later scheduler encounter
- **Parent:** exact OT-0153 open subject
- **Actor budget:** one fresh post-consequence assimilator; no package,
  implementation, routing, or repair actor
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Frozen reuse

Read the parent's exact `identity-gated-branch-filtering` stake. The unchanged
OT-0153 extension-aware compiler must resolve it solely from the retained
package binding. Load the exact retained `realize_contact` source under the same
restricted evaluator and run three new public cases. Bind source identity,
stake, route, and public results before hidden cases are materialized.

Independent hidden consequence then runs three disjoint cases: coherent
identity with one blocked option, changed identity with no blocked options, and
coherent identity with all options blocked. The retained operation must pass
3/3 without input mutation. No actor may modify, restate, reconstruct, or
authorize the operation.

Only after passing consequence, give one fresh assimilator the exact subject,
stake, package, route, and world receipt plus the full four-property vocabulary.
It may edit only `next-stake.json`. It must retire the completed property into a
different valid property whose success and surrender conditions are grounded in
the observed consequence.

## Controls and promotion

Seal before controls. An exact extension-erased parent must be unroutable by the
extension compiler, and the unchanged closed compiler must remain unable to
route the new property. The successor's actor-authored next stake must route
through either the unchanged closed compiler or an exact installed extension.

Promote only if retained package binding and source identity are exact, public
and hidden reuse pass 3/3, the one fresh actor passes complete trace and exact
effects audit, the successor retains every parent field and adds a reuse
capability and receipt, both negative controls remain unroutable, the next stake
routes, and the exact successor is sounding and open.

Passing would establish one complete expansion-to-later-use transition across
an actor and observer boundary. It would not establish repeated extension
growth, correction of a harmful extension, or that the expanded selector can
choose when extension is better than a fixed property.
