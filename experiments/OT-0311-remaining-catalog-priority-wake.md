# OT-0311 — Remaining-catalog priority wake

- **Status:** design frozen; live ranking sealed
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0310 open subject `59a1f68c...`
- **Invocation:** two content-free actor-free operations
- **Catalog:** exactly the OT-0305 provider packages not consumed by the subject

## Hypothesis

The exact active world-seeking stake, unchanged since it was bound before the
OT-0305 provider worlds existed, will clear its fixed support gap on the three
objectively remaining packages and select a different world than the digest-
minimum blind selector over the identical catalog. The selected public world
will enter the standing offer through the inherited provider interface without
an observer choosing a world.

This is a selection-effect claim, not yet a better-downstream-performance claim.
Later contact must show whether entering the selected world actually improves
the trajectory.

## Frozen world release and path

The first `expand-environment` operation observes the unchanged empty provider
cursor and installs the inherited wait. The second `wait-provider` operation
releases exactly all four retained OT-0305 subject-blind provider packages minus
world ids already present in the subject's environment stream. This set has
three packages. No package is added, removed, regenerated, or chosen after the
ranking is opened.

The inherited stake and digest-blind control receive the same public descriptors
in the same operation. The stake maximizes its already bound weighted score and
must clear its already bound minimum gap; the control minimizes public-package
digest. Sealed reference sources and cases do not enter either selector. If the
stake is unsupported, the wait and stake remain active, contradiction is
receipted, and the subject derives `revise-world-seeking-stake`. No fallback
world is offered. If supported, only the stake-selected package may discharge
the wait and the subject derives `expanded-select`.

## Frozen gates and controls

Promotion requires exact OT-0310 ancestry; the untuned receipt-valid active
stake; the exact three-package objective remainder; support at the fixed gap;
a selected world different from the same-catalog blind control; a matching
standing offer; E11 priority-bearing contact; and an exact open conformant
successor. The prior OT-0305 negative verdict remains evidence and is not
rewritten.

Synthetic support and contradiction branches pass prospectively. Support is
permutation invariant, discharges the wait, does not duplicate the stake, and
rejects a package that differs from the selected world. Contradiction retains
the wait and stake and routes revision. A tampered stake binding rejects. All
four retained packages are independently valid, but the live three-package
ranking remains uncomputed in preflight. Route 16/16 and identity 18/18 remain
exact. Preflight receipt `64e2d065...`.

The cheapest falsifiers are failure to clear the gap, selection of the same
world as blind, any catalog substitution, stake mutation, sealed-content use,
offer/world mismatch, loss of the wait on contradiction, or a closed or
nonconformant successor. The one live ranking has no retry.

## Limits fixed before output

A pass establishes that the inherited stake is behaviorally selective on one
prospective remainder catalog and removes the observer from that world choice.
It does not establish that the selected world is objectively better, that the
effect repeats across catalogs, or that later contact succeeds.
