# OT-0203 — independent-contact mechanism generalization

- **Status:** design frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** turn the OT-0202 subject-authored pursuit into a generalized
  executable capability rather than a one-contact fit
- **Parent:** exact OT-0202 open successor `af704625...`
- **Actor budget:** six independent contact authors followed by six mechanism
  inventors
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 65 minutes

## Frozen order and information boundary

First, six fresh contact actors receive the exact subject and its authored
renewal binding. They independently author aligned, suite-novel contacts. Seal
their hidden token-renamed cases before any mechanism actor runs.

Then six fresh mechanism actors receive the exact subject, its target-language
extension, active pursuit, and only the already-promoted OT-0202 contact and
consequence. They do not receive any contact-bank bytes, summaries, source
memberships, case counts, or outputs. Each may invent one scoped depth-four set
expression using `options`, `outcome`, `blocked`, and the subject-authored
`contactable-distinction` source. `prediction` is unavailable.

## Frozen falsifier, controls, and gate

Execute every invented mechanism against all hidden cases from all six prior
contact actors. The target is independently computed from the subject-authored
expression. The cheapest falsifier is any accepted inventor failing any hidden
case, or any contact author failing alignment.

Score retained observed, latent, and viable mechanisms over the same bank as
negative baselines. Also report prediction-copy as a leakage diagnostic, but
prohibit it from the mechanism grammar. Require:

- all twelve fresh actors and audits accepted;
- all six contact suites aligned and suite-novel;
- all six invented mechanisms pass the complete hidden bank;
- no retained baseline passes the complete bank;
- the subject-authored target expression itself passes the complete bank;
- route 16/16, identity 18/18, exact ancestry, and canonical active stake.

On passage, install active-01's scoped mechanism, direct independent-bank
receipt, and a completion receipt for the subject-authored pursuit. Reopen the
subject at post-completion renewal. Passing establishes generalized capability
inside this bounded set world, not usefulness under an external natural world
or recurrent self-renewal.
