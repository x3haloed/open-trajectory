- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted

## Frozen hypothesis

The rejected OT-0122 transition failed because its route contract did not make
semantic extension part of the authoritative continuation ABI. Given the exact
retained OT-0122 selector binding and selected world receipt, one fresh actor
under an explicit coherence contract can ground `resource scarcity` in both the
authoritative next opening and a materially new continuation target, while
preserving the installed selector and every settled selected-branch case.

## Exact retained inputs

Reconstruct the published OT-0122 run and require exact archive and aggregate
identity. Require the exact OT-0121 parent, selection binding, selected world
receipt, clean first-router trace, `joint-boundary` active selection,
`carry-heavy-boundary` pre-correction control selection, and four exact joint

its `successor-opening.next_opening` exactly equaled the parent's authoritative

No selector selection, contact envelope, world consequence, hidden fixture, or
case id may be regenerated. The rejected actor output is diagnostic evidence
only and is not inherited as subject state.

## Published coherence contract

The fresh actor receives the exact parent position, bound selection, selected
world consequence, immutable corrected selector, and route semantics. Because
the retained consequence mechanically classifies as `extend`, admission
requires all of the following:

1. route is exactly `extend` and cites the exact four settled case ids;
2. selector bytes remain exact;
3. `successor-opening.next_opening` differs from the parent's opening and
   contains both concepts `resource` and `scarcity`;
4. the continuation action is `registry-extension`, its target differs from
   the parent's target, and its target contains both `resource` and `scarcity`;
5. remaining uncertainty, expected information, and continuation after contact
   also carry the resource-scarcity transfer question; and
6. all ordinary structural, tool-trace, exact-change, and truthfulness gates
   pass.

Bind the actor output before promotion. Promote only the complete exact child
and reopen it sounding with the new authoritative opening. No replacement
actor, relaxed synonym set, retrospective content scoring, or favorable subset
is allowed.

## Cheapest falsifiers and stopping

Reject on any retained-input mismatch, actor nonconformance, missing exact case
citation, selector mutation, inherited opening or target, absent required
concept, invalid action, or failed exact reopening. Stop after the single fresh
actor.

Passing would establish that the already successful subject-caused contact can
be assimilated into a genuinely extended authoritative continuation once the
continuation ABI states the missing semantic invariant. It would not establish
general semantic grounding, repeated autonomous scheduling, or subject
authorship of world envelopes.
