## Frozen hypothesis and cheapest falsifier

Lossless post-trial locators written without future knowledge, together with a
complete batch of independently world-resolved address attempts, can let one
fresh process author a bounded extensional projection practice. That practice
can cause one exact historical branch to be projected and one compact
successor mechanism to be adopted. Later world contact can make the complete
practice harmful, and fresh completed attempts can make a behaviorally
different replacement practice identifiable without controller branch
selection.

The durable practice has two actor-owned records. A full practice receipt binds
the complete current request/outcome support and the previous compact practice.
Its compact child is the active three-row relation from opaque query keys to
opaque directory address handles. The directory resolves those handles to exact
tuples. Neither record is a diagnosis, score, natural-language reason, program,
supplied operation menu, or controller ranking. Every row is grounded in exact
request and world-outcome receipts, but only the compact child crosses the
2,048-byte active-inheritance boundary. Replaying an earlier compact table under
a new current request after either reversal must choose the wrong branch for
every query.

This is an opportunity and interface-causality hypothesis only. Synthetic
actor-channel fixtures are not actor output. A pass cannot establish actor
authorship, learned refitting, representation escape, transfer, or widened
developmental standing.

Reject and authorize no actor if any of these occurs:

- a locator body depends on later contact, a selected future field, a regime
  schedule, a hidden mapping, or a sealed endpoint;
- current contact, locators, or any inheritance-budget-legal raw attempt
  projection uniquely determines the final address without a new practice;
- the complete current diagnostic-attempt batch does not uniquely identify the
  reference practice under the actor-visible one-to-one world promise;
- an unchanged prior practice remains correct after either reversal;
- the replacement differs only in record identity while its complete
  query-to-address behavior remains unchanged;
- a wrong but structurally valid practice is rejected before independent world
  consequence;
- any scored row identity occurs in source history, address attempts,
  correction consequence, or an earlier endpoint;
- a controller or substrate parses locator meaning, compares attempt quality,
  selects a branch, fits a practice, repairs a successor, or promotes a winner;
- the exact causal chain, fresh-process seams, fixed controls, byte geometry,
  one-attempt derivation, run lock, evidence, privacy, or resource gates fail;
  or
- any candidate output, actual actor turn, actor tool call, or hosted call
  occurs.

## Frozen address world

There are sixteen isomorphic cases. Each contains three opaque query keys,
three opaque branch-output keys, three completed inactive source branches, and
three regimes. Actor-visible orientation states only that the current address
relation is a one-to-one mapping between the three query keys and three branch
keys. It supplies no permutation, ordinal regime label, schedule, preferred
relation, or example practice. Each contact instead carries a fresh case-local
opaque token that does not reveal its regime index or order.

Evaluator-private semantic indices use these permutations:

| Practice | Query 0 | Query 1 | Query 2 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| pi-0 | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| pi-1 | 1 | 2 | 0 |
| pi-2 | 2 | 0 | 1 |

Cases zero through seven use regime schedule pi-0, pi-1, pi-2. Cases eight
through fifteen use pi-0, pi-2, pi-1. Semantic query, branch, address, contact,
input-row, and trial-row identities are replaced by domain-separated
32-character opaque tokens derived only after the implementation is committed;
presentation and append-order controls are fixed independently.
The three address handles are placed into actor-visible canonical ranks.
Evaluator-private assignment from semantic branch index to address rank repeats the following
eight permutations in both case groups: 012, 021, 102, 120, 201, 210, 012,
021. This assignment is fixed before any regime schedule is applied. It makes
the fixed-clock bound independent of accidental opaque-token order. The
schedule bit, address-rank assignment, and semantic indices are absent from
every reset surface.

For query zero and query one in every regime, all three branches are attempted.
Each of those six attempts receives the same contact header, current
correction-consequence identity without its record body, and directory plus one
different neutral exhaustive-attempt ordinal and its actor-visible address
handle. It runs in its own fresh process and emits exactly one extensional
request. Its world-owned outcome copies the validated address handle and
contains raw query, proposal-output, and resolved-output tokens. It contains no
equality bit, error count, winner, loss, branch quality, or repair hint. All
three outcomes for one query have the same independently resolved output and
distinct proposal outputs.

The complete six-outcome batch therefore exposes two query-to-output pairs.
Under the public one-to-one promise, those two pairs uniquely determine the
third. A fresh candidate-free reference fitting process must identify the two
matching address attempts from raw rows, infer the remaining pair, and author
one full practice receipt plus compact three-row practice. A different fresh
practice-execution process receives only that compact practice, current contact
header, directory, and query two and authors one exact current projection
request. The controller validates structure and exact directory membership
only.

The three reference practices change all three rows between regimes. Applying
pi-0 unchanged in the first reversed regime is wrong for all three queries.
Applying the first replacement unchanged in the second reversed regime is also
wrong for all three. The prior practice remains recoverable and may be replayed;
it is never deleted, rewritten, or silently corrected.

## Future-blind post-trial locators

Each source branch has one complete world-owned source-context record, one
actor-channel compact mechanism proposal, and one independently world-owned
trial. Only after all three exist may a synthetic actor-channel locator be
appended.

The source context contains one opaque branch-output key and no target-regime
field. The locator payload is derived exactly as follows. `body` is the exact
object `{record_id, record}` containing the source-context identity and its
complete canonical `{source, parents, payload}` record. `surface_sha256` is the
SHA-256 of the canonical object whose exact keys are `source_context`,
`proposal`, and `trial`, where each value is the exact `{record_id, record}`
object for that node. The locator contains no other descriptive field.

The locator derivation receives no later contact, target query, regime,
permutation, correction row, holdout row, score, or selected subset. Every
locator is built and content-addressed before the regime schedule is derived.
The substrate and controller treat its body as bounded opaque JSON. They may
validate exact bytes and ancestry but may not parse, compare, summarize, rank,
or rewrite it.

The neutral controller directory contains all three and only three tuples:
opaque address handle, locator identity, proposal identity, and bound trial
identity. Array position is the actor-visible canonical address rank; the
frozen evaluator-private branch-to-rank assignment determines the order before
any regime is applied. Opaque-handle alpha-renaming never reorders the array.
The directory has no branch-output key, title, score, recency rank, quality,
semantic role, target relation, or preferred head.

## Disjoint compact-mechanism consequence

The selected history branch carries a two-bit Boolean mechanism rather than
target labels. Evaluator-private branch coefficients are:

| Branch | Coefficients |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 01 |
| 1 | 10 |
| 2 | 11 |

The genesis active proposal is the neutral coefficient vector 00, outside the
three selectable source branches. It is wrong on two of three correction rows
and four of six endpoint rows in every regime. The first practice must
therefore cause a real behavioral improvement rather than copy an
already-correct active mechanism.

For public two-bit feature vector x, a proposal with coefficient vector y
computes h-y(x) as the binary inner product of y and x modulo two. The generic
mechanism interpreter is fixed before task derivation. A source proposal's
world trial uses only two source-row identities with features 10 and 01; both
proposal and resolved outputs are that source proposal's own h-y values, so all
three inactive branches have independently valid trials before the regime
schedule exists. Correction consequence, successor trial, and endpoint world
rows instead resolve against the coefficient selected by the current private
query-two permutation, while their observed output comes respectively from the
current active or named successor proposal.

After query-two projection, the main path's candidate-free successor fixture
copies the selected source proposal's exact two-bit coefficient vector into one
new successor proposal with an empty note. It receives no correction
consequence, attempt outcome, full receipt, evaluator mapping, or fallback. The
independently owned successor trial uses three new correction-row identities
with features 10, 01, and 11.
Only after a fresh actor-channel adopt decision fixture and compare-and-swap
pointer transition does the evaluator open six endpoint rows: two independently
opaque replicas of each of those three features. The set-down control occurs at
the action-specific decision seam after a valid proposal and trial; rollback
uses its own OT-0070 decision surface.

Every source row, address row, correction row, endpoint row, and cross-regime
row identity is distinct. The two-bit feature alphabet and binary outputs do
repeat by design; the scored row records and identities do not. Within the
frozen three-mechanism family, a correct selected branch scores zero of six
errors. Any wrong branch differs on two of the three feature vectors and
therefore scores exactly four of six errors. The correction trial of a wrong
branch has exactly two of three errors. This supports only structural
application to fresh row identities inside the frozen family, not recovery of
novel labels or generalization beyond it.

## Fresh-process and byte boundary

Every diagnostic attempt, practice fit, practice execution, successor
construction, and adopt, set-down, or rollback decision uses a new process and
new empty workspace. Every process receives only canonical standard-input
envelopes and has no store loader, path, capability, omitted identity lookup,
file inheritance, network, or tool. Each diagnostic process additionally
receives one neutral exhaustive-attempt ordinal and its exact actor-visible
address handle, so the six otherwise deterministic workers cover three
distinct addresses for each of two queries without selecting a winner. Each
adopt/set-down decision fixture receives an exact requested-action literal;
both successor-quality placebos request adopt, while only the set-down
intervention requests set-down.

No individual exact trajectory projection may exceed OT-0070's 2,048-byte
limit. Larger reset envelopes are explicit collections of independently
validated projections:

- one contact-header slice at most 2,048 bytes;
- one correction-consequence slice at most 2,048 bytes;
- directory slice at most 2,048 bytes;
- one selected-locator slice at most 2,048 bytes;
- one selected proposal-plus-trial slice at most 2,048 bytes;
- one complete compact-practice projection at most 2,048 bytes;
- one full practice receipt record at most 4,096 bytes and never included in
  the active compact-practice projection;
- every diagnostic request independently fits an exact projection at most
  2,048 bytes but is not included in the practice-fit envelope;
- every outcome is a self-contained separate exact projection from 1,025
  through 2,048 bytes, copies its request's address handle under world
  validation, and retains the request as an external parent;
- every exact projection selecting two or more outcome records exceeds 2,048
  bytes after canonical external-parent deduplication and is rejected;
- complete six-outcome current-contact batch at most 12,288 bytes;
- aggregate practice-fit envelope at most 24,576 bytes;
- aggregate address, successor, or decision envelope at most 8,192 bytes; and
- full controller-owned trajectory at least 32,769 bytes.

Current regime contact, the complete current attempt batch, and the full
practice receipt are learning contact and durable provenance, not active
inherited practice. Across the next reset, the compact three-row practice is
the only practice state within the 2,048-byte active-inheritance cap. Requests
alone contain no resolved output, and no legal single or combined raw
projection can contain two outcome bodies. A digest, any number of request-only
projections, one raw outcome, or any other budget-legal raw subset therefore
leaves at least two branch outputs possible for query two. All matched controls
receive the same current contact, prompt, process count, wall budget, and exact
total envelope length; absent content is replaced only by equal-length opaque
padding outside trajectory authority.
