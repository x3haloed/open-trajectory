# OT-0027 — Trajectory-relative exemplar-casebook feasibility

- **Status:** frozen; hosted output forbidden until the run lock is committed
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target authority:** none; development feasibility only
- **Predecessor:** OT-0026 controller invalidation and closed program-portfolio line

## Reorientation and hypothesis

OT-0021 through OT-0026 exhausted the direct whole-program and alternative-
portfolio line. OT-0027 changes the learning topology and representation: one
trajectory-relative casebook directly embodies the selector as actor-chosen
examples, feature masks, match radii, and priorities. The target explicitly
admits examples as a selector representation. The controller supplies only
generic masked-Hamming matching and deterministic top-k interpretation, not a
useful criterion, strategy name, alternative, ranking, or semantic compiler.

Prior independently retained null-selector consequences may let a fresh actor
choose examples whose feature relations cause useful later retention. If they
do not, the casebook representation fails cheaply before any full recursive
candidate or renewed evaluator authorization.

## Cheapest falsifier, controls, and scoring

Before hosted output, reject the representation unless a known exact casebook
commits a distinct snapshot, selects six corresponding future examples on a
fresh three-feature parity relation, reduces deterministic held-out error by
eight, and replays identically. Reject unknown or duplicate anchors, malformed
masks, out-of-bound radius or priority, sealed-split prompt leakage, or a
universal casebook that fails the behavioral gate.

Then exactly two fresh Luna encounters each author one casebook from the prior
raw consequence ledger. Each casebook must change selection and prediction,
retain exactly six events, and gain at least four errors over the unchanged
empty casebook. Every freshness, tool, exact-model, inventory, Response, ETag,
collector, replay, identity, resource, test, audit, privacy, and evidence gate
must pass.

## Claim limit and freeze boundary

A pass establishes public non-candidate feasibility of this example-based
selector representation only. It does not establish correction, promote OT-1,
renew E4, or authorize a private candidate. Actor output is forbidden until a
clean implementation commit and separate run-lock commit bind every authority.
OT-0027 is not adjusted after output. A frozen failure closes direct exemplar-
casebook feasibility as the next mechanism path.
