unchanged parent three-of-nine result, and floor nine-of-nine result.

`compiled_constitution` under the inherited transition view key `constitution`
without changing its bytes or the original binding identity. Record a projection
receipt binding both names, exact digest, source binding, and consumer ABI. The
unprojected binding must still lack the required transition-view key.

Apply the unchanged OT-0139 transition function once to the exact parent,
authorization, binding view, q16 corrected receipt, q8 floor receipt, and
retained unchanged failure. Require installation of the exact compiled
constitution and offsets-sixteen capability, cycle six, cleared failure, and
verification due.

## One fresh verification

Let the installed scheduler authorize its next encounter. One fresh actor in an
isolated workspace with full ordinary tools receives exact subject position,
constitution, capability, and reuse authorization. It may edit only
`encounter-action.json`, must preserve exact program and constitution bytes,
and must be bound before new q16 bases open.

Require nine adversarial distinctions and three controls. The unchanged
transition must clear verification, admit q16, point to q32, and leave a
sounding open subject after the observer stops. A constitution-erased control
must lack authoritative next authorization.

Reject at the first manifest, identity, projection, installation, actor trace,
mutation, world, transition, or opening failure. No replacement actor, hidden
world resample, or constitutional resample is permitted.

Passing would complete the bounded higher-order loop begun in OT-0139:
retained consequence exhausts a subject-resident correction constitution, an
actor revises its semantic boundary, deterministic projection carries that
change through bookkeeping and installation, and a later fresh actor reuses the
result. It would not establish arbitrary constitutional revision,
subject-authored meta-rules, cross-world transfer, or indefinite operation.
