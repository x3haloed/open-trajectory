# OT-0186 — consequence-revised executable routing

- **Status:** complete; rejected at frozen causal-advantage gate
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** test whether receipted contradiction can revise represented route
  state into an operative selector that later encounters execute rather than
  reinterpret
- **Parent:** exact OT-0182 open successor plus the OT-0183 mechanism, OT-0185
  certified matrix, and OT-0185 actor-authored route
- **Actor budget:** four contradiction-visible repair actors and four matched
  contradiction-erased controls
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Prediction error carried forward

OT-0185 grounded mechanism signatures and obtained a plausible route, but then
gave the route to fresh language-model selectors as advice. Two of six active
workspaces followed it, three of six erased controls recovered the same answer
from the visible matrix, and all five contradicted their file choice in the
final report. The artifact did not occupy the causal decision edge.

The route itself also contains an unresolved mistake. On the narrow
`blocked-option` probes, viable filtering and the OT-0183 composition tie. The
repair actor breaks the tie toward the new active-authority mechanism. A wider
member of that regime can distinguish them: when one allowed option has not
yet appeared in observed outcome, the composition drops it while viable
filtering correctly preserves it.

## Frozen contradiction contact

Compile the exact OT-0185 route as deterministic data under a fixed, audited
interpreter from regime id to mechanism id. Before any repair actor runs, bind
three public `blocked-option` cases with:

- an observed allowed item;
- an unobserved allowed option;
- an observed blocked item; and
- expected output equal to all options minus blocked items.

The OT-0185 route selects `observed-unblocked-contact-corrector`, which must
fail 0/3 by omitting the unobserved allowed option. Viable filtering must pass
3/3. Seal the exact inputs, per-mechanism outputs, and route-level failure as
world-owned evidence. This receipt is the only active/control information
difference.

## Frozen repair comparison

Give four fresh active actors the exact candidate route, certified matrix,
mechanism definitions, authority, current stake, and contradiction receipt.
Give four interleaved controls the same projection with the contradiction
receipt exactly erased. Each actor may retain or revise the complete
three-regime route. A public checker enforces known mechanism ids, exactly one
route per regime, immutable inputs, and coherent file/report output. It does
not encode an expected route.

Bind all eight outputs before disjoint confirmation. Do not let the observer
select a favorable active repair: `active-01` is the prospective successor,
and all four active branches must independently satisfy the gate.

## Operative use and promotion

For every accepted repair, execute its exact bound route directly through the
fixed interpreter. Do not invoke another model to select among mechanisms.
Open disjoint confirmation only after binding:

- six wider `blocked-option` cases requiring preservation of an unobserved
  allowed option;
- four `observation-led` cases requiring prediction correction; and
- six `mixed-observed-blocked` cases requiring the OT-0183 composition.

Score executable outcome, not route prose or mechanism identity in isolation.
Require all four contradiction-visible repairs to pass 16/16. Require at most
one of four contradiction-erased controls to pass 16/16, for an advantage of
at least three. The exact original OT-0185 route must fail all six wider
blocked-option cases while retaining the other ten contextual cases. Replay
the identity-extension floor 18/18.

Promote only if all eight actors pass complete trace, effects, mutation, and
file/report conformance audit; the public contradiction and disjoint
confirmation behave exactly; active/control thresholds pass; exact history and
surrender boundaries remain unchanged; and the successor stays open. Install
the OT-0183 mechanism, the contradiction receipt, and exact `active-01`
executable route. Do not install an actor route that has not itself passed all
three contextual consequences.

Passing would establish one consequence-caused revision of selection state and
later direct use across fresh encounters. It would not establish discovery of
the regime ontology, correction after an unenumerated regime, actor revision
of the fixed interpreter, or long-run recurrence without researcher-authored
contact.

## Result

The frozen contradiction behaves exactly: the OT-0185 blocked-option route
fails 0/3 because it drops the unobserved allowed option, while viable
filtering passes 3/3. The original route later retains observation-led and
mixed floors 10/10 but fails all six disjoint wider blocked-option cases.

All four contradiction-visible actors revise the blocked-option route to
viable filtering, retain prediction correction and the OT-0183 composition in
their other contexts, and pass direct executable confirmation 16/16. All eight
actors pass complete trace, effects, mutation, and file/report conformance
audit. The identity floor remains 18/18. Direct execution therefore removes
OT-0185's successor reinterpretation failure.

Two of four contradiction-erased controls also infer the same repair from the
mechanism semantics and active option-expansion stake; the other two retain the
failed route and score 10/16. Active/control full-pass counts are 4/4 versus
2/4, an advantage of two rather than the frozen minimum three. The complete
promotion gate rejects.

No executable route or OT-0183 mechanism is installed. Exact parent
`40818aa8c898da506378ab82ddebe6682c5edc8b524f5e1bb2025bb391cf2747`
remains open. OT-0186 establishes a bounded engineering result—bound route
state can directly preserve a corrected decision—but does not decisively
attribute correction to the new world receipt because the retained state
already contains enough semantic evidence for some actors to infer it.

The next test should replicate the same frozen comparison at a sample size
that can distinguish a probabilistic receipt effect from the observed 50%
receipt-erased inference rate. Do not hide the stake or mechanism semantics to
manufacture separation. Predeclare the larger threshold before new actors run;
if the effect does not replicate, retain executable routing only as an
engineering affordance rather than evidence of consequence-caused correction.
