# OT-0333 — Subject-sized environment search

- **Status:** rejected
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0332 open child `38e9b5dd...`
- **Fresh actors:** one policy author, two or three subject-blind world providers,
  and at most one selected-world contact actor

## Frozen hypothesis

The exact subject derives environmental expansion, but the prior priority
experiments gave its stake an externally completed catalog. A fresh actor can
turn the subject's current developmental position into an explicit bounded
solicitation policy before any future world identity exists. Fixed machinery
can then request subject-blind providers one at a time and stop at the first
prefix authorized by that policy, rather than using an experimenter-fixed
catalog size.

The actor may choose two or three providers beneath an observer safety ceiling
of four and may require one to three consecutive supported prefixes, with or
without winner stability. A supported stop must expose only the selected world,
then a fresh target-untold actor must bind executable contact and receive exact
independent 2/6 contradiction. If the actor's own ceiling is exhausted without
support, the subject must retain the catalog contradiction and open stake
revision; it may not receive a fallback world.

## Prediction error and mechanism correction

The old OT-0311 handler rejects the exact current stake because it re-applies
OT-0305's obsolete ±4 weight gate. Later admitted machinery expanded the public
range to ±20, and the active binding uses that range. OT-0333 does not rescore
OT-0311. It validates the active binding through exact digest, admitted revision
ancestry, the current public stake ABI, and authority flags.

The same audit also falsified a tempting shortcut: the current stake is
supported on a two-world synthetic prefix, changes winner after the third, and
is unsupported after the fourth. Catalog support is therefore not itself
sampling authority. The new actor-authored policy is the named intervention.

## Frozen comparison and gates

Before actor output, require exact OT-0332 ancestry and promotion, open
`expand-environment`, zero local opportunities, a conformant exact subject,
valid current stake binding, rejection by the obsolete validator, G11, standing
provider and renewal interfaces, route 16/16, identity 18/18, and complete
synthetic support, exhaustion, tampering, and policy-erasure fixtures.

The policy actor sees the exact waiting subject and public descriptor/stopping
contract but no future world, package identity, target, source, case, outcome,
or provider output. Each later provider sees neither subject nor policy. Every
actor has a fresh workspace and broad tools; G11 and exact effects remain
authoritative. Providers must be mutually novel and must not collide with the
subject ledger.

Promotion of the full claim requires:

1. a valid policy bound before future identities;
2. two or three clean fresh providers, stopping exactly at the policy's first
   authorized prefix and below the observer ceiling;
3. a policy-erased branch that cannot authorize that same stop;
4. a unique stake-supported selected offer; and
5. one clean fresh target-untold actor whose executable contact receives the
   selected provider's independent 2/6 contradiction and leaves an exact open
   correction successor.

Policy exhaustion is a valid open subject transition but rejects this stronger
contact claim. Any invalid actor, collision, premature/late stop, fallback,
sealed leakage, audit failure, or nonconformant successor rejects without
retry. The selected world is not claimed objectively better than unrequested
worlds; this experiment tests subject-sized search and real downstream contact.

## Result

Rejected at the first provider, without retry. The policy actor passed G11 and
bound `three-provider-two-support-prefix` before any future world existed. It
chose the cautious prospective rule suggested by neither the harness nor a
future catalog: request at most three providers, require two consecutive
supported prefixes, and require the same winner across both. This valid actor
output and its exact unpromoted policy-bearing subject are retained.

The first subject-blind provider independently authored
`morrow-quay-ledger-v1`. Its package is structurally valid, scanner-visible,
world-novel, and contains three exact 2/6 surfaces. Its broad-tool trace is
clean. Admission nevertheless rejected the whole package because the bare
symbol `assign_berth` already occurs in the subject ledger. The new surface is
scoped to a different world and has different executable semantics, while its
other two surfaces are novel. No ranking, stop, selected offer, or downstream
contact occurred. Aggregate `2f731b99...`; the promoted current subject remains
OT-0332 child `38e9b5dd...`.

This exposes an additional closed-world assumption rather than falsifying the
solicitation policy: provider novelty is checked globally by bare callable name,
even though retained opportunity identity also includes environment and path.
The next experiment may prospectively test scoped collision admission against
the exact retained package and policy output. OT-0333 remains rejected.
