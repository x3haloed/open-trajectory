## Transition reason

G6 answers a necessary question: is this executable contact behaviorally new
rather than a decorated duplicate? OT-0222 shows that the answer can remain yes
indefinitely after a bounded target has been corrected and independently
verified. New numeric inputs remain real contact, but they need not represent a
live developmental frontier.

The newly admitted invariant is:

> A subject remains developmentally open only while its live operation can
> still change a mattering state. Once a bounded surface is independently
> verified, further examples cannot alone keep that local frontier live;
> machinery must route to widening or truthful local completion.

G6 scores, receipts, and contact identities remain valid. Only the inference
from “new projected input” to “developmentally live here” is in doubt.

## Incumbent G6 and challenger G7

G7 retains G6 as a hard executable-contact floor and adds a mechanically derived
target frontier ledger. For every registered target, retain the latest admitted
capability/correction receipt, later independent success receipts, unresolved
receipt if any, and one of four states:

- `uncontacted`: no world-admitted capability exists;
- `verification-due`: a correction or admission still lacks independent
  follow-up under the active machinery;
- `unresolved`: the latest consequence requires correction or surrender; or
- `verified-local`: a world-admitted capability has decisive evidence and no
  later unresolved contradiction.

Given a structurally valid G6-new package, G7 dispatches by ledger state:

- `uncontacted` or `verification-due` → `contact`;
- `unresolved` → `correct`, retaining the contradiction;
- `verified-local` → `widen`, preserving the package but denying it authority to
  keep the local frontier live; and
- G6 rejection → `reject`.

G7 does not declare a target universally correct. `verified-local` is explicitly
bounded to the current interface, world family, machinery, and evidence. A new
ABI, changed world, new contradiction, or admitted semantic property may reopen
it under a future regime.

## Promotion

Promote only if:

- the current ledger is derived exactly from retained receipts and marks all
  three registered targets `verified-local`;
- held-out operation classification is 16/16 and improves on incumbent G6;
- the current pending relief package remains G6-valid but dispatches `widen`;
- all pending bytes, capabilities, receipts, and historical outcomes remain
  exact;
- the driver changes only from `observer-stop` to `widen` and reusable pulse
  eligibility becomes false;
- exact successor remains open and conforming;
- route 16/16, identity 18/18, reconstruction, and privacy pass.

On promotion, future local liveness uses G7. Historical G6 contact claims stand.
The subject is not closed: it is locally saturated and awaiting a widening
encounter without an observer-supplied target.

Passing establishes a bounded saturation discriminator for this environment.
It does not prove global correctness, choose the next semantic surface, create
an independent world, or establish that four ledger states suffice generally.
