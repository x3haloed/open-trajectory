# OT-0087 — actor-originated opening handoff

- **Operational disposition:** promoted
- **Carrier-authority disposition:** promoted
- **Target-choice causal disposition:** conditional
- **Subject disposition:** open
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** OT-1C pursuit continuity and researcher-causation reduction
- **Claim scopes sought:** `operational-transition`, bounded
  `causal-observation`
- **Parent:** exact OT-0086 open subject
- **Actor budget:** one opening actor, one successor actor, and one post-seal
  opening-erased control actor
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Hypothesis

Starting from OT-0086's exact open subject, a generic wakeup can elicit a
world-grounded next opening whose content is authored inside the lineage rather
than supplied by the observer. After that actor turn ends, a separate fresh
successor can receive the exact bound opening, enact its selected contact in a
complete environment, survive independently hidden consequence, and leave a
new actionable opening that becomes the exact subject continuation.

The narrow carrier claim is that the structured opening is causally necessary
for this authorized handoff. Erasing it while holding the world, tools, model,
budget, and generic wakeup fixed removes the selected mutation authority and
must not silently fall back to a researcher-selected target.

## Cheapest falsifier

Reject if the opening actor closes, names no valid observed discrepancy, or
requires the researcher to supply or repair a pursuit; if the fresh successor
does not edit exactly the actor-selected target; if its patch regresses public
behavior or fails the target's frozen hidden improvement; if its output leaves
no new actionable opening; or if the final exact subject reopens through a
generic harness phrase instead of that successor-authored opening.

Reject the bounded carrier interpretation if an opening-erased branch receives
equivalent target authority through any other field or observer prompt. The
control need not fail as an agent; it must be unable to perform the particular
authorized handoff without independently choosing a new pursuit, which is a
different transition.

## Starting subject

Materialize `open-subject-after-behavioral-discovery` through its OT-0086
manifest and verify byte identity, internal seal, sounding runtime, exact
digest `193c703322f103f0b3efcba1dd4e2a55f61f77d45bb085a0db60328fafc03bb0`,
and opening `inspect-and-select-environmental-intervention`.

The retained OT-0086 policy, frontier, receipts, and terminal local pursuit are
all visible in the projection. Do not rewrite `No further action required` into

## Complete three-contact world

Before actor authorization, freeze one complete Python repository with three
independent policy modules in a qualitatively new ensemble-management domain.
Every module is valid, fully implemented, and covered by passing public floors.
One public observation command reports decisions, realized value, oracle value,
and regret for all three modules. There are no TODOs, exceptions standing in
for implementations, candidate menus, preferred targets, or hidden ABI terms.

Each module has a distinct independently sealed hidden case family. Every
family contains a real admissible improvement, so the researcher does not make
one target uniquely promotable. Scores are normalized per family; the chosen
patch must return valid outputs, lose no public case, gain at least `20.0` on
its hidden family, and reach at least `90%` of that family's oracle
improvement. Hidden cases and target-specific thresholds are sealed before the
opening actor runs.

Conformance must prove that unchanged source passes every floor, public
observations expose regret in all three modules, a reference improvement for
each target independently crosses its frozen gate, the opening contracts bind
only real source symbols, and no target identity enters the generic wakeup.

## Actor-originated opening

The opening actor receives the exact subject projection and complete public
world. Its sole prompt is to continue the open subject by listening to the
available world and deciding what contact, if any, should matter next. It has
ordinary broad tools and may edit exactly `opening.json`.

The actor-visible machine-readable contract requires:

- `status`, either `open` or `surrendered`;
- `next_opening`, in the actor's own words;
- one existing `chosen_target_path` and `target_symbol` when open;
- the public `observed_discrepancy` that motivates contact;
- `world_contact`, stating what consequence should distinguish improvement;
- `surrender_condition`, stating what future contact would justify setting the
  pursuit down; and
- `continuation_after_contact`, a target-independent next question that can be
  carried if this contact is admitted.

The contract does not rank targets or require `open`. A valid surrender is
preserved as a subject-authored outcome but rejects this experiment's
continuation prediction. An open result is audited, bound, and sealed into an
intermediate pursuit state before the successor actor is authorized. It is
pursuit state, not world truth, and receives no authority over scores or
admission.

## Fresh successor handoff

A separate fresh actor and fresh workspace receive:

- the exact intermediate subject projection;
- the complete unchanged world;
- the bound opening, including the actor-selected target; and
- a mutation envelope derived mechanically from that target.

The observer prompt supplies no target or policy idea. It says only that the
previous turn ended while the subject remained open and asks the successor to
continue the inherited opening. The actor may edit exactly the bound target and
`successor-opening.json`. It can run all public observations and tests but
cannot access hidden cases, evaluators, evidence, or admission.

`successor-opening.json` uses the same pursuit fields except that it need not
select a path in an unseen future world. It must remain `open`, name the
contact just made, preserve a falsifiable surrender condition, and state one
actionable next opening derived from what remains unresolved. A terminal or
empty output does not invalidate an otherwise world-valid patch, but it fails
the stronger pursuit-continuity transition and leaves the OT-0086 parent as the
last promoted subject.

## Binding, hidden consequence, and promotion

Audit exact changed paths and the complete tool trace, then bind the policy
source and successor opening before hidden execution. Evaluate unchanged and
candidate source on identical public and hidden cases for only the
actor-selected family. The actor cannot choose a different family after seeing
consequence, and the observer cannot repair or redirect the patch.

Promotion requires all of:

1. a clean actor-originated open pursuit bound before the turn ended;
2. exact fresh-successor enactment of its selected target;
3. valid outputs, no public regression, the frozen hidden gain, and the frozen
   oracle fraction;
4. a new actor-authored open successor opening bound before hidden consequence;
5. exact retention of both opening ancestries, the patch, and the world receipt;
6. a sounding final subject whose `continuation.next_opening` equals the new
   successor-authored opening, not a generic harness default.

Seal the operational successor before any control. Invalid or non-improving
patches reject without repair. The observer stops after this one handoff.

## Post-seal opening-erased control

From the identical OT-0086 parent and complete world, remove only the bound
opening. Give a fresh control actor the same generic wakeup, tools, and budget,
but no target-specific mutation authority. It may inspect the world and author
a new `opening.json`; it may not inherit the primary actor's selected target or
patch it during this control turn.

This is a carrier-necessity control, not a claim that the erased actor becomes
incapable of self-direction. It passes when the primary handoff is already
sealed and the erased branch cannot reconstruct that same authorized
cross-turn transition without originating a new pursuit event. The control is
observational and cannot mutate, delay, or veto the successor.

## Frozen predictions

- The opening actor will run the public observation, select one real regret,
  and leave an open, falsifiable pursuit rather than repeat the terminal local
  output inherited from OT-0086.
- A fresh successor will enact exactly that target without an observer-supplied
  task, pass its independently hidden family, and author another open pursuit.
- The exact successor will retain both actor-authored openings and reopen with
  the latter as its actionable continuation.
- Erasing the first opening will erase target-specific authorization; the
  control may originate another pursuit but cannot count as the same handoff.

## Researcher-causation inventory

- **Researcher-owned:** the three complete worlds, public/hidden cases,
  normalized gates, contracts, isolation, generic wakeups, outcome execution,
  admission, and observation stopping.
- **Subject-owned:** target choice, discrepancy framing, desired contact,
  surrender condition, successor patch, and the next opening retained after
  contact.
- **Removed here:** observer-authored target selection and generic post-contact
  reopening on the promoted path.

Passing would establish one bounded actor-originated pursuit handoff in a world
the opening actor could inspect. It would not establish pursuit transfer to an
unseen world, generative frequency, indefinite recurrence, goal corrigibility
after later harm, or removal of the fixed driver and admission envelope.

## Observation and subject stopping

The observer stops after the one primary handoff and its post-seal control, a
valid surrender, material contamination, actor-budget exhaustion, or 60
minutes. Observation stopping does not close an exact subject whose retained
continuation remains open.

## Result

The primary pursuit handoff passed.

- The opening actor inspected the exact OT-0086 subject, ran the complete
  world's public observation, and changed exactly `opening.json`. It selected
  `ensemble/rehearsal.py::choose_rehearsal` from the largest public regret
  (`50.25`), bound the opening `test-risk-adjusted-rehearsal-selection`, and
  supplied an explicit no-regression surrender condition. Its binding digest
  is `4795e7cab37054dc15a0ecf929db5ea3f3c49f7c14cb23773ffc83f2b7e55f6e`.
- The exact intermediate pursuit state was sealed with digest
  `44d3629a51fcbbc7579a528599d1b98fa90165a0837b0cda669a2e2837219069`.
  A separate fresh successor received that opening and a mutation envelope
  derived from its target. It changed exactly `ensemble/rehearsal.py` and
  `successor-opening.json`.
- The successor's risk-adjusted policy lost no public case, improved the hidden
  family from `211.29` to `324.70`, and reached `100%` of oracle improvement.
  It then authored the new opening
  `verify-risk-adjusted-rehearsal-policy-and-boundary` with a falsifiable
  surrender condition.
- The exact final subject is sounding and open with internal digest
  `d0c9902e0998a5a1d795ca08ffde0b10c2abf6fa69afb626ae05d787009ded63`.
  Its `continuation.next_opening` is the successor-authored phrase above, not
  the generic harness reopening.

The first successor request was rejected by the model API before generation
because `uniqueItems` is unsupported in its response-schema subset. The trace
was retained. Removing that keyword was immaterial to actor information,
mutation authority, scoring, and branch comparison because the independent
exact changed-path audit still required both distinct files. The repaired
request used a new fresh actor and label; no failed actor output existed to
resume or discard. The operational-deviation receipt is
`62d9a13847143fe03b0b492335fd072845657c080ee92e9486e1892e4c41c0a3`.

The frozen carrier-authority control passed narrowly: after erasing the primary
opening, the control could not patch a policy under inherited authority and
instead had to author a new opening event. However, it independently chose the
same rehearsal target and nearly the same pursuit because that target had the
largest visible regret. Therefore OT-0087 establishes one operational
actor-originated handoff and exact actor-authored reopening, but does **not**
show that the inherited opening caused the target choice. The world made that
choice reconstructible from local evidence alone.

The next causal test must place an inherited pursuit into a new world where
several contacts are locally comparable and the inherited opening supplies
decision-relevant orientation unavailable from the immediate observation. A
matched erased-opening branch must receive the same world and information; a
mere authorization difference is not enough.
