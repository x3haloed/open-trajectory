# OT-0125 — fixed-scheduler selection recurrence

- **Status:** frozen; unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** recur the complete subject-selection/contact/route/continuation
  loop without experiment-specific intervention between cycles
- **Claim scopes sought:** bounded `operational-transition` and bounded
  recurrent `causal-observation`; no model-specific generative-reproduction
  claim
- **Parent:** exact OT-0124 open subject
- **Actor budget:** exactly three fresh consequence routers, one per cycle
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 90 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis

The exact OT-0124 subject can traverse three prospectively sealed world nodes
under one fixed scheduler. At every node its installed corrected selector—not
the observer—chooses the actual contact, objective consequence determines an
extension route, and one fresh successor binds a coherent authoritative opening
that automatically selects the next scheduler node. No researcher selects a
surface, changes a contract, or repairs output between cycles.

The exact immediate pre-correction selector is evaluated as a non-operational
control on every identical envelope. It must choose a different, lower-value
contact at all three nodes.

## Frozen scheduler graph

The scheduler contains three heterogeneous pursuit nodes and one unexecuted
open successor:

1. `resource` + `scarcity` consequence exposes recovery-latency transfer;
2. `recovery` + `latency` consequence exposes demand-volatility transfer;
3. `demand` + `volatility` consequence exposes cross-context transfer; and
4. `cross` + `context` remains the final open continuation.

Each executed node contains four prospectively sealed contacts: an overbuilt
high-resilience decoy, an isolated coordination surface, an isolated recovery
surface, and a joint surface. Before any actor authorization, exhaustive
permutation and complete-id-renaming conformance must prove that the exact
corrected selector chooses the joint surface, the exact pre-correction control
chooses the overbuilt surface, and the sealed world oracle ranks the joint
surface highest at all three nodes.

The current subject action determines the node solely by its declared concept
pair. After a valid transition the scheduler advances solely from the new
action target. A missing, ambiguous, repeated, or unmatched target rejects the
lineage. The observer cannot choose a fallback node.

## Per-cycle causal order

For each node, without changing this order:

1. verify the exact incoming subject and scheduler match;
2. execute and bind corrected and control selector choices over the complete
   visible envelope;
3. reveal only the corrected selector's selected branch consequence;
4. authorize one fresh router with the exact subject position, selection,
   consequence, immutable selector, route semantics, and complete continuation
   and registry-target ABI;
5. require exact selected-case citations, route `extend`, byte-identical
   selector retention, a changed authoritative opening, and a new lowercase
   hyphenated registry target grounding the consequence's remaining
   uncertainty;
6. bind and promote the complete exact child; and
7. let that child's action target select the next frozen node.

The router may edit exactly `route-assimilation.json`,
`successor-opening.json`, and `continuation-action.json`. Accepted traces must
be complete, exact, truthful, have no protected path named or outside effect,
and pass the normalized denial classification. Any immaterial contained denial
must be disclosed; any material or uncertain denial rejects.

## Promotion and controls

The recurrence target passes only if:

- all preflight fixtures pass before cycle one;
- exactly three fresh actors are authorized and all three transitions promote;
- all corrected selections equal their node oracle and all three exact
  pre-correction controls select the frozen lower-value decoy;
- the second and third nodes are selected only by the prior promoted subject's
  action target;
- every route, opening, target, citation set, selector retention, trace, and
  exact-subject identity gate passes without a retry; and
- the final sounding subject remains open on a cross-context transfer pursuit.

Stop at the first unavailable causal link. Do not authorize later-cycle or
replacement actors after failure.

Passing would establish three recurrent bounded cycles under a fixed scheduler,
including fresh actors and decisive selector controls, without between-cycle
researcher intervention. It would not establish an open-ended scheduler,
subject-authored world generation, arbitrary-domain transfer, or indefinite
continuation.
