# OT-0218 — unresolved-consequence correction handoff

- **Status:** frozen; not yet run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** correction and reopening from an unresolved G5 world receipt
- **Parent:** exact OT-0217 live intermediate `ee5b552a...`
- **External consequence:** exact OT-0217 receipt `0dd175ff...`
- **Actor budget:** one corrector and one post-correction assimilator

## Question

Can an unresolved independent consequence improve the subject's active
capability through a fresh actor, then cause a different fresh actor to carry a
new executable contact across the next turn boundary?

The experiment begins after contact, not before it. Repeating OT-0217 with a
new originator or steering the actor toward recovery would discard the
developmental position that the subject actually reached.

## Starting state

The exact parent carries G5-live maintenance contact `895aa8ed...`, whose
actor-authored public cases match the installed criticality-only scheduler 1/4.
The independently sealed six-case world matches 2/6. Both receipts, the exact
pending package, the installed source, and the complete three-surface repository
are visible to the corrector. Hidden follow-up cases are not.

Before actor authorization, bind the external OT-0217 receipt into an
`outcome-unresolved` pending-state projection. This does not decide correction
or alter the installed capability; it prevents the subject from pretending the
contact is still unreceipted.

## Correction handoff

The fresh corrector may inspect and execute the complete repository. It edits
only `operations/system.py` and `correction-decision.json`. The decision must:

- retain, revise, or surrender the maintenance contact;
- bind the exact source and receipt identities;
- state fixed success/surrender/unresolved predicates;
- report the selected callable and changed function; and
- carry a conditional next pursuit.

Revision must change exactly `schedule_maintenance`, pass all four original
public cases against the visible reference, preserve every other function, and
bind exact patch bytes before follow-up consequence. Retention must explain how
1/4 + 2/6 leaves a still-live falsifiable condition and bind new cases that can
resolve it. Surrender must preserve the negative receipt and cannot silently
install a patch.

No observer repair or replacement actor is allowed.

## Follow-up world

Freeze six new maintenance-capacity cases before actor authorization. For a
revision, success requires 6/6 reference agreement while the unchanged control
remains at most 2/6; surrender requires the frozen surrender condition; all
other outcomes remain unresolved. Execute the changed function only after exact
binding and record all outputs.

Preflight reconstructs the original package at 1/4 and unresolved world at 2/6.
The frozen revision fixture reaches 4/4 original and 6/6 follow-up contact while
the unchanged follow-up control remains 2/6.

A valid corrected or surrendered subject seals before the second actor. An
unresolved follow-up remains open with its exact new receipt and does not start
assimilation.

## Consequence assimilation and reopening

After decisive follow-up consequence, a second fresh actor receives the exact
corrected/surrendered subject, both world receipts, correction binding, and the
complete repository. It must preserve the consequence-driven disposition and
bind one G5-valid contact on a callable other than `schedule_maintenance`.

The new package must contain four executable cases, at least two canonical
inputs absent from completed contact, and no independent receipt. The actor
chooses the target. The final subject records maintenance correction or
surrender, closes the old pending contact, and ends G5-live on the new package.

## Controls and promotion

Freeze exact ancestry, source identity, both OT-0217 receipts, original public
replay, new hidden cases, unchanged-source control, G5 duplicate/malformed
controls, both actor-visible checkers, both prospective compilers, route 16/16,
identity 18/18, and package replay before actors.

Both actor-visible checkers, target-only source audit, correction and surrender
shapes, both prospective compilers, and the exact parent/receipt reconstruction
pass before authorization.

Promotion requires:

- both fresh actors and audits accepted;
- exact assimilation of the unresolved receipt;
- a bound correction or surrender permitted by the contract;
- decisive follow-up world consequence against the unchanged control;
- no regression outside the selected function;
- exact retention of all earlier subject state and receipts;
- a different-target G5-valid pending package; and
- a conforming exact successor whose liveness is `live`.

The cheapest falsifier is the first failed receipt, contract, audit, patch,
world, control, assimilation, G5, replay, or identity gate. Failure preserves
the latest valid subject and every contradiction receipt.

## Claim boundary

A pass establishes one bounded correction cycle from unresolved world contact
through fresh-actor capability change, independent recontact, fresh
assimilation, and executable reopening. It does not establish recurrent
indefinite self-correction, arbitrary-world adaptation, or independence from
observer-authored environments and admission.
