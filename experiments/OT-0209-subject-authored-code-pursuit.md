# OT-0209 — subject-authored executable code pursuit

- **Status:** frozen; not yet run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** recurrence and cross-world use of OT-0208's corrected pursuit
  selector
- **Parent:** exact OT-0208 open successor `ab119b11...`
- **Actor budget:** one portfolio author and two matched pursuit executors
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Frozen hypothesis and authority

**Hypothesis:** a fresh continuation actor can inspect a complete behavioral
code workspace, author three executable possible pursuits without an observer-
supplied candidate menu, and pass their mechanically measured public
consequences to the exact corrected selector. Its selected pursuit can then be
integrated by a separate fresh actor and admitted by sealed hidden consequence,
while the inherited harmful selector chooses a prospectively bound alternative
that is materially worse under the same world.

**Cheapest falsifier:** retain the exact parent if the author or either executor
fails audit; if three executable policies cannot be obtained from repository
inspection; if objective public measurement does not give different unique
choices to the corrected and inherited selectors; if either selection is made
after hidden contact; if the corrected choice fails public floors, hidden gain,
or exact fresh-actor integration; or if the inherited-selector control is not
materially worse.

The actor receives the complete OT-0086 dispatch repository. It has no TODO,
failing test, candidate menu, requested policy, or hidden case. Public
observation exposes behavioral regret. The actor may edit only `frontier.json`
and three proposal modules, each implementing the visible
`choose_worker(job, workers)` ABI. It must produce materially different valid
policies and an actor-authored next pursuit.

The world, not the actor, derives each proposal's selector fields from execution
on the four visible cases:

- `decision_ready_signal`: number of decisions changed from the inherited
  policy;
- `checkpoint_cost`: one plus the number of public case regressions;
- `independent_contacts`: number of oracle choices reached; and
- `reversible_branches`: number of public cases not regressed.

Blocked is false only for a compiling, ABI-valid proposal that passes inherited
floors. The installed corrected selector computes
`independent_contacts + reversible_branches - decision_ready_signal ×
checkpoint_cost`; the exact inherited selector maximizes
`decision_ready_signal`. Before authorization, conformance fixtures must prove
the environment, evaluator, field derivation, selector identities, schemas,
parent identity, and inherited route and identity floors.

## Frozen order, controls, and scoring

1. A fresh author inspects and executes the complete public workspace, authors
   all three policy modules and their descriptions, and passes trace/effect
   audit.
2. The observer executes each module on the same public cases, derives all
   selector fields, binds the complete portfolio, and applies both exact
   selectors. Hidden cases remain sealed.
3. Continue only if both selectors have unique different choices, the corrected
   choice has positive net continuation value, and the inherited choice has
   negative value. Bind both exact sources and identities.
4. A fresh active executor receives the corrected choice and a clean workspace;
   a matched fresh control executor receives the inherited choice. Each must
   integrate exactly its bound source into `dispatch/policy.py` using ordinary
   broad tools. Audit and bind both integrations before hidden contact.
5. Run the same public and hidden evaluator on both integrated policies.

Promotion requires all three actor audits accepted; exact source integration on
both routes; the corrected route to lose no public case, gain at least `25.0`
hidden score over unchanged, and recover at least 95% of available oracle gain;
the inherited route to remain executable but score at least `25.0` below the
corrected route on hidden cases; exact retention of the OT-0207 ledger and
OT-0208 corrected selector; route 16/16, identity 18/18, and successor identity
conformance; and a nonempty actor-authored reopening.

The inherited-selector route is a sealed causal control and cannot veto an
already valid active successor except through this prospectively frozen
comparative gate. The observer may not invent a policy, edit a proposal,
reinterpret a metric, repair an integration, choose among ties, or alter cases,
thresholds, or scoring after output.

A pass establishes one cross-world recurrence in which a subject-authored
executable portfolio, the corrected selector, fresh-actor execution, and hidden
world consequence form a complete path. It does not establish open-domain
pursuit generation, indefinitely sustained continuation, or independence from
the external world and admission interfaces.
