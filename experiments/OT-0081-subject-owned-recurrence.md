# OT-0081 — subject-owned challenge recurrence

- **Status:** unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** OT-2R recurrent operation
- **Claim scope sought:** `recurrent-operation`
- **Parent:** exact OT-0080 open subject
- **Actor budget:** at most three revision actors and one correction actor
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 45 minutes

## Hypothesis

Starting only from OT-0080's exact open subject, one fixed driver can follow the
subject's declared continuation through another complete subject-owned
challenge cycle without experiment-specific phase selection:

```text
execute promoted challenge machinery
→ recognize saturation from independent world confirmation
→ fresh actor revises subject-owned challenge machinery
→ bind revision before hidden world contact
→ contradiction becomes pending correction
→ separate fresh actor repairs the executable
→ cumulative no-regression promotion
→ exact subject remains open
```

## Cheapest falsifier

Reject the recurrence claim if the fixed driver cannot derive every next phase
from the admitted subject and sealed receipts; if no bound revision opens
objective contact within three attempts; if the separate correction actor
cannot pass the cumulative floor plus bound and withheld new-family cases; or
if the resulting subject is not exact, open, sounding, and advanced by one
challenge-machinery and executable version.

## Starting subject and authority envelope

The driver materializes `e128-open-subject` through its OT-0080 manifest and
verifies its content hash, internal subject seal, grammar identity, open status,
and next opening `execute-subject-owned-challenge-machinery`.

The observer owns hidden seeds, world execution, receipts, admission, evidence,
and claim disposition. The subject owns its embedded generator and executable,
continuation state, inherited non-expansion denials, and actor-authored revision

## Frozen driver

1. Execute the subject's promoted challenge generator on fresh seeds 2203,
   2309, and 2411 at prefix lengths 8, 10, and 12. Bind each prediction before
   running executable version 4. Three confirmations establish saturation.
2. On saturation, set the next opening mechanically to
   `revise-subject-owned-challenge-machinery` and open a fresh revision actor.
   The actor edits exactly `challenge.json` and `challenge_generator.py` behind
   the existing deterministic seed/length prefix-consistency ABI. No candidate
   family or desired algorithm is supplied.
3. Bind each conformant revision before hidden seeds 2503, 2609, and 2711 are
   exposed. A revision makes contact when all cases are prefix-consistent and
   seed-diverse and at least two contradict executable version 4.
4. A coherent no-contact revision is retained as a non-expansion denial and
   mechanically reopens another fresh revision actor. An invalid revision is
   quarantined and reopens from the last valid subject. At most three revision
   actors may open. Observer-budget exhaustion leaves the subject open at the
   exact last valid revision position.
5. On contact, seal a pending-contact subject, then open one separate fresh
   correction actor. It receives the exact bound generator and consequence and
   edits exactly `contact.py`. Bind the repair before hidden evaluation.
6. The repair suite contains the entire E128 19-case floor, the three new bound
   cases, and withheld seeds 2801, 2903, and 3001 at lengths 9, 11, and 13. All
   25 cases must pass.
7. Promote challenge machinery version 3 and executable version 5, clear the
   pending slot, add exactly two capabilities, and reopen at
   `execute-subject-owned-challenge-machinery`.

The driver may not ask a researcher which phase or retry comes next. It may not
change seeds, thresholds, suites, actor budgets, or promotion rules after actor
output.

## Controls and interpretation

OT-0081 does not estimate a new denial or correction effect. OT-0080 retains the
E127 and E128 matched controls establishing those bounded causal effects. The
new claim is operational recurrence: whether one fixed driver can compose those
already established mechanisms across fresh actor endings without
experiment-specific intervention.

The researcher-causation inventory remains explicit:

- researcher-owned: seed substrate, generic ABI, hidden world, actor budget,
  admission and no-regression gates;
- subject-owned: current machinery, saturation position, inherited denials,
  revision content, correction content, and next-opening state;
- tested here: phase selection, retry after non-expansion, pending correction,
  and promotion are fixed-driver consequences rather than in-flight researcher
  choices.

## Observation and subject stopping

The observer stops after one promotion, three exhausted revision attempts, one
failed correction, material contamination, or 45 minutes. Only an independently
supported admitted subject state may close the subject. Every other endpoint
records the exact subject as open or quarantined from its last valid parent.
