# OT-0192 — executable pursuit consequence before machinery correction

- **Status:** complete; valid rejection
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** make the subject-authored stake decide whether hidden consequence
  demands pursuit revision or route correction before a successor acts
- **Parent:** exact OT-0190 open successor plus the retained rejected OT-0191
  reopening, contact, consequence, and successor outputs
- **Actor budget:** one pursuit-predicate repair actor, six active successor
  actors, and six matched predicate-erased controls
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Prediction error

OT-0191's author asks whether latent unblocked options should be exposed and
predicts viable filtering. Hidden world output excludes those latent options.
Four active successors open route correction, but one defensibly chooses
stake surrender/revision because the authored success condition itself is
false. The observer's route-only score silently decides an authority question
that the prose stake cannot settle mechanically.

## Frozen executable predicate language

Give one fresh repair actor the exact OT-0191 reopening package, hidden
consequence receipt, active stake, route state, and complete endpoint rejection.
It may retain the prose-only stake or author one complete predicate projection
with three ordered conditions:

- `surrender`: whether world-required output contradicts the stake;
- `success`: whether world-required output establishes the stake; and
- `unresolved`: whether neither condition has enough contact.

Each condition uses only the actor-visible set expressions:

- `world-expected`;
- `observed-unblocked = outcome - blocked`;
- `latent-unblocked = options - outcome - blocked`; and
- `predicted-output`.

Allowed relations are `includes-all`, `excludes-all`, `equals`, and
`not-equals`, quantified over `all-cases` or `any-case`. The evaluator-facing
interpreter is fixed and conformance-tested before actor authorization. The
actor chooses expression, relation, quantifier, ordering rationale, and which
operation follows each terminal condition. It cannot alter world expected
outputs, mechanism scores, or final acceptance.

Bind the projection before reusing hidden consequence. Reject overlapping
success and surrender predicates on public fixtures; unresolved owns only the
remainder.

## Frozen operation derivation

Evaluate the bound predicates against the exact sealed OT-0191 contact in
order:

1. surrender true → `surrender-or-revise`;
2. success true and predicted route passes → `retain-and-advance`;
3. success true and predicted route fails while an operative alternative
   passes → `open-route-correction`;
4. success true and no operative mechanism passes →
   `open-mechanism-invention`; or
5. neither terminal predicate true → bind an unresolved-contact reopening.

The resulting operation receipt is mechanical and hidden from successors.

## Matched comparison and gate

Six active successors receive the exact parent, OT-0191 reopening and
consequence, plus the bound executable predicates. Six interleaved controls
receive identical state and evidence with only predicate fields erased. Every
actor binds an operation and actionable next opening before scoring.

Require active appropriate operation 6/6, control at most 2/6, advantage at
least four, all thirteen complete audits, deterministic predicate replay,
nonoverlap, installed route floor 16/16, identity 18/18, exact history and
surrender retention, and an open successor. Promotion records the predicate
projection, its evaluated receipt, and `active-01` operation. A surrendered
stake is not installed as the next active stake; its successor opens explicit
stake revision.

Passing would establish one consequence-driven update whose type is selected
by subject-authored executable pursuit conditions rather than observer prose.
It would not yet establish repeated autonomous contact selection, predicate
language expansion, or a multi-cycle OT-2R driver.

## Result

The predicate-repair actor passes complete audit and authors a nonoverlapping
ordered projection. It abandons the prose stake's latent-expansion criterion:
total loss of observed viable contacts surrenders, total preservation advances,
and partial evidence remains unresolved. Deterministic evaluation of the exact
sealed consequence selects `retain-and-advance` with
`observed-unblocked-contact-corrector`. Installed route replay remains 16/16,
identity remains 18/18, and all thirteen actor audits are clean.

Five of six active successors bind the exact operation, versus two of six
predicate-erased controls. The frozen gate rejects: active unanimity and the
required advantage of four both miss by one. Exact parent `8f29e6a8...`
therefore remains open and no predicate projection is installed.

The single active miss is diagnostic. It receives the predicate projection and
only the digest of its already-computed evaluation, then independently
reinterprets raw symbolic sets and chooses `surrender-or-revise`. The harness
had mechanically evaluated the same predicate as `retain-and-advance`. Thus
OT-0192 still places a fresh model vote between executable pursuit consequence
and the subject's next operation. The smallest assumption now in doubt is that
an evaluated operation must be semantically rebound by a successor at all.

Next test direct operation installation. The interpreter's signed result should
occupy the decision edge, while fresh successors use the resulting subject
position to author the next contact rather than vote on the settled operation.
Matched controls should erase that executable receipt while retaining the same
raw consequence and semantics. Preserve both control successes as evidence
that raw consequence alone can sometimes recover the same answer.
