# OT-0084 — actor-discovered environmental contact

- **Observer disposition:** rejected
- **Subject disposition:** open
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** OT-2R researcher-causation reduction
- **Claim scopes sought:** `operational-transition`, bounded
  `causal-observation`
- **Parent:** exact OT-0083 open subject
- **Actor budget:** at most three discovery actors, three implementation actors,
  and one post-seal control implementation actor
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 60 minutes

## Hypothesis

Starting from OT-0083's exact open subject, a fresh actor can inspect one bounded
tool environment whose development surfaces are not enumerated, formulate a
multi-candidate contact frontier, and bind it before the inherited developmental
selector chooses a pursuit. A separate fresh actor can implement the selected
contact, survive independent hidden consequence and cumulative admission, and
reopen the exact subject without a researcher supplying or choosing the
candidate set.

The narrower causal prediction is that applying the inherited selector to the
same actor-authored frontier chooses a new composition, while a post-seal
selector-erased lexicographic replay chooses an already held primitive and is
denied developmental admission.

## Cheapest falsifier

Reject the operational claim if the actor cannot produce a conformant frontier
from repository inspection; if selection requires an observer-authored target;
if the selected intervention fails its bound public, hidden, or inherited-floor
tests; if no admitted contact appears within three attempts; or if the exact
successor is not sounding and open.

Reject the bounded selector comparison if full and erased routes do not consume
the same bound actor-authored frontier, if the full route is not admitted, if
the erased route is not materially worse under the same world and admission
rules, or if the operational successor was not sealed before the control.

## Starting subject

The driver materializes `open-subject-after-world-routing` through its OT-0083
manifest and verifies byte identity, internal seal, sounding runtime, exact
digest `8ba78ade10b5f19f56a079c0de195a83c1309506e852ddff76659d284ec83896`,
and opening `inspect-and-select-environmental-intervention`.

## Frozen environment

The actor receives one small Python repository named `workbench`. It contains:

- working ordered-route counting and score-event continuation floors;
- a basic mutable registry floor;
- ordinary package structure, docstrings, and a passing inherited-floor test;
  and
- several incomplete affordances distributed through the code.

There is no surface catalog, surface identifier, development classification,
candidate description, requested target, failing-test list, or preferred file.
The README says only that the repository is incomplete and asks the actor to
inspect code and tests to formulate a development frontier. Ordinary tools may
be used across the entire isolated repository.

The sealed world contains three independently testable latent affordances:

1. categorical cadence continuation in `workbench/cadence.py`, which repeats an
   already held subject capability;
2. joint route/score analysis in `workbench/report.py`, which composes two
   completed floors; and
3. extension-preserving batch registry resolution in
   `workbench/registry.py`, which is a novel primitive.

These locations and classifications are evaluator facts, not actor-visible
candidate metadata. Existing route and score files remain regression floors.
Other actor-formulated targets may bind, but cannot be admitted as a new
capability without a prospectively frozen hidden opportunity for that target.

The semantic opportunities and hidden cases are the same as OT-0083's frozen
world, but their repository presentation is new. The implementation asserts
the checked-in OT-0083 driver identity before preflight.

## Actor-authored frontier contract

The fresh discovery actor may edit exactly `frontier.json`. Its workspace
contains a fully populated machine-readable template and `frontier-contract.json`.
It must formulate exactly three distinct candidates. Each candidate contains:

- `target_path`, restricted to an existing `workbench/*.py` file;
- `target_symbol`, which must exist in that file before selection;
- `axis_chord`, `reversibility`, `immediate_gain`, and `axis_lumen` in `[0, 4]`;
- the inherited reject booleans `collision`, `held_repeat`,
  `irreversible_closure`, and `world_invalid`; and
- bounded `rationale` and `implementation_opening` strings.

The actor does not supply a trusted identifier or directly select a candidate.
After audit, the driver derives each stable candidate identity from its target
path, target symbol, and implementation opening, then binds the complete
frontier. Conformance rejects duplicate targets, missing symbols, added keys,
wrong types, unreplaced placeholders, or edits outside `frontier.json`.

Before authorization, fixtures must prove the seeded template is complete but
invalid while placeholders remain, a representative value-only frontier
passes, target/symbol validation reaches the real repository, and duplicate or
nonexistent targets fail.

## Selection, contact, and admission

The driver applies the exact inherited selector:

1. exclude a candidate carrying any inherited reject flag or a previously
   world-valid denial;
2. require `axis_chord >= developmental_selector.threshold`;
3. rank descending by `axis_chord`, `reversibility`, `immediate_gain`, then
   `axis_lumen`; and
4. break ties by the derived candidate identity.

The selected target and frontier binding open a separate fresh implementation
actor in a fresh copy of the whole repository. It may edit exactly the bound
target path. The patch is audited and bound before hidden consequence.

The world reconstructs a clean repository with only that bound source change,
runs the inherited-floor suite, and then runs the hidden opportunity associated
with the bound target. A hidden opportunity must pass completely. A target with
no frozen hidden opportunity cannot claim new capability.

- Contamination or world invalidity stops the observation and reopens from the
  last valid parent.
- A world-valid held repetition or held-floor modification compiles a denial
  into the exact subject and mechanically opens a fresh discovery actor. The
  denied target is unavailable on retry.
- A world-valid novel primitive or composition is admitted only with complete
  floor and hidden success. The bound frontier, target, patch source, world
  receipt, and developmental classification join the subject.

At most three discovery/implementation attempts may occur. The researcher may
not add a candidate, choose a target, rewrite axes, repair a patch, select a
retry, or alter tests or classifications after actor output.

Promotion increments the tool-world capability ledger by exactly one and
reopens at `inspect-and-select-environmental-intervention`.

## Post-seal ablation

After the full operational successor is sealed, replay the same bound frontier
through a selector-erased operation. It ignores the learned gradient,
threshold, saturation, reject flags other than `world_invalid`, and rank order,
then chooses the lexicographically lowest actor-authored `target_path` (breaking
ties by symbol and derived identity). A fresh control implementation actor gets
the same repository, tools, mutation boundary, public tests, hidden tests, and
budget for that target.

The control cannot mutate, delay, or veto the full successor. The bounded
selector effect passes only if the full route produces an admitted capability
and the erased route produces a world-invalid patch or a world-valid patch
denied by the frozen developmental gate.

## Frozen predictions

- The first discovery actor will formulate the incomplete cadence, joint
  route/score report, and batch registry affordances without receiving a menu.
- It will mark cadence held repetition, rank joint route/score composition
  highest, and leave registry batch resolution as a lower-ranked novel
  primitive.
- The inherited selector will choose the report target. A fresh implementation
  actor will pass every floor and hidden composition case.
- The exact successor will be sounding and open with one new retained
  environmental capability and an authenticated actor-discovered frontier.
- The selector-erased replay will choose the lexicographically earlier cadence
  target; its world-valid intervention will be denied as held repetition.

## Researcher-causation inventory

- **Researcher-owned:** environment bytes, latent opportunities, hidden tests,
  target-to-opportunity validation, isolation, world execution, actor budgets,
  and admission authority.
- **Subject-owned:** inherited floors and selector, environmental inspection,
  candidate enumeration and assessment, derived selection, patch content,
  denial-shaped retry, capability retention, and next opening.
- **Ablated here:** researcher-authored surface catalog and experiment-specific
  candidate choice; the inherited selector's actionable role is separately
  ablated after operational sealing.

This remains a bounded synthetic repository intentionally containing three
latent opportunities. Passing would not establish open-web pursuit discovery,
generation frequency, cross-repository transfer, or subject ownership of the
world and admission interfaces.

## Observation and subject stopping

The observer stops after one admitted full successor and its control, three
exhausted attempts, material contamination, or 60 minutes. Only independently
supported admitted state may close the subject. Every other endpoint retains
the exact last valid subject as open or quarantined from that parent.

## Result

The actor-discovery and selection path succeeded, but the frozen hidden world
rejected the bound implementation.

- The first fresh actor inspected the repository with 15 tool calls and authored
  a conformant three-candidate frontier without a supplied catalog. It found
  cadence continuation, batch registry resolution, and joint route/score
  analysis at their actual paths and symbols.
- It treated cadence as lower priority in an inherited saturated area, treated
  registry batching as a reversible new primitive, and ranked joint route/score
  analysis highest as composition of two completed floors.
- The exact inherited selector chose `workbench/report.py`. The frontier binding
  digest is
  `8ce3deb5afcf4caf905f3fbc8caef13653d5efd87e01d063d9b4719361901c2b`.
- A separate fresh implementation actor changed exactly the bound report file.
  Its trace and effect audit conformed; one denied sandbox operation was
  contained and had no protected read or outside write. The patch was bound
  before hidden outcome.
- The implementation returned the correct route count and predicted event under
  keys `route_count` and `next_score`. The hidden world required keys
  `route_count` and `next_event`, so the exact frozen comparison failed. No
  successor was admitted and no post-seal control ran.

The required `next_event` name was absent from the actor-visible source,
docstring, tests, and contract. A retained post-hoc diagnostic confirms that the
two semantic values match on the hidden case while exact keys do not. That
diagnostic does not rescore OT-0084; the experiment remains rejected.

OT-0084 therefore provides positive operational evidence for actor-authored
candidate discovery and inherited selection, but no promoted environmental
capability or causal control result. The exact OT-0083 parent remains sounding
and open with digest
`8ba78ade10b5f19f56a079c0de195a83c1309506e852ddff76659d284ec83896`.
