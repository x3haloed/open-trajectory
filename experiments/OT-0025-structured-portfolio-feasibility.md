# OT-0025 — Structured-decision portfolio feasibility

- **Status:** implementation freeze pending; actor output forbidden
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target authority:** none; development feasibility only
- **Predecessors:** OT-0023 and OT-0024 free-form decision-carrier failures

## Hypothesis

OT-0023 and OT-0024 each produced three valid selector alternatives but failed
before comparison because free-form multiway decision text was unreliable. An
exact structured decision list may preserve actor authorship without semantic
compilation: the actor authors clause order, indexed choice, integer error-
advantage threshold, and requirements for changed selection and prediction;
the controller only evaluates those clauses in order.

Thresholds range from -16 through 16 rather than being forced positive, so the
schema does not guarantee credit causation. The frozen gate still requires the
actor-authored list to choose a useful alternative under true paired outcomes
and current under complete error-credit neutralization.

## Cheapest falsifier, controls, and scoring

Before hosted output, reject the representation unless an exact known output
produces three distinct programs, at least two selection sets, a useful cross-
half alternative on the fresh XOR public relation, a deterministic true-credit
commit, and a neutralized current choice. Reject duplicate alternatives,
duplicate/missing choices, out-of-bound thresholds, invalid Boolean fields,
receipt tampering, budget failures, or any sealed-split prompt leak.

Then exactly two fresh Luna encounters run. Each must author three distinct
selectors and a three-choice clause permutation in one turn. The chosen
alternative must change selection and gain at least four errors; neutralization
must choose current. Every freshness, tool, exact model, inventory, Response,
ETag, collector, replay, identity, resource, test, audit, privacy, and evidence
gate must pass.

## Claim limit and freeze boundary

A pass establishes public non-candidate representation feasibility only. It
does not promote OT-1, renew E4, or authorize a private candidate. Actor output
is forbidden until a clean implementation commit and separate run-lock commit
bind every authority. OT-0025 is not adjusted after output. If this exact
structured carrier also fails, no further public carrier retry is authorized
without a materially different hypothesis.
