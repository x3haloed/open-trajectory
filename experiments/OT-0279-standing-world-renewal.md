## Frozen hypothesis

An exact waiting subject can carry a generic renewal policy that derives
`renew-world-feed` only when its standing feed is empty. One null opening then
authorizes a fresh world-side actor without an experiment-specific researcher
choosing a world, target, pursuit, or package content. The actor receives no
subject state, ledger, prior world identities, or sealed lineage evidence.

The resulting world must pass the already-published restricted-package checker,
contain three independently named executable surfaces, and place each visible
policy at exactly 2/6 against its sealed reference. Only after output may the
observer compare world and target identity with the exact subject. The standing
scanner must then observe the public package as unseen.

## Frozen gates and controls

Before actor authorization, the installed renewal policy must preserve the
subject's operational core, fifth wait, route floor, identity floor, and exact
opening. With renewal enabled, an empty feed derives `renew-world-feed`; with
the policy disabled, the same exact subject and feed derive `wait-provider` and
authorize zero actors. A nonempty feed must derive ordinary wake rather than
another provider actor.

The published example must pass the checker and scanner. Imports, attributes,
matching visible/reference code, wrong source or case counts, escaping paths,
and extra fields must fail. The seeded actor corpus must exclude the parent
digest, every consumed world id, and every ledger target. Promotion requires
one exact actor effect, G10 containment, three post-hoc novel targets, exact
2/6 consequence on all three, scanner availability, false external authority,
and an exact open waiting successor carrying the renewal policy. No retry is
permitted.

## Limits fixed before output

This tests standing, non-researcher-specific world provision and its causal
contrast, not wake, subject selection, learning, or recurrence through the new
world. The world-side actor is still an external source of objective contact;
the claim is that experiment-specific human world choice is no longer needed,
not that the subject authors its own consequences.

## Result

The sole live null opening derives `renew-world-feed`; the renewal-disabled
control remains at the exact wait with zero actors. One fresh subject-blind
scout authors `morrowglass-civic-ledger-v1`, changes exactly the package file,
reports it truthfully, and passes G10. The frozen observer nevertheless rejects
before scanner admission because executable evaluation returns `execution`.
No retry occurs and OT-0278's exact fifth-wait subject remains current.

The retained package passes the actor-visible published checker and all three
surfaces are exactly 2/6 there. Post-hoc diagnosis finds a context-dependent
evaluator defect: `safe_module` obtains allowed calls such as `max` through
attribute access on `__builtins__`. The checker process receives a module, but
the imported lineage evaluator receives a dictionary, so `max` is absent only
when the observer executes `levy_due`. This is not rescored. A prospective
evaluator must use the `builtins` module explicitly, prove script/import parity
over every allowed call, then reconsider these exact retained bytes without a
new world actor.
