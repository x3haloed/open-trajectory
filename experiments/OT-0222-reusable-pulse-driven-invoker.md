# OT-0222 — reusable pulse-driven invoker

- **Status:** completed; promoted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** reusable cross-invocation OT-1C machinery
- **Parent:** exact OT-0221 open subject `b5c955a4...`
- **Actor budget:** at most four fresh actors across two invocations; no replacement
- **Observer budget:** two calls to one unchanged reusable invoker

## Realization contract

**Capability:** given an exact open subject at `observer-stop`, one reusable
operation must apply a content-free pulse, expose the subject's already-bound
contact to independent consequence, dispatch any required correction through a
provided fixed callback, assimilate decisive consequence with fresh actors, and
return an exact open subject at `observer-stop` with new G6-live contact.

**Envelope:** the OT-0220/0221 three-function Python/JSON environment, G6
admission, fresh isolated Luna actors, one contact per invocation, two serial
invocations, retained raw evidence, and exact subject identity. Each invocation
may use at most one corrector and one assimilator. Invalid or unresolved paths
return the last exact subject and a typed stop reason; they are never retried.

**Accepted revisions:** actor-authored contact content and target choice may
differ. **Exclusions:** semantic expansion, indefinite running, autonomous wake
timing, new world construction, and a live correction event in this specific
observation. **Evidence horizon:** deterministic fixtures, unit tests, two real
world contacts, two real fresh actors unless correction is required, exact
artifacts, and audit traces. **Risk frontier:** the same reusable function must
carry the second exact successor without experiment-specific phase code.

Central causal truth: an eligible exact subject causes a content-free pulse;
its pending contact causes sealed world consequence; that consequence causes a
fresh actor's G6-valid reopening; the returned exact subject is eligible for
the same operation again.

## Frozen machinery

Move pulse eligibility, receipt construction, phase dispatch, failure returns,
correction callback routing, assimilation, compilation, and observer stop into
`open_trajectory_harness.continuation_pulse.continue_once`. Experiment code may
provide only stable authority callbacks: seal/digest, world contact, world
compilation, correction, assimilation, pending compilation, and observer stop.
It may not select a target or branch around an actor result.

Call that unchanged function twice. Each call grants one encounter and at most
two actor allowances. On decisive success, one fresh assimilator must bind a
different-target G6-valid contact. On unresolved contact, the same function may
invoke the fixed OT-0220 correction path and then assimilate; failure preserves
the last exact subject and rejects the observation. Each completed call ends at
`observer-stop`, not subject closure.

## Freeze, controls, and promotion

Before live output, freeze base/parent/receipt identities, callback set, task
order, worlds, budgets, scoring, and gates. Unit and conformance fixtures must
cover ineligible input, exact pulse locality, decisive success, unresolved
without correction, successful correction routing, rejected actor output,
second-call reuse, G6 replay, route 16/16, identity 18/18, and privacy.

The cheapest falsifier is inability to call the same function on its own exact
first output. Do not replace an actor or write per-invocation phase logic.
Controls remove pulse eligibility and inject task-bearing pulse content; both
must reject. Preserve an exact intermediate subject before the second call.

Promote only if both calls complete through the same invoker identity, two
independent worlds are decisive, every opened actor is fresh and accepted, both
outputs conform, adjacent contacts use different targets, the final subject is
open at observer-stop with G6-live unreceipted contact, all controls/floors pass,
and the complete path is reconstructible. Score `(completed invocations,
decisive worlds, fresh accepted actors, exact reopenings)`.

Passing establishes reusable bounded recurrence rather than semantic growth.
Repeated parameter contact in a fully corrected three-function repository may
be stagnation; saturation and widening remain the next frontier.

## Result

Promoted. The same shared invoker SHA `9eaf9375...` completed two serial calls,
with its exact first output used as the second input. Invocation one exposed the
pending relief package to independent 6/6 consequence and a fresh actor reopened
recovery. Invocation two exposed that recovery package to independent 6/6
consequence and another fresh actor reopened relief. Both adjacent targets
changed, both actors were accepted, and both exact outputs conform.

Exact successor `5a205d35...` is open, pulse-eligible, at `observer-stop`, and
G6-live with unreceipted contact `64a3aa8e...`. Every frozen gate and control
passes. This realizes reusable cross-invocation continuation for the decisive
success path; no experiment-specific phase or target code was added between
calls.

The result also makes the next weakness concrete. Encounters four and five
alternate relief and recovery after all three installed functions are already
correct. G6 correctly distinguishes new behavioral inputs, but those inputs may
only prolong local confirmation. Reusability is promoted; semantic expansion is
not. The next experiment must make saturation decision-relevant before allowing
the reusable invoker to continue indefinitely.
