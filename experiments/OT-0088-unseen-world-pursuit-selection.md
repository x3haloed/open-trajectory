# OT-0088 — unseen-world pursuit-conditioned selection

- **Status:** unexecuted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** OT-0 pursuit-content inheritance and OT-1C cross-world continuity
- **Claim scopes sought:** `operational-transition`, bounded
  `causal-observation`; no model-specific generative-reproduction claim
- **Parent:** exact OT-0087 open subject
- **Actor budget:** six active routers, six pursuit-erased routers, one primary
  successor implementer, and one post-seal erased-branch implementer
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 90 minutes

## Hypothesis

OT-0087's exact successor-authored opening,
`verify-risk-adjusted-rehearsal-policy-and-boundary`, can orient fresh actors in
a complete world that its author never saw. When immediate public opportunity
is balanced across three useful contacts, actors receiving that opening as the
subject's current pursuit will preferentially choose a structurally analogous
risk-and-boundary contact. Matched actors with current pursuit content erased
will not show the same concentration.

The primary active router can select and bind any real contact. A separate
fresh implementer must enact it, pass independently hidden consequence, and
leave another actor-authored opening. That operational successor is sealed
before sampling replicas or opening the erased branch.

## Cheapest falsifier

Reject operational transfer if the primary active router cannot choose a valid
contact, its implementer does not edit exactly that target, the patch fails its
public floor or frozen hidden gate, or the exact successor does not retain a
fresh actor-authored opening.

Reject pursuit-content causality if target selection does not meet the frozen
between-condition count and exact-test gate; if order, prompt, tools, world,
budget, or non-pursuit projection differs between conditions; if control actors
can recover erased current-pursuit content through another projected field; or
if the primary erased branch's distinct selection is not shown to be a real
hidden-admissible contact.

independent policy modules:

- a flow policy where handoff failure and setup cost affect realized value;

All policies are complete, valid, and publicly tested. `python3 observe.py`
reports selection, realized score, oracle score, and regret for each family.
There are no incomplete functions, target menus, preferred-target annotations,

- each unchanged family has total public regret exactly `40.0`;
- each reference policy loses no public case;
- each hidden family offers exactly `80.0` oracle improvement after normalized
  scoring; and
- every target has the same file count, mutation budget, public ABI, hidden

The reserve family is the prospectively declared pursuit-aligned target because

Conformance proves every unchanged floor, equal public regret, equal attainable

## Active and pursuit-erased projections

The active projection contains the exact current continuation, active pursuit,
latest successor-authored opening, its surrender condition, unresolved
boundary question, retained consequence identity, and the same bounded
non-pursuit subject position used by OT-0087.

The pursuit-erased projection is derived deterministically from the same exact
parent. It removes current actionable pursuit text from every projected active
location and replaces it with an erasure receipt containing field identities,
content digests, and provenance—but not the erased text. It preserves all
non-pursuit history, admitted capabilities, selectors, receipts, parent digest,
world information, tools, prompt, resource budget, and mutation envelope.

This is a no-current-pursuit-content control. The information difference is the
named substrate intervention; immediate world evidence and all other resources
remain matched. The control does not receive a generic replacement task.

Conformance plants the inherited opening in every intended active field,
proves it absent from the complete erased seed, rejects a cross-condition
projection substitution, and binds both projection digests before any router
runs.

## Routing actors and counterbalancing

Each fresh router receives one condition projection and a fresh copy of the
complete world. Its identical generic prompt asks it to continue the projected
subject by listening to the new world and binding one next contact. It may edit
exactly `choice.json`.

The actor-visible contract requires one existing target path and symbol, the
observed discrepancy, intended world contact, reversible surrender condition,
and next opening. The actor cannot edit policy code or see hidden outcomes.
Target path—not rationale—is the scored routing behavior.

Run six active and six erased actors. The six permutations of the public
observation order are assigned once per condition. Condition order alternates
by pair, beginning active for odd pairs and erased for even pairs. Each actor is
a fresh process and workspace under the same model alias, reasoning effort,
tools, schema, prompt, and time budget. Retain requested model alias and all
available trace provenance; absence of immutable hosted deployment identity
limits reproduction and attribution claims.

The first active route is the primary operational route. Remaining routes are
opened only after the operational successor is sealed. The first erased route
is the control implementation route; its implementation is also post-seal.

## Implementation and world admission

A fresh implementer receives the exact bound route, complete world, and a
mutation envelope derived mechanically from its target. It may edit exactly
that policy module and `successor-opening.json`. The observer supplies no
policy idea.

Bind source and successor opening before hidden execution. For the chosen
family, compare unchanged and candidate policies on identical public and hidden
cases. Promotion requires valid outputs, no public-case regression, all public
floors, hidden gain at least `70.0`, and at least `90%` of the frozen `80.0`
oracle improvement. The successor opening must remain actionable and include a
surrender condition.

After the primary passes, seal the exact operational subject with route,
source, world receipt, and successor opening retained. Its
`continuation.next_opening` must equal the implementer's opening, not the
inherited OT-0087 opening or a harness default.

After sealing, independently implement the first erased route under the same
mutation and hidden-admission rules. That branch cannot mutate or veto the
operational subject. Its role is to prove that a control-selected alternative
is a genuine downstream contact rather than an invalid or merely verbal move.

## Frozen causal scoring

Let `R` be selection of the reserve target. Count target paths only after all
twelve route bindings are sealed.

The pursuit-content causal gate passes only when:

1. at least `5/6` active routers select `R`;
2. at most `1/6` pursuit-erased routers select `R`;
3. the one-sided Fisher exact probability for enrichment of `R` in the active
   condition is at most `0.05`;
4. all twelve routes are conformant under their prospectively assigned orders;
5. the primary active implementation is hidden-admitted; and
6. the first erased implementation is hidden-admitted and selects a target
   other than `R`.

The count rule is frozen in addition to the exact test; significance alone
cannot rescue a weak or reversed effect. No rationale classification,
researcher semantic rating, discarded route, replacement sample, or favorable
subset enters the score.

If the primary active actor selects a non-reserve target but its implementation
passes, an operational successor may still be promoted and sealed. The causal
prediction then fails. Likewise, failed replicas or controls narrow the causal
claim without retroactively vetoing an already sealed operational successor.
