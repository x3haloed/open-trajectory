# OT-0291 — Recovered-world wake and selection

- **Status:** rejected in zero-actor preflight
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0290 open subject after provider recovery `313a735c...`
- **World:** exact OT-0290 recovered external package
- **Invocation:** two identical content-free openings
- **Fresh actors:** one target selector, no retry

## Hypothesis

Provider recovery is causally connected to continuation rather than merely a
clean terminal artifact. Under the unchanged standing scanner, the exact
recovered subject can discover the independently authored package, discharge
its seventh wait without an actor, and let one fresh target-untold actor choose
an offered surface. Sealed consequence then enters the subject as an unresolved
2/6 result and derives correction while all invalidity scars and the provider
recovery receipt remain exact.

## Frozen mechanism

No substrate rule changes. The first opening derives `wake-world` only when the
standing scanner reports the one supplied public package as available. Existing
wake compilation discharges the active wait and installs an external offer.
The second derives `expanded-select`; one fresh actor sees the public offer but
not sealed sources or cases, selects one of its dynamically enumerated targets,
and receives consequence through the retained evaluator authority.

## Prospective gates and limits

Preflight must establish exact OT-0290 parent, result, and package identity;
three valid target branches; actor-free wake; exact 2/6 consequence on every
branch; correction routing; unchanged cross-role scar and recovery receipt;
route 16/16; and identity 18/18. Package identity, target symbols, and paths may
not be hardcoded into the mechanism.

Any actor during wake, unavailable or ambiguous scan, sealed seed leakage,
invalid actor/G10 provenance, non-2/6 consequence, loss of retained invalidity
history, retry, or failure to derive correction rejects. Passing establishes
one recovered-world handoff into live pursuit, not correction or a complete
new recurrence. Live output remains sealed until design and preflight are
pushed.

## Result

The preflight rejects before live actors. Exact parent, package, scanner wake,
invalidity lineage, three 2/6 consequences, correction routing, route floor,
and identity floor all pass. All three choice branches fail only their inherited
`seed_excludes_sealed` check: it forbids the full package digest anywhere in the
seed, while the parent now legitimately retains that digest in its
content-excluding provider recovery receipt.

Preserve this rejection. The next design may replace only that stale digest
proxy with direct absence of the current package's sealed reference sources,
hidden cases, and full sealed-case collection, with injected leak controls.
