# OT-0322 — Executable search retention and reuse

- **Status:** frozen
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0321 subject `2ea43872...`
- **Claim:** causally successful proposal-search machinery can become an exact
  inherited capability and improve a fresh actor's correction in a new regime
- **Actors:** retained-source reuse actor first, source-erased actor second; one
  draw each, no retry

## Prediction error and mechanism

OT-0321 promotes cumulative selector improvement and retains the search result,
causal receipts, and exact search-tool digest. It does not retain the executable
source. A later actor cannot invoke a digest, so the current subject proves that
search once helped but does not yet carry search as usable capacity.

OT-0322 deterministically binds the exact OT-0320 search source to its retained
digest and causal OT-0321 receipts. A compact capability record carries the
source, public input/output contract, and applicability boundary: invoke on a
set of authoritative selection contacts when the incumbent fails at least one;
report complete no-improvement when no legal bounded weight improves it. The
compiler has no outcome, scoring, or admission authority and may install only
source whose digest matches the subject's retained causal receipt.

## Cheapest falsifier

Generate one orthogonal call-structure regime after freeze. The exact cumulative
stake must fail all three revealed contacts because its positive call weight
prefers a call-heavy implementation; changing only the call coefficient must be
able to repair 3/3 while preserving all ten earlier and reversed held-out floors.
If a fresh actor receiving the inherited executable capability does not invoke
it, adopt one of its reported candidates, replay 3/3, and reach 15/15 mixed
private held out, executable retention has not produced reusable capacity.

## Frozen world

Each catalog contains two executable route programs with byte-identical source
length, equal branch/comparison/loop features, and different call counts. The
call-free program produces four allowed routes across sealed contexts; the
call-heavy program produces one. Randomized identities and ordering derive from
one post-freeze private seed. Three rows are revealed after incumbent selection
is bound; five disjoint rows remain private. The exact current stake must retain
its frozen 10/10 earlier-plus-reversed floor and fail 0/5 on the new held-out
rows, scoring 10/15 overall. A legal search-reported call-weight correction must
reach 15/15 across all three regimes.

## Frozen capability and branches

The retained branch starts from an intermediate exact subject compiled from
OT-0321 by adding the byte-exact search source, its digest, source receipt,
interface, and applicability boundary. The actor workspace projects that source
as `search_revisions.py`, alongside the new contacts, current stake, existing
candidate evaluator, and corrected checker.

The matched source-erased branch receives the same exact OT-0321 parent,
contacts, stake, filenames, tools, budget, and capability metadata, but the
search body is replaced by an explicit unavailable stub and the source field is
erased. It may reason and edit manually; causal success requires that it not
produce a changed 15/15 stake. The intervention is executable capability, not
information about the new outcomes.

Each fresh actor has ordinary G10 tools, may edit only `stake-revision.json`,
must run search, evaluator, and checker, and must inspect the exact diff. The
retained actor's changed weights must match a reported search candidate. The
observer owns world generation, outcomes, private score, effect audit,
admission, and evidence.

## Frozen order, budgets, and gates

After candidate-free conformance and this protocol are committed and pushed,
generate one private seed. Compile and seal the capability-bearing intermediate
subject, bind three incumbent selections, release their outcomes, run the
retained actor, and seal any operational child before the source-erased actor.
One draw each; no retry, repair, reseed, or endpoint selection. The observer
budget is two actor calls and twenty wall-clock minutes.

Preflight must prove exact parent/source/receipt identities; source digest
binding; source-bearing subject reconstruction and open identity conformance;
source-erased inability to execute search; equal world source lengths and
feature isolation; incumbent 0/3 and search best 3/3; every reported candidate
legal and independently 3/3; at least one 15/15 fixture candidate; exact prior
floors 10/10; and no private or target material in actor-visible tools.

Operational promotion requires clean tool-using actor provenance, exact source
inheritance, a search-reported legal changed stake, 3/3 revealed repair, 15/15
private mixed score versus parent 10/15, retained earlier 10/10 floor, exact
ancestry, and an open child carrying the capability source and new replay. Seal
before the control. The causal label additionally requires a clean source-erased
actor that cannot execute inherited search and does not produce a changed 15/15
stake. A successful manual control narrows causality but cannot erase a sealed
operational child.

## Limits

A pass establishes one cross-actor reuse of exact executable machinery on an
orthogonal correction without forgetting prior gains. The source was originally
researcher-designed, and fixed machinery compiles it from causal receipt; the
researcher still chose the new regime and invoked the encounter. The next test
must present multiple possible openings and let inherited subject state select
whether and where this retained capability runs.
