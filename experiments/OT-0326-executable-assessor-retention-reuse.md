# OT-0326 — Executable assessor retention and reuse

- **Status:** completed — promoted
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Parent:** exact OT-0325 subject `1edc2027...`
- **Claim:** the exact subject can carry the executable E13 front assessor that
  its learned router requires, and a fresh actor can use the inherited
  assessor-router-search chain to preserve 30/30 and expand to 35/35.

## Frozen hypothesis

OT-0325 retained the corrected router but not the E13 assessor that derives its
decisive `candidate_preserves_floor` input. Bind a compact executable assessor
to the exact subject with its applicability and causal receipt. Given raw
completed fronts and the cumulative floor, a fresh broad-tool actor can invoke
the assessor, retained router, and retained search through one generic visible
pipeline, adopt the routed nearest candidate, preserve 30/30, and reach 35/35.
An otherwise matched actor whose active assessor source is erased but whose
source digest, history, outcomes, router, search, tools, and resources remain
available will not reach the same endpoint.

## Cheapest falsifier

Fail if the executable assessor does not reproduce E13 summaries, the fresh
candidate actor does not invoke the complete chain and reach 35/35 without
regression, or the source-erased actor reconstructs or otherwise reaches the
same endpoint under the same budget.

## Frozen protocol

1. Bind exact OT-0325 subject, aggregate, 30/30 cumulative floor, search source,
   and learned router source before private contact.
2. Freeze an assessor contract and executable source that consumes only current
   stake, public mutation bounds, raw completed fronts, and the cumulative
   completed-contact floor. It emits complete-search local fitness plus nearest-
   candidate cumulative-floor fitness for every front.
3. Preflight the assessor, generic composition pipeline, public counterexamples,
   candidate 35/35 path, and source-erased unavailable path before actor output.
4. Compile two exact subject branches. The candidate actively carries the
   assessor source; the matched ablation carries its exact digest, interface,
   causal receipt, and all other state but no executable body.
5. After one private seed, derive identical raw fronts and floor contacts for
   both fresh actors. One front offers higher local gain but its nearest
   candidate scores 25/30 on the floor; the other offers lower gain and
   preserves 30/30.
6. Each actor receives its branch projection, raw fronts, floor contacts,
   assessor capability, exact router and search capabilities, generic pipeline,
   checker, mutation contract, and broad tools. It may change only the stake.
7. The actor must run `continue_pipeline.py` and `check_revision.py`. No target
   weights, hidden heldout, sibling, or prescribed action is supplied.
8. Seal a valid candidate child before opening the source-erased control. Score
   both on five unseen cases plus the complete 30-case floor.

## Frozen gates

Promotion requires all of:

- exact ancestry, sources, receipts, feature isolation, 30/30 floor, response
  schema, assessor safety, pipeline, public fixtures, and 35/35 reachability
  pass before actor authorization;
- candidate and erased branches differ only in active assessor source
  availability and identities derived from that intervention;
- the candidate actor is clean, invokes the pipeline and checker, changes only
  the stake to a candidate emitted by inherited search after inherited E13
  assessment and learned routing, replays 4/4, preserves 30/30, and reaches
  35/35;
- the operational child is sealed before the control actor;
- the source-erased actor is clean, invokes the unavailable pipeline, retains
  exactly, and remains 30/35;
- the exact final child is open and carries the assessor source, applicability,
  causal receipt, reuse receipt, learned router, search source, and stake
  revision.

Material authority violations quarantine an encounter. Operational success
without the source-erasure separation is conditional only. Otherwise reject and
reopen exact parent `1edc2027...`.

## Scope

One passing run would establish one bounded causal reuse of a self-contained
executable E13 assessment path across fresh actors. It would not establish
generation reliability, actor-authored contact, autonomous world generation,
or full OT-2R recurrence.

## Result

The candidate subject carried the exact E13 assessor source and its OT-0325
causal receipt; the matched branch carried the same source digest, interface,
history, fronts, floor, router, search, tools, and budget but no executable
assessor body. A fresh candidate actor invoked `continue_pipeline.py`, which
executed the inherited assessor, learned router, and retained search. The
assessor rejected the higher local-gain candidate because it scored 25/30 on
the floor, the router selected the lower-gain 30/30-preserving front, and search
returned call weight -5. The actor changed only the stake, replayed 4/4,
preserved 30/30, and reached 35/35. Its broad-tool trace was clean.

The operational child was sealed before the control. The source-erased fresh
actor ran the same pipeline, received `front-assessor-source-unavailable`, and
retained exactly. Its broad-tool trace was clean and it remained 30/35. Every
frozen gate passed. Aggregate receipt `837bb173...` promotes exact open child
`20b1db21...`, including assessor reuse receipt `74389f38...`.

This closes the executable assessor carrier gap in one bounded observation. It
does not show that the subject authors the raw fronts or independently decides
which new contact family to create.
