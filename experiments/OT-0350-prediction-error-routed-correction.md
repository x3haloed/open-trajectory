# OT-0350 — Prediction-error-routed correction

- **Status:** frozen — awaiting live actors
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Operational parent:** exact OT-0347 child `708ea5a3...`; OT-0348 rejected
  and OT-0349 remains conditional
- **Fresh actors:** at most five: one active corrector, active and control
  selectors, and active and control contact actors

## Hypothesis and cheapest falsifier

OT-0349 showed that a prediction-error record did not uniquely change an actor
already assigned to correct the policy. The hypothesis is instead that its
necessary causal role is pursuit selection: when the retained policy's declared
continuation proxy contradicts completed consequence, the subject-held error
makes one fixed driver open correction; when only that linkage is erased, the
same driver continues unchanged policy reuse. The routed correction must then
beat the unchanged route on a disjoint post-binding catalog and produce a wider
subject-held frontier after matched fresh contact.

The cheapest falsifier is failure of the error to change the next operation,
control access to a correction actor, active retention or invalid revision,
held-out derivation before active binding, equivalent active/control selection,
no downstream-yield advantage, either contact actor failing independent
consequence, floor regression, or inability to reopen the active child from its
own public frontier.

## Frozen driver and branch construction

Reuse the exact OT-0348 consequence and OT-0349 prediction-error compiler
without resampling. A fixed consequence compiler installs the raw receipt in
both exact-parent copies. The active copy additionally carries the derived,
non-authoritative unresolved prediction error and names
`resolve-selection-prediction-error`; the linkage-erased copy has no error and
retains `test-world-consequence-policy-reuse`. After removing those named
intervention fields, the branches are byte-identical.

The fixed driver has only two rules:

1. a valid unresolved error with exact subject, policy, and consequence ancestry
   opens the generic policy-correction workspace;
2. otherwise the exact inherited selection architecture opens ordinary policy
   reuse.

It has no world, outcome, score, candidate, or promotion authority. The active
corrector sees the error-bearing subject and unchanged raw consequence. The
control receives no corrector call: giving it one would reintroduce the
observer-selected phase that defeated OT-0349's causal claim.

If the active actor revises cleanly and improves retained consequence, bind its
exact source immediately, before any control actor or held-out derivation. Then
create one private seed producing new ids, order, cutoffs, and the frozen
six-to-zero, four-to-one, two-to-four delayed-continuation roles. Both branches
receive identical public rows. The active selector runs the revised source;
the control selector runs the exact incumbent source. Separate fresh actors
complete one selected contact per branch against matched public and hidden
consequence. All use broad tools, isolated workspaces, and G13/G11 audit.

## Score, controls, and promotion

Primary endpoint is independently verified downstream public contacts after
one equivalent completed current contact. The named intervention is the
prediction-error-to-next-operation path, including the correction actor call it
causes. Exact linkage erasure must preserve raw consequence, skip correction,
select the immediate-rich world under unchanged policy, and yield zero. Exact
machinery erasure on the active held-out rows must reproduce the same control
choice. An inadmissible high-yield decoy remains blocked by both sources.

Promotion requires: active and erased next operations differ exactly as frozen;
only the active route opens correction; active policy binding predates both the
held-out seed and all control output; all five authorized actors pass G13/G11;
both contacts pass 3/3 public and 5/5 hidden; active downstream yield is four
and control yield zero; exact 40/40 floor state survives both branches; the
active child is open and subject-only materializable; and the control reopens
unchanged policy reuse rather than falsely terminating.

This is a bounded causal test of artifact-routed self-correction inside one
synthetic policy/world family. Researcher-designed discrepancy compilation and
world interfaces remain disclosed. It does not establish reliability,
cross-domain recurrence, or that the revised delayed-yield rule is corrigible;
the latter requires a later regime where maximizing delayed yield becomes
harmful.

## Budgets and stopping

At most five fresh actors, no retries, no post-output repair, one retained
training consequence, one post-binding held-out derivation, and fifty observer
minutes. Stop after both branch endpoints and deterministic controls are
sealed. Observation completion does not close either open subject.

## Result

The fixed driver derived `resolve-selection-prediction-error` only for the
error-bearing subject and `test-world-consequence-policy-reuse` for the matched
linkage-erased subject. Consequently only the active route opened a corrector.
That fresh actor revised immediate viable-contact count to verified reopened-
contact count, passed a clean five-command G13/G11 trace, and improved the exact
retained training consequence from zero to three. Binding `552d8ffb...` was
sealed before held-out seed creation and before any control actor ran.

On the one new opaque catalog, the active program selected the world with two
current contacts and four independently verified downstream contacts. The
unchanged control selected six current contacts and zero downstream contacts.
Separate fresh selector and contact actors enacted both branches. All four
passed G13/G11; both contact sources passed 3/3 public and 5/5 hidden cases.
Exact machinery erasure reproduced the control choice, and both branches
preserved the exact 40/40 floor.

The active outcome compiles four complete public contact specifications and an
active contact into open subject `263126eb...`; it materializes without the
private seed. The zero-yield control does not terminate: open control subject
`ab931654...` returns to unchanged policy reuse. Aggregate `205af67e...`
promotes the bounded routing-causal claim.

This isolates the useful role OT-0349 missed. The prediction-error artifact is
not proven to make a model infer a correction it otherwise could not infer.
Instead, it makes unresolved contradiction select correction as the subject's
next pursuit, removing the observer's experiment-specific phase choice in this
bounded transition. The revised delayed-yield rule has not yet survived a later
regime where its own proxy becomes harmful.
