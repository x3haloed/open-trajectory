# OT-0187 — executable-routing receipt-effect replication

- **Status:** design and executable conformance frozen; not run
- **Evidence class:** exploratory-only
- **Target:** determine whether OT-0186's 4/4 versus 2/4 correction difference
  is a replicable consequence-receipt effect while preserving executable use
- **Parent:** exact OT-0182 open successor plus the same OT-0183 mechanism,
  OT-0185 route and matrix, and no actor-visible OT-0186 result
- **Actor budget:** eight contradiction-visible repair actors and eight matched
  contradiction-erased controls
- **Observer budget:** one complete driver invocation; 80 minutes

## Frozen replication rationale

OT-0186 proves that a bound route can occupy the causal decision edge: every
accepted corrected route is executed directly and passes all three contexts.
It does not pass its causal gate because two of four controls infer the same
repair from retained mechanism semantics and the option-expansion stake.

Do not remove that semantic information. It is genuinely available to the
subject, and a control that reasons correctly from it is not contamination.
Instead repeat the unchanged contrast with enough fresh actors to distinguish
a stable reliability increase from the observed small-sample split.

## Frozen inputs and isolation

Use the exact OT-0185 actor-authored route, certified signature matrix,
mechanism definitions, authority, active stake, and fixed route interpreter.
Create a disjoint token-renamed three-case wider blocked-option contradiction
with the same causal structure as OT-0186:

- the inherited composed route omits an unobserved allowed option and fails
  0/3; and
- viable filtering preserves it and passes 3/3.

Eight active actors receive the sealed receipt. Eight interleaved controls
receive its exact field erasure. Neither branch receives OT-0186 outcomes,
interpretation, actor routes, or sample frequencies. The public checker and
mutation envelope are unchanged and do not prescribe a route.

Bind every actor output before confirmation. `active-01` remains the
prospective successor; the observer may not select a favorable repair.

## Frozen direct confirmation and gate

Execute each accepted bound route directly, without a language-model selector,
on disjoint confirmation:

- six wider blocked-option cases;
- four observation-led cases; and
- six mixed-observed-blocked cases.

A branch passes only at 16/16. Require:

- contradiction-visible repairs: 8/8 full passes;
- contradiction-erased repairs: at most 4/8 full passes; and
- active advantage: at least four full passes.

At the boundary 8/8 versus 4/8, the frozen one-sided Fisher exact probability
is `70 / 1820 = 0.03846`; 8/8 versus 5/8 would not pass. This calculation is a
prospective gate, not a post-result reinterpretation. Also require all sixteen
fresh actors to pass complete trace, effects, mutation, and file/report audit;
the original route to retain its 10/10 contextual floor while failing wider
blocked-option contact 0/6; and the identity-extension floor to remain 18/18.

Promote only if every gate passes and the exact successor stays open. Install
the OT-0183 mechanism, sealed contradiction, and exact `active-01` executable
route. Passing would establish that objective contradiction reliably increases
the probability of a correct machinery revision and that the revision becomes
operative without successor reinterpretation. It would not establish a
receipt-exclusive cause, autonomous regime discovery, revision of the fixed
interpreter, or indefinite recurrence.

If the replication rejects, retain OT-0186's direct-execution result as an
engineering property only. Do not continue resampling the same contrast merely
to obtain a favorable split; reorient to a different causal question.
