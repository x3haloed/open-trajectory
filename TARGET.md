# Target

Open Trajectory studies whether completed contact with a changing world can
cause bounded inherited state to improve later behavior across complete actor
resets, whether consequences can improve the machinery of inheritance itself,
and whether later consequences can correct that machinery when it becomes
harmful.

The cumulative former target is preserved at `docs/archive/TARGET_G1.md`. It is
historical context, not a checklist for every experiment.

## Claim ladder

### OT-0: causal inheritance

A fresh actor receives a bounded external substrate derived from completed
prior contact. Against a matched branch without that inheritance, it performs
better on a held-out opportunity because of the inherited state.

### OT-1: longitudinal adaptation

Across multiple fresh actors and changing encounters, completed consequences
update inherited state through fixed researcher-designed machinery. The live
state outperforms unchanged-parent or frozen-state controls over a prospective
trajectory and remains correctable after an earlier improvement becomes
harmful.

The seed substrate and update machinery may be fully functional and
researcher-designed. Actor invention of the seed is not required.

### OT-2: machinery refinement

Consequences change not only inherited content but the machinery that selects,
projects, updates, or applies it. A changed-machinery branch beats its unchanged
parent under matched information and resources, and a later regime can revise
or replace the learned machinery when appropriate.

### Open developmental trajectory

Repeated OT-1 and OT-2 evidence across qualitatively different environments may
support a stronger interpretation: a lineage expands how it represents,
inherits, and corrects commitments without a fixed final ontology. This is a
long-horizon interpretation of accumulated evidence, not an entrance gate.

## Minimum causal path

A promoted mechanism claim needs:

- fresh actor context and workspace at every encounter;
- continuity only through the named substrate projection;
- an independently owned outcome that the actor cannot alter;
- a prospectively declared task order, score, stopping rule, and cheapest
  falsifier;
- a matched unchanged-parent, no-inheritance, frozen-state, or other control
  appropriate to the claim;
- a held-out opportunity separating inheritance from repetition;
- a decisive ablation of the claimed inheritance or machinery-change path;
- exact retained actor inputs and outputs plus enough deterministic downstream
  reconstruction to recompute the claimed comparison; and
- a harmful or contradictory later regime when corrigibility is claimed.

Promote only the complete causal path relevant to the claim. A working store,
evaluator, interpreter, carrier, reconstruction path, or updater is component
evidence until changed downstream behavior is observed.

## Claim dimensions

Report these separately under claim regime G2 (`docs/CLAIM_REGIME.md`):

- **Causal validity:** whether the retained intervention, controls, outcomes,
  and ablation establish the bounded mechanism observation.
- **Generative reproducibility:** whether fresh actor generations repeat under
  a declared sampling or deployment contract.
- **Actor provenance:** how precisely the producing model or deployment is
  identified.

Missing immutable hosted-response receipts can limit model attribution and
reproduction claims without erasing an otherwise complete bounded causal
observation. Never generalize a hosted alias to exact weights or future
deployments.

## Scope and attribution

State exactly what changed: inherited content, fixed-machinery state, selection
machinery, goal state, coordination structure, or embodiment. Keep stronger
claims separate unless an experiment tests the transition between them.

Researcher-specified structure is allowed and must be disclosed. Attribute
only the operations actually produced by the actor or updater. More mutable,
complex, autonomous, or open-ended machinery is not inherently a stronger
result.

## Evidence boundary

Raw outputs live outside Git under `$OT_EVIDENCE_ROOT` or ignored `.evidence/`.
Tracked experiment records state the frozen hypothesis and bounded result;
tracked manifests publish raw-artifact identities. Storage reconstruction,
causal validity, provenance, and generalization are separate questions.
