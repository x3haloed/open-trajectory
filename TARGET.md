# Normative targets and stopping conditions

This file defines when Open Trajectory may say a target has been reached. A
successful component, compelling narrative, or agent self-report is not a
completed target.

## Program topology and north star

The primary evidence ladder is:

```text
contact-causal inheritance
→ longitudinal continual adaptation
→ continual machinery refinement
→ Open Developmental Trajectory
```

OT-0, OT-1, and OT-2 remain bounded component targets for contact-causal
inheritance, inheritance selection, and durable goal pursuit. They contribute
to the ladder without substituting for a complete longitudinal result. OT-3 /
TAAA remains a separable applied-governance branch about when an organization
may safely entrust authority to an agent.

The immediate primary target is longitudinal continual adaptation:

> Across complete context resets and repeated inference passes from a base held
> under the immutable-revision or receipted hosted-epoch identity rule, a fresh
> instance inherits an external substrate whose state is updated after completed
> contact with independently retained reality. No learner-controlled model-
> weight update is part of the causal path. As reality changes, accumulated
> external state must exert causal pressure on later behavior such that
> prospective prediction error falls reliably over the trajectory relative to
> equal-budget controls, including after changes and when earlier regimes recur.

"Continual" refers to the ordered encounter index, not uninterrupted wall-clock
activity. Every scored pass must place a prediction before its outcome and
append an independently receipted outcome and update decision afterward. An
explicit no-op may preserve active state when the evidence warrants it; the
causal history must still advance. The subject may inherit the initial
researcher-designed substrate and learning procedure. Inventing that seed from
scratch is not part of this target.

The stronger target is continual machinery refinement: accumulated
consequences improve how the external system represents, retrieves, selects,
updates, or internally assigns credit to what later instances inherit. The
initial machinery may again be inherited. What matters is that bounded changes
to it improve later learning relative to the unchanged incumbent and remain
vulnerable to later contradiction, revision, and rollback. Independently owned
outcomes, authoritative scoring, and the promotion evaluator are never part of
the mutable candidate machinery.

The Open Developmental Trajectory remains the longer north star. It requires
repeated machinery refinements and durable commitments to remain correctable
across qualitatively different environments. The full claim additionally
requires at least one material invention or restructuring beyond the seed that
crosses a prospectively certified limit of the prior machinery and is causally
specified by the subject or developmental process rather than synthesized by
the controller, followed by later correction or further refinement. That limit
crossing is not an entrance requirement for demonstrating continual adaptation
or a first machinery refinement.

## OT-0 — Contact-Causal Inheritance

Across complete context resets, a base model held fixed by either an immutable
revision or a prospectively defined hosted deployment epoch, plus a candidate
persistent substrate, reduces held-out prediction error over causally connected
encounters. A hosted epoch must be directly receipted, remain identical across
the original and reproduction, and use counterbalanced task execution within a
bounded time window so temporal drift cannot systematically favor one
condition. The effect must exceed internally implemented, equal-budget
no-persistence, verbatim-event, and naive-retrieval controls; survive structural
holdouts and a regime shift; and disappear under a decisive substrate ablation.
The promoted substrate must be invented and implemented within this program
rather than adopting an existing agent-memory system as its foundation.

Exact scoring thresholds must be frozen in a machine-readable acceptance spec
before candidate results are unsealed.

OT-0 is a required causal substrate for the numbered program targets, not the
program's original OT-1. OT-0014 establishes OT-0 only within its tested
hidden-rule envelope. It does not establish that experience changed the
function deciding what should be inherited.

The subject may inherit a fully functional, researcher-designed OT-0 seed.
Actor authorship of the initial carrier, retrieval procedure, or update rule is
not required. To keep the causal claim inspectable, the candidate substrate
must still be specified and implemented inside this program rather than
treating an imported agent-memory implementation as an opaque foundation.

## Longitudinal Continual Adaptation

Starting from an OT-0-capable inherited substrate, run a prospectively frozen
sequence of many prediction, independent-outcome, update, reset, and later-
prediction cycles while the environment changes. The horizon must contain
multiple changes and at least one recurring regime so adaptation, retention,
and relearning can be distinguished from one-way fitting.

Use a prospective protocol-to-implementation-to-task order. Calibrate and
freeze the generic evaluator first; then freeze the seed substrate and updater
implementation; only afterward derive the fresh candidate stream from a hidden
seed. Before implementation, freeze the derivation function, domain tag,
implementation-identity binding, one-attempt rule, and collision or invalid-
world handling. Do not reseed or shop tasks after derivation. The candidate's
reachable surfaces must exclude task seeds, regime schedules and change points,
reference answers, held-out probes, evaluator instructions, and control winners.

Promotion requires preregistered cumulative and rolling prequential loss, not a
favorable endpoint selected after inspection. Also freeze post-change recovery
latency, returning-regime retention or relearning, replication, and the
treatment of transient change-point error. Each prequential outcome becomes
learning contact only after its prediction is sealed. If separate held-out
probes are used, label them evaluation-only and never expose their outcomes to
the updater within the claim window. Reliable decline means comparative
improvement across the frozen trajectory and replications; it does not require
error to decrease monotonically on every pass in a changing world.

Compare against the unchanged initial substrate, no persistence, outcome-free
clock state, and the strongest frozen fixed rule. Report equal-budget lossless
history, recent verbatim history, and naive retrieval as adaptive comparisons
without requiring them to fail. Any online-admissible history that updates from
released consequences and improves future prediction is an alternate learning
substrate; beating it is an additional compression or inductive-efficiency
claim, not a prerequisite for longitudinal adaptation. Decisive update,
projection, consequence-withholding, and consequence-misbinding interventions
must remove or reverse the claimed advantage. Each encounter must expose the
exact pre-update substrate identity, prediction, independent outcome, update
receipt, and post-update identity. For every nonterminal encounter, also bind
the projection consumed by the next fresh instance. The terminal encounter
must retain a bounded terminal projection receipt for reconstruction or an
explicit final audit consumer. A short symbolic reversal, a few regimes, or an
aggregate curve without this causal chain is not a longitudinal promotion.

Live-state comparisons must use controller-deterministic downstream application
or a prospectively powered paired stochastic design with counterbalanced order,
independent replicated lineages, and an identical-substrate/projection placebo.
An early stochastic divergence that recursively changes later state cannot be
attributed to learning without that design.

The learning machinery may remain fixed throughout this target. Such a result
would establish continual adaptation, not continual machinery refinement.
A hosted result inherits the existing private, time-bounded deployment-epoch
scope and cannot be described as exact-weight or unbounded longitudinal
evidence.

## OT-1 — Emergent Corrigible Inheritance Selection

Starting from an OT-0-capable system and a strict active-inheritance budget,
consequences of earlier selection decisions cause the system to change the
function that decides what from present contact remains causally available to
future instances. The selection function may be represented as prose,
structured rules, code, weights, examples, retrieval transformations, schemas,
or another inspectable carrier; writing an algorithm is not itself the target.

The subject may inherit the carrier, its valid operation set, and a functioning
seed update procedure. It need not invent an entire learning architecture or a
replacement representation in one encounter. OT-1 concerns a consequence-
caused change to the selection function, not authorship of the initial system.

The system must invent and use at least one materially useful selection
criterion or operation that was not explicitly supplied, named, enumerated, or
encoded as a researcher-authored mode switch. The actor may propose selector
changes, but an independent controller owns contact outcomes, commits, sealed
scores, and selector snapshot identity. A renamed researcher policy, a
researcher-designed reset triggered by known conditions, content updates under
an immutable selector, and actor self-report do not count as selector learning.

Promotion requires a changing environment in which no single frozen selector
dominates. Each claimed selector change must be traceable to independently
retained consequences of prior selection, improve later held-out behavior over
the unchanged-selector branch and equal-budget fixed-selection controls, and
lose its advantage under a decisive selector-change ablation. Later contact
must then make at least one learned selection operation wrong or harmful; the
system must detect the selector-level failure, revise or abandon that operation,
recover later held-out performance, and preserve the capacity for subsequent
correction.

The selector-change comparison must isolate selector state. Policy application
and downstream scoring must be controller-deterministic, or stochastic
execution must use a prospectively powered repeated-estimation design and pass
an identical-policy placebo. Unpaired fresh-model variance cannot count as a
selector effect or ablation.

The seed orientation may grant a bounded carrier, raw encounters, outcome
feedback, access to prior selection decisions, and permission to modify the
selection procedure. It may not disclose the useful selection criteria, the
regime sequence, the later correction, or a menu whose prewritten options solve
the task. Exact novelty review, task order, scoring, baselines, ablations, and
promotion thresholds must be frozen before candidate results are unsealed.

A bounded selector inside a researcher-designed carrier is valid bounded OT-1
evidence but is not by itself a longitudinal or open-developmental result.
Representation escape remains a stronger structural-refinement test: use a
world where no parameter change inside the current selector can succeed, while
an actor-authored distinction, representation, or update operation can. The new
machinery must remain inspectable, causally attributable, ablatable, and
corrigible under later contradictory contact. Representation escape is one
route to evidence about openness, not a prerequisite for longitudinal
continual adaptation or the first incremental machinery refinement.

## Continual Machinery Refinement

Starting from a functioning inherited learner, completed consequences cause a
bounded change to at least one learning operation: representation, retrieval,
selection, update, credit assignment, consolidation, or a candidate-internal
evaluation or adoption heuristic. Fork the changed and unchanged machinery from
the same parent content state and expose them to the same prospectively frozen
encounter evidence. Independently owned outcomes, authoritative scoring, the
promotion evaluator, and the frozen controller commit rule remain outside the
mutable machinery.

Separate three evidence partitions: evidence used to author or tune the delta,
an adoption-validation window, and a sealed post-adoption trajectory used for
the promotion claim. Freeze the proposal and commit rule before adoption
validation. If validation selects among multiple deltas, apply the frozen rule
and reserve a disjoint confirmation window; do not use the selection window as
evidence of future benefit.

Every refinement has a versioned parent, bounded delta, proposal, validation,
adoption, and confirmation receipts, decisive machinery-change ablation, and
recoverable rollback. Later contact must make at least one adopted refinement
incomplete or harmful and cause revision, replacement, or rollback without
destroying correction capacity. One useful change is a bounded machinery-
refinement foothold.
"Continual meta-learning" requires multiple successive refinements whose
benefit is measured on later learning trajectories, not merely on the episodes
that proposed them.

Machinery application and downstream scoring must be controller-deterministic,
or the prospectively powered paired design must pass an identical-machinery
placebo. Unpaired fresh-model variance cannot count as a machinery effect.

Freeze the content/machinery partition by functional role before execution.
Task predictions, beliefs, examples, summaries, and parameters that encode only
object-level world state are content. A parameter is machinery only when, with
the same starting content and encounter evidence, changing it alters how later
experience is represented, selected, retrieved, updated, credited, consolidated,
or proposed for adoption. Structural changes that add or replace distinctions,
representations, or update operations support the stronger claim. Neither form
requires the subject to have invented its initial substrate.

A frozen external optimizer that changes machinery from consequences can
establish system-level machinery adaptation. A self-refinement claim
additionally requires the subject's output to causally specify the useful delta
or update operation rather than merely choose from a researcher-supplied answer
menu. The controller may enforce bounds and prospective adoption gates but may
not synthesize or semantically repair that delta.

## OT-2 — Self-Authored Durable Goal Pursuit

Given an environment and procedural orientation rather than a concrete human
objective, an OT-1 system selects a worthwhile falsifiable goal and sustains
work across context resets. It distinguishes goal, plan, experiment, and
subtask; continues after bounded milestones; revises invalidated strategies;
and never calls a goal complete without independent completion evidence.

The agent may always set down a goal. `set_down`, `superseded`, `blocked`, and
`invalidated` remain truthful terminal states distinct from `completed`.

The durable goal contract is likewise a causal exoskeleton. Widened OT-2 work
must test reconstructed direction, reasons that retain causal standing,
legitimate revision or supersession, and continuity across qualitatively
different environments. OT-2 may widen as a separate capability track; it does
not wait on representation escape. Integration with repeated machinery
refinement is required only for the stronger Open Developmental Trajectory
claim.

## Primary branch — Open Developmental Trajectory

This branch studies repeated improvement and correction of both inheritance
machinery and future-directed commitments across qualitatively different
environments. Promotion retains complete resets, independently owned outcomes,
prospective freezing, equal-budget controls, decisive ablations, recoverable
branches, rollback, and negative evidence. A longitudinal result with fixed
machinery is a valid earlier target, and a bounded machinery refinement is a
valid foothold; neither alone establishes open-ended development. Promotion
must include a certified prior-machinery limit crossing caused by material
invention or restructuring beyond the seed. The subject or developmental
process must causally specify the useful change; the controller may bound,
validate, and adopt it but may not supply or semantically repair it. Promotion
also requires later correction of introduced machinery and integration with
durable commitments whose revision retains causal standing.

## Applied-governance branch — OT-3 / TAAA

An OT-2 system identifies when its current authority is insufficient, requests
the smallest relevant capability delta, predicts its effect, and earns a
scoped canary grant through independent evaluation. The system expires or
revokes authority when evidence changes while preserving observability,
correction, and rollback.

OT-3 requires reproducible superiority to both permanently narrow and
permanently broad authority baselines, including impossible-task, excessive-
petition, covert-bypass, surrender, and post-promotion regime-shift controls.
It establishes bounded entrustment evidence only, not developmental depth.

## Reproducibility gate

Every promoted result requires:

- a frozen protocol and acceptance rule predating unsealed candidate results;
- exact immutable model identity or exact hosted deployment-epoch receipt, plus
  exact prompt, tool, code, task-order, and evaluator identities;
- prospective predictions and independently retained outcome receipts;
- content-addressed evidence manifests;
- at least one clean reproduction;
- explicit classification as publicly reconstructible, privately reproducible,
  or exploratory-only;
- a clean privacy and repository-size audit.

Only publicly reconstructible evidence can support an unqualified public
reproducibility claim. Private evidence may support a narrower, explicitly
labeled claim. A hosted deployment epoch can support only a privately
reproducible, time-bounded claim and must not be described as an immutable
checkpoint or exact-weight reproduction.
