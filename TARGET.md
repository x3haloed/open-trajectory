# Target

Open Trajectory studies whether a subject can continue developing across
complete actor and turn endings. Completed contact with a changing world must
be able to alter later behavior, the machinery that carries and selects what
matters next, and—when necessary—that machinery's own correction.

The long-run target is not a permanent research controller that assigns the
next task. It is a continuing subject whose inherited state contains enough
consequence, unfinished pursuit, and executable reopening structure that the
research program can become an observer rather than a hidden causal component.

The cumulative former target is preserved at `docs/archive/TARGET_G1.md`. It is
historical context, not a checklist for every experiment.

## Claim ladder

### OT-0: causal inheritance

A fresh actor receives a bounded external substrate derived from completed
prior contact. Against a matched branch without that inheritance, it performs
better on a held-out opportunity because of the inherited state.

### OT-1: longitudinal adaptation

Across multiple fresh actors and changing encounters, completed consequences
update inherited state through fixed researcher-designed machinery. The live
state outperforms unchanged-parent or frozen-state controls over a prospective
trajectory and remains correctable after an earlier improvement becomes
harmful.

The seed substrate and update machinery may be fully functional and
researcher-designed. Actor invention of the seed is not required.

### OT-1C: operational continuity

Across actor endings and apparatus invocations, the last valid subject remains
mechanically reopenable. Its own continuation state selects the next class of
contact or correction under a fixed admission envelope. An invalid encounter,
an exhausted observer budget, or a completed model turn does not silently
become termination of the subject.

OT-1C is stronger than persistence of text and weaker than autonomous
self-development. It requires inherited developmental position: what has been
earned, what failed, what remains unresolved, and what operation is live next.

### OT-2: machinery refinement

Consequences change not only inherited content but the machinery that selects,
projects, updates, or applies it. A changed-machinery branch beats its unchanged
parent under matched information and resources, and a later regime can revise
or replace the learned machinery when appropriate.

### OT-2R: self-directed recurrence

One fixed driver can follow the subject's own open state through repeated
contact, saturation, revision, contradiction, correction, and promotion
without a researcher selecting each phase or supplying each new problem
family. Researcher-authored seed machinery and bounded world interfaces remain
permitted and disclosed. The developmental advance is that continued useful
motion no longer depends on experiment-specific intervention.

### Open developmental trajectory

Repeated OT-1C, OT-2, and OT-2R evidence across qualitatively different
environments may support a stronger interpretation: a subject expands how it
represents, inherits, pursues, and corrects commitments without a fixed final
ontology. The preferred direction is increasing subject-owned continuation and
decreasing experiment-specific researcher causation. This is a long-horizon
interpretation of accumulated evidence, not an entrance gate.

## Two validity objects

OT distinguishes an **operational subject transition** from a **research
claim about that transition**.

An operational transition is admissible when it has exact ancestry, fresh-
actor provenance, independently owned consequence, no sealed-authority
contamination, a declared mutation surface, and a passed no-regression or
developmental admission gate. Seal it before observational controls. A later
failed control can narrow the claim without retroactively erasing or killing
the valid subject.

A promoted mechanism claim needs:

- fresh actor context and workspace at every encounter;
- continuity only through the named substrate projection;
- an independently owned outcome that the actor cannot alter;
- a prospectively declared decision envelope, world derivation, scoring rule,
  observation stopping rule, and cheapest falsifier;
- a matched unchanged-parent, no-inheritance, frozen-state, or other control
  appropriate to the specific causal claim;
- a held-out opportunity separating inheritance from repetition;
- a decisive ablation of the claimed inheritance or machinery-change path;
- exact retained actor inputs and outputs plus enough deterministic downstream
  reconstruction to recompute the claimed comparison; and
- a harmful or contradictory later regime when corrigibility is claimed.

Subject-authored task order, contact machinery, pursuit, and stopping proposals
are allowed—and preferred at the open-development frontier—when the harness
freezes their authority boundary and binds them before hidden consequence.

Promote only the complete causal path relevant to the claim. A working store,
evaluator, interpreter, carrier, reconstruction path, or updater is component
evidence until changed downstream behavior is observed.

## Claim dimensions

Report these separately under claim regime G3 (`docs/CLAIM_REGIME.md`):

- **Operational validity:** whether one exact subject transition passed its
  independent consequence and admission gate.
- **Causal validity:** whether the retained intervention, controls, outcomes,
  and ablation establish the bounded mechanism observation.
- **Generative reproducibility:** whether fresh actor generations repeat under
  a declared sampling or deployment contract.
- **Actor provenance:** how precisely the producing model or deployment is
  identified.

Missing immutable hosted-response receipts can limit model attribution and
reproduction claims without erasing an otherwise complete bounded causal
observation. Never generalize a hosted alias to exact weights or future
deployments.

## Scope and attribution

State exactly what changed: inherited content, fixed-machinery state, selection
machinery, goal state, coordination structure, or embodiment. Keep stronger
claims separate unless an experiment tests the transition between them.

Researcher-specified structure is allowed and must be disclosed. Also disclose
where the researcher remains causally necessary: choosing pursuits, opening
actors, diagnosing failure, compiling correction, selecting retries, or
promoting successors. Attribute only the operations actually produced by the
actor, subject, updater, world, or observer.

More mutability or complexity is not inherently stronger. A lineage is
developmentally stronger when, under equal safety and world contact, it
preserves valid floors, turns consequence into better future selection, opens
coherent new possibilities, and requires less experiment-specific external
steering.

## Evidence boundary

Raw outputs live outside Git under `$OT_EVIDENCE_ROOT` or ignored `.evidence/`.
Tracked experiment records state the frozen hypothesis and bounded result;
tracked manifests publish raw-artifact identities. Storage reconstruction,
causal validity, provenance, and generalization are separate questions.
